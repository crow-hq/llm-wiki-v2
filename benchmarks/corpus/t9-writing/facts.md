# Lumen Coffee Roasters: facts (true for every document)

A small specialty roastery in Turin (fictional). About 14 people. One drum roaster, running at about 85% of capacity.

- Products: Aurora espresso blend (the best seller), Ethiopia Guji seasonal single origin, Night Shift decaf (Swiss Water, Colombia Huila; 12% of subscription orders). A price list took effect on 1 July 2026 after green coffee prices rose.
- Sales: a web shop, home subscriptions (roasted Monday, packed Tuesday morning, shipped Tuesday afternoon; monthly churn was 6.1% in Q2 2026), and a few wholesale cafés.
- Café Portello, near Porta Nuova, is the largest wholesale café: about 18 kg of Aurora and 2 kg of Night Shift a week, pays at 30 days, deliveries Wednesday morning. New cafés get a tasting, a 2 kg trial, a grinder calibration visit, payment on delivery for three orders and then 30 days; minimum order 5 kg.
- Green coffee: Andes Direct imports all Brazilian and Colombian coffee (FOB Genoa, 60 days, one-pallet minimum); 70% of yearly volume is bought at fixed prices six months ahead.
- Shipping: Italy 1-2 working days (€5.90, free over €45), EU 3-5 days (€12.90), pickup 15:00 every weekday. Refunds within 30 days.
- The board met in June 2026: approved a study of a second roaster, a cold brew pilot of 500 cans, a part-time packer; not a second shop.
