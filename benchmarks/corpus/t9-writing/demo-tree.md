## Folder /customers - Wholesale cafés, subscribers and the policies that apply to them.
- note `customers/cafe-portello.md`: Café Portello - Wholesale customer since 2024: 18 kg a week of Aurora; wants training on decaf.
- note `customers/refund-policy.md`: Refund policy - Refunds within 30 days of delivery; damaged bags are replaced without sending them back.
- note `customers/subscription-churn-q2-2026.md`: Subscription churn, Q2 2026 - Churn was 6.1% a month in Q2 2026; people leave mostly because they have too much coffee, then because of price.
- note `customers/wholesale-cafe-onboarding.md`: Wholesale café onboarding - New cafés get a tasting, a 2 kg trial, a grinder calibration visit, and 30-day payment after three orders.

## Folder /operations - How the roastery runs day to day.

## Folder /operations/roasting - Roast profiles, quality control and the roaster itself.
- note `operations/roasting/aurora-roast-profile.md`: Aurora roast profile - Charge at 205 °C, first crack at 9:10, drop at 11:30, with 1:45 of development.
- note `operations/roasting/quality-control-and-cupping.md`: Quality control and cupping - Every batch is cupped the day after roasting; below 82 points a coffee is not sold as single origin.
- note `operations/roasting/roaster-maintenance.md`: Roaster maintenance - Chaff cleaned weekly, exhaust monthly, burner serviced yearly; the roaster runs at 85% capacity.

## Folder /operations/shipping - Packing, carriers and delivery times.
- note `operations/shipping/carriers-and-delivery-times.md`: Carriers and delivery times - Parcel carrier for Italy in 1-2 days and the EU in 3-5; free shipping over €45 in Italy.
- note `operations/shipping/subscription-packing-day.md`: Subscription packing day - Subscriptions are roasted on Monday, packed on Tuesday morning and shipped on Tuesday afternoon.

## Folder /products - Coffees Lumen sells: blends, single origins and decaf, with their prices.
- note `products/aurora-espresso-blend.md`: Aurora espresso blend - House espresso: 60% Brazil Cerrado and 40% Ethiopia Guji, medium roast; the best seller.
- note `products/ethiopia-guji-seasonal-single-origin.md`: Ethiopia Guji, seasonal single origin - Washed Guji lot, floral and citrus, sold from March to August while the lot lasts.
- note `products/night-shift-decaf.md`: Night Shift decaf - Swiss Water decaf from Colombia Huila; 12% of subscription orders.
- note `products/price-list-from-july-2026.md`: Price list from July 2026 - Retail and wholesale prices from 1 July 2026, after the rise in green coffee prices.

## Folder /sourcing - Green coffee: importers, origins, contracts and how prices are fixed.
- note `sourcing/andes-direct-importer.md`: Andes Direct, importer - Main importer for Brazil and Colombia lots: FOB contracts, payment at 60 days.
- note `sourcing/green-coffee-price-hedging.md`: Green coffee price hedging - Prices are fixed for six months on 70% of the expected volume; the rest is bought at market.
- note `sourcing/guji-washing-station-visit-february-2026.md`: Guji washing station visit, February 2026 - Notes from the visit to the Guji washing station: capacity, cupping and the 2026 contract.
