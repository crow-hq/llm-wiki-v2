# What happened at Lumen, June to November 2026 (story A)

Many things happen at once every month; most documents are about ordinary work. These threads run through the six months:

- June: the tasting bar inside the roastery opens (licence, first menu, opening weekend, staff rota). Ordinary roasting work (a new Guji lot profile, roaster cleaning schedule). One new café, Bar Stazione, starts its trial. Customer care sees complaints about a late carrier. Weekly syncs.
- July: tasting bar gets steady weekends, adds a cupping evening. The July price list causes some subscription cancellations; a churn check-in. Café Portello asks for a credit line increase. A sourcing call with Andes Direct on the next Brazil shipment. Product page rewrite for Aurora.
- August: Luca Moretti's push to cafés works: three new cafés sign (Caffè Lagrange, Bar Vanchiglia, Pasticceria Monti), each with trial notes, price agreements, delivery days. A wholesale order spreadsheet summary appears. A late payment from one café. Summer staff holidays and a reduced roasting schedule. Tasting bar summer opening hours.
- September: the café business keeps growing (two more cafés, a café asking for a grinder loan, a quarterly review of wholesale volumes, a dispute about a damaged delivery to a café, café credit-limit policy changes). Lumen starts "Lumen Academy": barista courses for home users and café staff (course calendar, a trainer contract, booking rules, a room-rental quote, first course feedback). A board meeting covers all of it plus the second roaster study.
- October: cafés now 9; a dedicated café account manager is hired; cafés ask for a loyalty discount; a café closes and returns equipment. Academy runs four courses, adds a latte art module, a trainer complains about the room, booking cancellations policy. Tasting bar hosts a harvest event. Roaster service. Price hedging for H1 2027.
- November: Christmas: shipping cut-offs, extra packing shifts, a carrier surcharge, gift boxes, record parcel volumes and a backlog incident. Cafés order holiday blends; year-end café contracts are renewed. Academy sells gift vouchers for courses and plans 2027 dates. Tasting bar Christmas menu. A board meeting pre-read.

Some documents revise or repeat an earlier one (a corrected course calendar, a second version of a café price agreement, a forwarded email chain).
