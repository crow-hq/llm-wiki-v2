# Board meeting, September 2026

Held on Friday 25 September 2026. Present: Marco (chair), Giulia, Elena and two outside board members.

## Agenda and results

1. **Half-year results.** Sales are 14% above the same period of 2025. Gross margin dipped by two points in July because of the price rise in green coffee, but recovered in August.
2. **Subscriptions.** The base is at 438 weekly bags, with a monthly churn that went down to 5.4% in the third quarter.
3. **Part-time packer.** The packer approved in June has been hired and starts on 12 October.
4. **Tasting bar.** The board approved the budget of about €11,800 for a small tasting bar to open in the roastery on 24 October.
5. **Cold brew pilot.** The shelf-life test started on 21 September. No decision on the second batch yet.

## Approved

- The tasting bar budget.
- A purchase of 600 additional shipping cartons for December.
- Giulia's proposal to look at a new carrier for Italy.

## Not approved

A campaign on paid social media of €5,000 in the autumn; the board prefers to wait for the tasting bar results.

## To discuss in November

The second roaster decision and the first sustainability projects, including organic certification.

Next board meeting: Friday 20 November 2026.
