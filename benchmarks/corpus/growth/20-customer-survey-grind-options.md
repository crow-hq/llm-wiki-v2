# Customer survey, September 2026: grind options

We sent a short survey to the 1,900 retail customers and subscribers who bought in the last six months. 412 replied (22%). The main subject was grind: today bags are sold as whole beans or ground for moka only.

## Results

| How do you prepare your coffee? | Share |
|---|---|
| Espresso machine | 34% |
| Moka pot | 27% |
| Filter or pour-over | 19% |
| Aeropress | 8% |
| Other | 12% |

- 58% of respondents grind at home from whole beans.
- 31% would buy ground coffee if the grind matched their method; the main request is filter and espresso.
- 11% never knew we offered a grind option.

## Comments

The most common comments were about the grind setting for moka being too fine, which makes bitter coffee, and requests for a "filter" grind at the checkout. A few subscribers asked for the grind to be changeable for each delivery without contacting support.

## Conclusions

1. Add espresso and filter grind options at the checkout and in the subscription settings.
2. Review the moka grind, which seems too fine.
3. Tell existing customers about the grind choice in the next newsletter.

Sofia will work with the web shop developer on the new options; the work should be ready in November.
