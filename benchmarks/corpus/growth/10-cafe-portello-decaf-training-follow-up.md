# Café Portello: follow-up after the decaf training

Notes from Marco after a visit on Wednesday 16 September 2026, when delivering the usual order.

## Training

The decaf session promised in July took place on 8 July at the roastery, with the head barista and two baristas of Café Portello (together with a second café). The key points were a coarser grind for Night Shift, a slightly longer ratio (18 g in, 40 g out) and temperature 1 °C lower. Café Portello reports fewer sour shots and has put decaf on the menu as a regular espresso option.

## Volumes

- Aurora: up from 18 kg to about 22 kg a week since the end of the summer, because the terrace stays open longer.
- Night Shift: up from 2 kg to 3.5 kg a week.
- Payment is still at 30 days, never late.

## Requests

- The owner would like to receive delivery on Tuesday afternoon as well as Wednesday morning during the busiest weeks. We said we would check with the packing team.
- They asked about a branded tasting event in their café in the autumn.
- They mentioned that a competitor roaster offered them a lower price; they stayed with Lumen and want to discuss it with Marco.

## Next contact

Marco will phone the owner on 7 October about the price conversation and the delivery day.
