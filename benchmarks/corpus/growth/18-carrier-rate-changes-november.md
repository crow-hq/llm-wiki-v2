# Carrier rates and delivery times: changes from 1 November 2026

The parcel carrier we use for Italy and the EU sent its new price list and service conditions on 25 September. The changes affect the shipping fees shown in the shop.

## New rates

| Destination | Delivery time | Fee to customer |
|---|---|---|
| Italy | 1-2 working days (unchanged) | €6.50 (was €5.90) |
| European Union | 3-5 working days (unchanged) | €13.90 (was €12.90) |

Free shipping in Italy stays at orders over €45. For the EU we are considering a free-shipping threshold of €90 from January.

## Other changes

- The carrier will collect parcels every weekday at 15:00 as now, but the last time to hand over parcels for next-day delivery is 14:30 on Fridays.
- Damaged parcels must now be reported within 5 working days instead of 8, with photos from the customer. Support must tell customers to answer quickly.
- A fuel surcharge of 3% is added to the carrier invoice from December.

## What to do

1. Update the shipping fees in the web shop before 1 November.
2. Change the support templates that mention the 8-day deadline for damage reports.
3. Tell Tuesday packers that the Friday deadline does not affect subscriptions.

Davide is also asking a second carrier for a quote, which could lead to another change in early 2027.
