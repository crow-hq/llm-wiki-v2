# Andes Direct: new contract terms from 2027

Andes Direct sent a revised standard agreement on 29 September 2026, to apply to all contracts signed from 1 January 2027. Marco and Giulia reviewed it on 1 October.

## What changes

- **Payment**: 45 days from delivery instead of 60. Andes Direct says this is due to its own bank financing costs. A discount of 1.5% is offered for payment at 30 days.
- **Minimum order**: reduced from a full pallet (1,150 kg) to half a pallet, about 575 kg, for lots from Colombia, so that a small lot like the decaf can be bought in two parts.
- **Shipping**: still FOB with the importer arranging the shipping to Genoa, but the price of freight is now listed separately in the contract, so we can compare it with other shippers.
- **Samples**: still arrive two weeks before shipment. Andes Direct will now send two samples per lot instead of one.

## Our view

The shorter payment term has a real cost for Lumen: with about 22 tonnes a half year this means roughly €25,000 less of working capital. The 1.5% discount for 30 days is better than the interest on our overdraft, so it may be worth taking.

The half-pallet minimum is good news, because it makes the Colombia decaf lot easier to manage.

## Next steps

Giulia will calculate the effect on cash flow before the January fixings and ask whether the 60-day term could be kept for the first contract of 2027.
