# Aurora roast profile: adjustments after the September cupping

Since the second half of September the cooler weather and lower humidity in the roastery have changed how Aurora behaves on the 15 kg roaster. The 12 kg reference profile for Aurora still works, but the head roaster has made small corrections that should be added to the profile.

## What we saw

- First crack arrived at 8:50 on average, instead of 9:10, on the first batches of the morning.
- Development time stretched to 1:55 and the cup showed a slightly roasty finish.
- Weight loss stayed at 14.5%, in range.

## Adjusted values for the first batch of the day

| Step | Time | Bean temperature |
|---|---|---|
| Charge | 0:00 | 203 °C (drum) |
| Turning point | 1:25 | 91 °C |
| First crack | 9:10 | 196 °C |
| Drop | 11:20 | 207 °C |

The charge is two degrees lower for the first batch only, and the drop is ten seconds earlier. Development time returns to about 1:40 to 1:45, which is the target of 15%.

## Result

Cupped on 25 September against the reference sample: 84.5, with chocolate and orange, clean finish. The previous batch scored 83.0.

## Action

Keep the lower charge for the first batch until the roastery warms up again, then go back to 205 °C. Note the change in the roast log with the batch number.
