# Green coffee price hedging: fixings for the first half of 2027

This note follows up on the six-month hedging rule introduced in January 2026. The estimate for the next period has been made and the first fixings are in.

## Volume estimate

From the Q3 sales and subscription growth we expect to need about 22 tonnes of green coffee between January and June 2027, up from 19 tonnes in the first half of 2026.

## Fixings agreed so far

- 70% of the volume (15.4 tonnes) is the target fixed share, as per the rule.
- Brazil Cerrado: 6.1 tonnes fixed at USD 4.35 per kg, which is 4% above the January 2026 price and 6% below the July 2026 fixing.
- Colombia Huila decaf: 0.9 tonnes fixed at a price that will be confirmed once the sample is approved.
- The Guji lot will be negotiated separately after the next visit.

## What it means for prices

The July 2026 rise was based on a fixing 11% higher; the new fixing is lower, so the cost of Aurora should fall by about 3 cents a bag. We do not plan to lower retail prices, but we will not raise them in January.

## Remaining 30%

The unfixed part will be bought at market as needed. Because the market has been calmer this autumn, Giulia suggests keeping the 70/30 split instead of moving to 80/20.

## Next review

First week of March 2027, before the following fixings.
