# Colombia sourcing trip: discussion at the weekly meeting

Minutes of the point raised at the weekly sync on 23 September 2026, with the people present: Marco, Giulia, Elena and the head roaster.

## Background

Night Shift is made from a Huila lot bought through Andes Direct. The importer has invited Lumen to visit the cooperative in January 2027 during the main harvest, to see the decaf lot's origin and taste the next crop before the contract is signed.

## Discussion

- The head roaster thinks the visit is useful because the 2026 Huila lot was cupped lower than expected and we would like to choose the next lot ourselves.
- Giulia pointed out the cost: about €2,400 for flights and accommodation for one person for a week, and the shop must be covered while the head roaster is away.
- Elena asked whether Lumen should go instead to Peru to look at the organic lot, as discussed in the certification note, and combine the two in one trip.
- Marco noted that the Guji visit in February was the best source of information on the 2026 contract, so a similar approach for Colombia seems right.

## Outcome

No budget was approved yet. Marco will ask Andes Direct whether they would share the costs of the trip and whether a visit to a Peruvian cooperative could be included. The head roaster will prepare a short list of goals for the trip.

## Decision needed by

Mid-November, to book travel at an acceptable price.
