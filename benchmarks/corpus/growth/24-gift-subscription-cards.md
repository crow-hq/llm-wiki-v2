# Idea: gift cards for subscriptions

Elena proposes to let customers give Lumen subscriptions as presents. At the moment, someone who wants to give coffee to a friend can only buy a bag.

## The proposal

A gift card that can be bought in the web shop or at the roastery for 1, 3 or 6 months of a monthly subscription. The recipient chooses the coffee, grind and delivery date, and starts the subscription with a code. Nothing is charged to them after the gift ends unless they choose to continue.

## Pricing

| Gift | Price | Included |
|---|---|---|
| 1 month | €28 | one 250 g bag Aurora, free shipping |
| 3 months | €80 | three bags, any coffee |
| 6 months | €155 | six bags, any coffee |

The prices are built on the standard subscription discount of 10%.

## Benefits

- A gift is a strong way to find new subscribers: our guess is that one in four recipients will continue.
- It sells well before Christmas and Mother's Day.
- Cash comes in before the coffee is delivered.

## Problems to solve

- Gift codes need a small change in the web shop, with an expiry date of 12 months.
- Accounting for prepaid gift cards has to be agreed with Giulia.
- Packing: a printed card to include in the first bag.

## Decision

Not decided. To be discussed after the advent calendar, which competes for the same December period and the same time of the web shop developer.
