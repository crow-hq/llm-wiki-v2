# Idea: a coffee advent calendar for December

Proposal from Sofia (web shop) for a seasonal product that is not yet decided.

## The idea

A box with 24 small sachets of 12 g, one for each day of December, each a different coffee or a different roast of the same coffee. It would be sold only online from mid-November and shipped before 1 December.

## Why it could work

- December is the strongest month for gift sales, and we have no product at a gift price point.
- Sachets let us show off lots that are too small for 250 g bags, for instance end-of-lot Guji and test roasts.
- A box with a story gets shared on social media.

## Open questions

- Sachet filling: by hand it takes about four minutes a box. At 300 boxes this is roughly 20 hours of extra work in November, when the team is busiest.
- Packaging: a printed box costs about €2.80 with a minimum run of 300.
- Price: we are thinking of €39, which gives a margin of roughly 45% if volunteers fill the sachets and 30% if we pay for the time.
- Whether it would draw sales from regular bags or bring new customers.

## Next step

Sofia will ask two packaging printers for quotes and test one filled box with five colleagues. A decision is needed before 15 October to have time to print.
