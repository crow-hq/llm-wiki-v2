# Kenya AA sample evaluation, September 2026

We received two 300 g samples of Kenyan washed coffee from Cafeteria Nairobi Trading, a small exporter introduced by a contact at a trade fair. We are not buying anything through Andes Direct for this, because Andes does not supply East African coffee.

## The samples

| Sample | Grade | Altitude | Process |
|---|---|---|---|
| KE-1 | AA | 1,800 m | washed, 72 h fermentation |
| KE-2 | AB | 1,700 m | washed, 48 h fermentation |

## Cupping results

Cupped by the head roaster and two colleagues on 18 September, three rounds each.

- KE-1: 87.0 points. Blackcurrant, grapefruit, brown sugar, high but clean acidity.
- KE-2: 84.5 points. Tomato leaf, orange, a little flat on the finish.

## Commercial terms offered

- KE-1: USD 9.40 per kg, ex-warehouse Mombasa, minimum 600 kg.
- Shipping and insurance to Genoa about USD 0.55 per kg.
- Payment 50% on order and 50% at shipping, unlike our 60-day terms with Andes.

## Assessment

KE-1 would make an excellent filter coffee for the winter, but 600 kg is a lot for a single origin we have never sold, and the prepayment is a cash cost. We could ask for 300 kg or propose a shared container with another roaster. No decision yet; we will ask for a second sample from a different lot.
