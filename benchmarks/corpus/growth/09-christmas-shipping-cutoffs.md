# Christmas shipping cut-off dates

Draft for the web shop and the support team, prepared by Davide on 28 September 2026.

In December the carrier volumes rise sharply and delivery times in Italy usually move from 1-2 working days to 2-4. To keep promises we will publish last order dates.

## Cut-off dates for delivery before Christmas

| Destination | Last order date | Expected delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Italy, standard | Friday 18 December | by 23 December |
| Italy, express (new, €9.90) | Monday 21 December | by 24 December |
| European Union | Monday 7 December | by 22 December |

Subscription parcels keep the usual Tuesday packing. In the week of 21 December the packing day moves to Monday and the carrier collection to 12:00, because the carrier closes early.

## Things to prepare

- Extra pallets of cartons: order 600 more by 20 November.
- Ask the carrier for a second collection on Fridays from 4 December to 18 December.
- A banner on the shop with the cut-off dates from 15 November.
- Parcel tracking links added to the confirmation emails, which should reduce questions to support.

## Risk

If the carrier misses the Tuesday collection in the busiest week, we will have no backup. Davide will request a quote from a second carrier for Italian deliveries.
