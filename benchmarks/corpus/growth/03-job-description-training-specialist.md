# Job description: wholesale training specialist

Lumen is opening a part-time position (24 hours a week) for a person who trains the baristas of our wholesale cafés and supports our own team's learning. The role is new and sits between customer support and production.

## Why now

Two cafés asked for decaf training in June and we had to improvise with whoever was free. Several cafés have also asked how to dial in a new grinder. We want one person who owns this and keeps a training programme up to date.

## Responsibilities

- Run on-site and roastery-based barista sessions: espresso dialling, milk, decaf extraction, cleaning routines.
- Write and maintain short training guides and one-page recipes for each coffee.
- Prepare internal sessions for new Lumen staff on cupping basics and product knowledge.
- Report recurring problems to the head roaster.

## Requirements

- At least two years behind an espresso bar, preferably as a head barista.
- A recognised barista or sensory certificate, or willingness to obtain one within six months.
- Clear communication in Italian and English.
- Driving licence for café visits within the Turin area.

## Conditions

Fixed-term contract of 12 months, with the possibility of becoming permanent. Gross monthly pay of about €1,350 for 24 hours, plus a coffee allowance of 2 kg a month. Applications to hiring@lumen.example by 30 October 2026.
