# Sustainability report 2026: outline and data to collect

Lumen has never written a sustainability report. Sofia and Elena propose a short public report, around six pages, to be published in January 2027. The work links the organic certification study and the new packaging, and gives customers one place to read what we do.

## Proposed structure

1. **Our coffee**: where it comes from, what we know about the farmers, and what we pay compared with the market.
2. **Our packaging**: what the bags are made of, what we want to change, and the result of the compostable bag tests.
3. **Our energy**: gas used per kilo of roasted coffee, and the plan for a smaller carbon footprint.
4. **Our team and community**: jobs, training, events at the tasting bar.
5. **Goals for 2027**: three goals that can be measured.

## Risks

Making claims we cannot prove. Every figure must come from a document, and we will say "not measured" when we do not have the data. No claim about the carbon footprint will be made without a calculation checked by an outside advisor.

## Timetable

Data by 30 November, first draft 15 December, final version and design by 15 January.
