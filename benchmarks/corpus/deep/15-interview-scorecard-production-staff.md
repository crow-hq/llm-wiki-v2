# Interview scorecard for production and packing staff

This is the shared scorecard for interviews of candidates for the part-time packing position approved by the board in June, and for other production roles. It exists so every interviewer evaluates candidates on the same criteria and decisions can be compared afterwards.

## How interviews run

Two interviewers, 30 minutes. First 10 minutes: the candidate's background. Then a 10-minute practical exercise at the packing table. Last 10 minutes: questions from the candidate.

## Scoring

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 4 immediately after the interview, before the interviewers talk to each other.

| Criterion | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Reliability | Punctual, a clear record of past commitments |
| Speed and accuracy | Packs 12 sample bags with the right labels in under 8 minutes with no errors |
| Teamwork | Works well in a small team on a Tuesday morning rush |
| Interest in coffee | Curiosity is enough; no experience is required |
| Physical suitability | Can lift 10 kg bags repeatedly |

## Rules

- Do not ask about family plans, age or health.
- A score of 1 on reliability rules out the candidate whatever the total.
- Write two sentences of comment for each score.
- Final decision within 3 working days, by Giulia and the head of production.

## After the interview

Send the scorecard to Giulia by email. Candidates who are not selected receive a reply within a week.
