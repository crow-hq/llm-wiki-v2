## Changes requested by insurers

1. **Generali (Option A):**  
   - Increase property deductible from €1,000 to €1,500.  
   - Require annual inspection of the roaster’s gas line by a certified technician (Luca Bianco confirmed we already do this in March – copy of last inspection report available).  
   - Exclude coverage for “spontaneous combustion” of green coffee unless stored in metal bins with fire-rated lids. Current storage is in GrainPro bags on wooden pallets – we would need to switch to steel bins for the 2,500 kg Guji lot arriving from Andes Direct in December. Cost of 10 steel bins from CaffèTech: approx. €800.

2. **Allianz (Option B):**  
   - Fire safety audit (€350–€500 fee).  
   - Monitored smoke detection in green coffee storage (€1,200–€1,500).  
   - Require that all subscription shipments include a printed warning: “Do not store near open flame” – we can add this to our existing packing slip template (Elena to coordinate).

3. **AXA (Option C):**  
   - Quarterly stock inventory – Chiara can prepare this from existing purchase records. No additional cost, but 2 hours per quarter administrative time.

---

## Recommendation from broker (Alessandro Fiori)

Alessandro recommends **Option A (Generali + AXA for BI)** for the following reasons:
- Lowest total premium (€11,000 vs €11,600 or €11,800).
- Only one new condition that requires capital outlay (steel bins for Guji storage, €800).
- No recurring quarterly administrative burden (unlike AXA’s inventory requirement in Option C).
- Allianz’s fire safety audit and smoke detector installation would total ~€1,700–€2,000, making their effective cost €13,500+.

**Alessandro’s note:** “The steel bin investment is a one-time cost and also improves fire safety for your most expensive green coffee. I’d move forward with Generali for property and liability, and keep AXA for BI with the current deductible.”
