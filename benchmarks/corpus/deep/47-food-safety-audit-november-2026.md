# Food safety audit, November 2026

**Date of audit:** 10 November 2026  
**Inspecting authority:** ASL Torino – Servizio Igiene Alimenti e Nutrizione (SIAN)  
**Inspector:** Dr. Elena Rinaldi  
**Roastery representative:** Marco Bellini (Head Roaster)  
**Attendees:** Sofia Conti (Operations Manager), Luca Ferro (Wholesale Lead), Dr. Rinaldi  

The annual food safety inspection was conducted at Lumen Coffee Roasters, Via delle Arti 23, Turin, from 09:30 to 13:15. The inspection covered HACCP plan execution, pest control, cleaning records, allergen management, and physical premises. Below are the findings, deadlines, and required actions.

---

## 1. HACCP Plan

**Status:** Partially compliant.

- **Critical control points (CCPs) identified:** Roasting (CCP1 – time/temperature), cooling (CCP2 – moisture removal), packaging (CCP3 – seal integrity).  
- **Monitoring logs reviewed:** CCP1 logs from August–October 2026 show 12 missing entries (dates: 3 Aug, 17 Aug, 31 Aug, 14 Sep, 28 Sep, 5 Oct, 12 Oct, 19 Oct, 26 Oct, 2 Nov, 9 Nov, 10 Nov).  
- **Corrective actions:** For missing entries, Marco Bellini must complete retrospective log entries using production batch notes by 17 November 2026.  
- **Non-conformance:** No written procedure for CCP3 seal integrity checks. Dr. Rinaldi requires a standard operating procedure (SOP) for daily seal tests on all vacuum-sealed bags (Aurora, Ethiopia Guji, Night Shift) by 24 November 2026.

**Deadline:** 24 November 2026.

---

## 2. Pest Control

**Status:** Non-compliant.

- **Contractor:** EcoPest Solutions (contract #EP-2026-04, renewed 1 March 2026). Monthly visits recorded through October 2026.  
- **Findings:**  
  - Insect glue traps in the green coffee storage area (room 2) have not been replaced since 15 September 2026 (overdue by 8 weeks).  
  - One trap near the loading dock (south wall) was found upside down with dust accumulation.  
  - No pest sighting log maintained in the roasting hall.  
- **Required action:** Replace all traps by 14 November 2026. Submit a signed log of weekly trap checks to Sofia Conti by 21 November 2026.  
- **Note:** No live pests observed during inspection. Rodent bait stations were correctly placed and sealed.

**Deadline:** 21 November 2026.

---

## 3. Cleaning Records

**Status:** Partially compliant.

- **Daily cleaning:** Roasting drum, cooling tray, and floor sweep logs are complete for October 2026 (signed by shift lead).  
- **Weekly deep cleaning records:**  
  - Missing entries for 5 October, 19 October, and 2 November 2026.  
  - The weekly cleaning of the green coffee receiving bay (area 4) has no documented proof since 28 September 2026.  
- **Monthly cleaning:**  
  - Exhaust hood filters in the roasting hall were last cleaned on 15 August 2026 (overdue by 3 months). Filter inspection shows 30% grease buildup.  
- **Required actions:**  
  - Complete missing weekly records by 14 November 2026.  
  - Clean exhaust hood filters by 17 November 2026, with photos attached to the cleaning log.  
  - Implement a digital cleaning checklist (shared via Google Sheets) by 1 December 2026.

**Deadline:** 1 December 2026.

---

## 4. Allergen Handling

**Status:** Compliant with minor recommendations.

- **Allergens declared on labels:** None (coffee is not a regulated allergen; no cross-contact from shared equipment).  
- **Observation:** The roastery also processes flavored coffee (e.g., Vanilla Night Shift) for a limited run (50 kg in October 2026).  
  - No separate cleaning protocol exists for the roasting drum after flavored batches.  
  - Dr. Rinaldi recommends a written rinse-roast procedure (run 5 kg of unflavored green coffee through the drum after flavored batches, then discard that batch) to prevent trace flavor carryover.  
- **Action:** Draft a one-page allergen management addendum to the HACCP plan by 24 November 2026.

**Deadline:** 24 November 2026.

---

## 5. Additional Findings

- **Thermometer calibration:** The infrared thermometer used for cooling tray checks (model RayTemp 8, serial #RT-422) was reading 2.3°C high compared to a calibrated probe. Recalibrate by 14 November 2026.  
- **Personal hygiene:** Two staff members (Giorgio R., Elena M.) were observed without hairnets in the packing area. Retrain all 12 staff on hygiene protocols by 21 November 2026.  
- **Waste management:** The organic waste bin (coffee chaff) near the back door was overflowing. Empty daily and maintain a log of removal times.
