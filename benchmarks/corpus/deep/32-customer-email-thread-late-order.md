Ciao Marco,  

Thank you for reaching out. I want to apologise for the delay and explain exactly what happened. The root cause was a mechanical failure on our 15 kg roaster that forced us to halt production for two days. Here is the full account from our engineer, Lorenzo, who handled the repair.  

**Incident timeline**  

| Date & time | Event |
|-------------|-------|
| 10 Oct 2026, 14:30 | During the 4th roast of the day (Ethiopia Guji, batch #E-1026) the drum bearing seized. The burner flame sensor tripped the gas safety valve, shutting down the roaster automatically. |
| 10 Oct 2026, 15:10 | Lorenzo arrived. Initial inspection showed the drum would not rotate and the burner would not relight. He isolated the roaster from the gas line and began disassembly. |
| 11 Oct 2026, 09:00 | After removing the drum, Lorenzo found the front bearing (part #B-15K-6) had welded itself to the shaft. The bearing housing was also scored. The burner thermocouple (part #TC-240C) had cracked from the heat spike when the bearing seized. |
| 11 Oct 2026, 11:30 | Lorenzo sourced replacements from our spare parts inventory and from a local industrial supplier (bearing housing was not in stock; he had it machined overnight). |
| 12 Oct 2026, 08:00 – 16:00 | Repair work: removed the old bearing and housing, pressed in the new bearing, reassembled the drum, replaced the thermocouple, recalibrated the burner gas valve, and performed a cold rotation test. |
| 13 Oct 2026, 07:30 | Lorenzo completed a test roast (2 kg of a decaf blend) to verify even drum rotation and burner stability. The roaster passed all checks. |
| 13 Oct 2026, 10:00 | Production resumed. Your order was roasted and packed that afternoon, but because the packing day is normally Monday, the courier had already collected the Monday shipments. Your order went out with the Tuesday afternoon collection. |

**Parts replaced**  

| Part | Part number | Quantity |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Drum bearing (front) | B-15K-6 | 1 |
| Bearing housing (front) | BH-15K-6 | 1 |
| Burner thermocouple | TC-240C | 1 |
| Gas valve gasket set | GVS-15 | 1 (preventive) |
| Drum shaft seal | DS-15K | 1 (preventive) |

**Why the bearing failed**  
Lorenzo’s diagnosis: the bearing had been running dry – the lubrication wick inside the housing had degraded. He noted that the last scheduled maintenance (September 2026) missed checking the wick because the access cover was seized. He has since replaced that cover and added a monthly wick-inspection task to our maintenance log.  

**What we changed to prevent recurrence**  
- Monthly lubrication check added to the roaster’s PM schedule.  
- Bearing temperature sensor (part #TS-200) ordered and will be installed next week to give an early warning.  
- All remaining bearings (rear drum and cooling tray) inspected and re-greased.  

I understand the delay caused you inconvenience. We have already credited your account with a 10% discount on next week’s order as a goodwill gesture. If you would like to discuss the technical details further, I can put you in touch with Lorenzo directly.  

Again, my apologies.
