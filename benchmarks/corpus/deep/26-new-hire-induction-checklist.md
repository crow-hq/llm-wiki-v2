# Induction checklist for new team members

Every new person at Lumen, whatever the role, follows the same first-week plan. The checklist was written by Elena after the summer, when two temporary staff members started without a clear plan, and will be used for the part-time packer and the training specialist.

## Before the first day

- Contract signed and sent to the accountant.
- Uniform apron and keys prepared.
- A buddy is named: an experienced colleague who answers every question in the first month.

## Day 1

- Tour of the roastery: green coffee store, roaster, packing table, shipping area.
- Safety briefing: gas shutoff, fire extinguishers, the exhaust duct and chaff rules, lifting 20 kg sacks.
- Meeting with Marco on how Lumen works and who our customers are.

## Days 2 and 3

- Shadow the buddy during roasting day or packing day, depending on the role.
- First cupping session with the head roaster: learn the vocabulary, taste Aurora, Night Shift and the seasonal Guji side by side.

## Rest of the week

- Short quiz on the products and prices; everybody must know the three prices of the price list.
- Practise one real task alone with a colleague checking the result.
- Friday 16:00: a talk with Elena, who asks what was unclear.

## After one month

A feedback meeting with the buddy and the manager, and a decision about the next learning goals. Training hours are logged in the shared sheet.
