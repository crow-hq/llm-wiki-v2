### Agenda: 2027 Green Coffee Forward Cover

#### 1. Decision on 2027 volume to cover with futures contracts

Sofia presented the projected 2027 green requirement: 8,200 kg total (6,500 kg for blends and single origins, 700 kg for decaf, 1,000 kg buffer for new wholesale accounts). The board agreed to cover **70% of the blend and single-origin volume** (4,550 kg) via ICE “C” futures contracts, leaving 30% to be bought spot or through forward contracts with Andes Direct. Decaf and buffer will remain uncovered to allow flexibility.

**Decision:** Lock 4,550 kg of Arabica coffee futures, equivalent to 100 bags (46 kg each), using the ICE “C” contract.

#### 2. Fixing dates and contract structure

Marco proposed three fixing windows to spread risk:

| Fixing Tranche | Volume (bags) | Month of Fixing | Target Month |
|----------------|---------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Tranche A      | 40 bags       | December 2026   | February 2027 |
| Tranche B      | 30 bags       | January 2027    | April 2027    |
| Tranche C      | 30 bags       | March 2027      | June 2027     |

Each tranche will be fixed at the average of the last five trading days of the fixing month. Sofia noted that Andes Direct’s differential for Ethiopia Guji (washed) is currently +$0.45/lb over ICE “C”, and for the Aurora blend (Brazil + Colombia) the differential averages +$0.18/lb. The board accepted the structure.

**Decision:** Fix Tranche A on 15 December 2026, Tranche B on 20 January 2027, Tranche C on 17 March 2027.

#### 3. Cost of the cover and margin impact

Marco presented the current ICE “C” price (17 November 2026): **265.00 US¢/lb** (December 2026 contract). Assuming a conservative 5% upward movement to 278.00 ¢/lb by March 2027, the cost calculation for 4,550 kg (10,000 lb) is:

- Futures cost: 10,000 lb × 272.00 ¢/lb (blended estimate) = **$27,200**
- Differential (blended average +0.30 ¢/lb): 10,000 lb × 0.30 = **$3,000**
- Total green coffee cost (covered portion): **$30,200** (≈ $3.02/lb)

Current wholesale margin on covered coffee (after roasting, packing, shipping) is 38%. The cover adds **$0.09/lb** to the cost (futures brokerage + margin on differential). This reduces margin to **36.5%** if selling prices remain unchanged. Sofia proposed absorbing the 1.5% margin compression rather than raising wholesale prices, to maintain Café Portello and other accounts.

**Decision:** Accept the 1.5% margin compression; do not adjust 2027 wholesale prices for covered volumes.

#### 4. Broker and contract administration

Luca confirmed that the roastery’s broker, **Commodity Trade Services S.r.l.** (Milan), charges $0.75 per bag for ICE “C” execution. For 100 bags, total brokerage = **$75**. Marco will execute the December fixing via phone order on 15 December, with written confirmation by email.

**Decision:** Authorise Marco to place the December fixing order with CTS.

#### 5. Next steps and action items

- **Sofia:** Send Andes Direct the 2027 volume estimate for Ethiopia Guji and Aurora blend by 25 November.
- **Marco:** Prepare cash flow for the margin deposit (10% of $27,200 = $2,720) due by 1 December.
- **Luca:** Update the roasting schedule to align production with the fixing months.
- **Chiara:** Inform Café Portello and other key wholesale accounts that pricing is stable through June 2027.
