# Sample roasting procedure for new green coffee lots

Procedure written by the head roaster. It applies to every sample that arrives before a contract is signed, from Andes Direct or from any other supplier.

## Equipment

Samples are roasted on the 1 kg sample roaster, not on the production roaster. Using the production roaster for a 300 g sample would waste coffee and distort the log.

## Steps

1. Weigh 200 g of green coffee and check the moisture with the meter. Anything above 12.5% is noted and the supplier is asked about it.
2. Photograph the green beans on a grey card and count visible defects in a 300 g sample. More than five full defects is a warning.
3. Roast three samples of 150 g each: light, medium and medium-dark, all to the same development time of 15%.
4. Rest for 24 hours and cup on the second morning in the same way as batches are cupped.
5. Record in the sample sheet: lot name, supplier, moisture, defects, cupping score of each roast, comments.

## Decision rules

- Below 82 points: not purchased as single origin; could be considered for a blend only if the price is low.
- 82 to 85: purchase only if no other option is available at a similar price.
- Above 85: sent to the head of the company with a draft contract.

## Storage

Remaining green sample is stored in a sealed bag at 18 °C for six months in case the supplier disputes the result.
