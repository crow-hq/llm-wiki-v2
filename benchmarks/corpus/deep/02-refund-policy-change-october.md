# Refund policy: changes from 1 November 2026

After the summer we reviewed the refund policy and the support inbox. The refund policy that applies to retail customers and subscribers will change on 1 November 2026.

## What changes

- The refund window stays at 30 days from delivery, but for the Christmas season (orders delivered between 20 November and 24 December) it is extended to 45 days, so that gifts can be returned in January.
- Refunds will go back to the original payment method within 3 working days instead of 5, because the payment provider now settles faster.
- A photo is still enough for a damaged bag. From now on a photo is also enough for a bag with the wrong grind: we replace it and do not ask for it back.
- Subscriptions can still be refunded for the last delivery only, but a subscriber who cancels within 48 hours of a charge gets that charge back in full, even if the bag has already shipped.

## What does not change

Open bags are replaced, not refunded, when the complaint is about taste. Wholesale customers keep their own terms in the onboarding agreement.

## Next steps

Support will update the answer templates, and the web shop footer must show the new wording before 1 November. Questions go to Elena in customer support.
