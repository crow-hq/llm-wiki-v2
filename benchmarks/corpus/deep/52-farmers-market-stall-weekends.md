## Observations

- **Best Saturdays:** 17 Oct (€603) and 28 Nov (€541) both had clear weather and high footfall near the cheese hall. Worst: 7 Nov (€328) – rain kept casuals away, stall under a leaky awning.
- **Van issues:** Luca reported the Ducato’s parking permit zone changed on 1 Nov – we now have to park 20 min walk from pitch on Porta Palazzo side. Cost us 15 min setup/breakdown and one lost sale (customer left while we were fetching stock). Miglietta (green grocer neighbour) offered to let us store one bin of bags under his table – declined due to humidity.
- **Staffing:** Nina worked four Saturdays, Matteo and Silvia two each. Elena and Clara filled gaps. No complaints – team liked the extra shift pay, but Marco found 7 Nov “demoralising”.
- **Signups:** Only 11 home subscriber signups over the eight Saturdays (target was 25). Most people who bought Guji or Aurora were already subscribers. Decaf Night Shift moved via impulse tastings – one 5-minute talk per sample.

---

## Recommendation: Continue in spring?

**Proceed, with conditions.** We lost money on the 7 Nov and 21 Nov cancellations, but the average net of €96/Saturday covers green coffee and gives a small profit margin. Spring (from Easter 2027) could yield better weather and footfall, but we need to:

- Negotiate pitch fee down to €35 (or switch to the cheaper “Borgata” spot at €30, though smaller – 6 m x 2 m). Silvia said she met the market manager (Lorenzo) on 28 Nov and he hinted at a spring discount for regular vendors.
- Drop the Ducato rental for March–May. Instead use Luca’s personal van (blue Citroën Berlingo, needs only fuel comp of €15/Saturday). Parking zone issue solved.
- Replace the awning with a pop-up gazebo (budget €80 from Decathlon). Leakage cost us an estimated €80 in lost sales on 7 Nov.
- Shift staff to one person + one floater from the roastery. Nina can run the stall alone if we pre-pack and load on Friday. Saves €56 per Saturday.
- Add a **tasting flight** (three 50 ml cups of Guji, Aurora, Night Shift) for €3 – small add-on tested on 28 Nov with leftover sample packs, sold 7 flights + 4 bags upsell.

**Decision deadline:** 15 January 2027 – Lorenzo needs confirmation for spring stalls by then. Chiara, Matteo and Silvia to vote after quarterly accounts review (10 Jan).
