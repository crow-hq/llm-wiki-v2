# Tasting bar events: calendar for autumn 2026

After the opening of the tasting bar on 24 October the team has planned a first series of evening and weekend events. Places are limited and booked through the web shop.

## Calendar

| Date | Event | Places | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri 30 October, 19:00 | Opening evening with free tastings | 40 | free |
| Sat 7 November, 10:30 | Introduction to espresso at home | 10 | €25 |
| Thu 19 November, 19:00 | Decaf versus regular: a blind tasting | 14 | €15 |
| Sat 28 November, 10:30 | Filter coffee workshop | 10 | €25 |
| Thu 10 December, 19:00 | Winter blend and mulled coffee evening | 20 | €12 |

## Organisation

- Each event is led by the head barista or the head roaster, with one colleague to help.
- Coffee for the events is taken from our stock; each workshop costs about €60 of coffee and pastries.
- Participants receive 10% off a bag bought the same day.

## Promotion

A newsletter on 12 October, a post on social media each week, and a poster in the nearby cafés that are our customers. Café Portello has offered to host the poster.

## Measures of success

We will count bookings per event and the number of participants who sign up for a newsletter or subscription within a month. If a workshop is full in less than a week, we add a second date.

## Open points

Private bookings by companies, which several people have asked about, are not planned yet.
