# Lumen Tasting Bar: opening plan and licence

Summary of the project to open a small tasting bar in the front room of the roastery. The plan was prepared by Sofia and Elena in September 2026.

## The concept

A counter with six seats and two standing tables, serving Lumen coffees only: espresso, filter coffee brewed to order, and a changing "flight" of three small cups for €7. A few pastries from a baker round the corner. The aim is to let customers taste before they buy a bag and to give the web shop community a physical place.

## Licence and rules

- Notification to the municipality for the sale and consumption of food and drink on the premises.
- A hygiene training certificate for each person serving, which two members of the team already hold.
- Since we only serve coffee and bought pastries, we do not need a full kitchen. We do need a handwash sink and a dishwasher, about €3,800 for both.
- The room is within the noise limits; the grinder will be sound-insulated.

## Opening hours

Thursday to Saturday, 9:00 to 14:00, starting Saturday 24 October 2026. The shop front stays closed on Monday to Wednesday, when the roaster is working.
