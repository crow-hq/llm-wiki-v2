# Organic certification: feasibility note

Prepared by Elena, 9 September 2026, after three customers asked whether any Lumen coffee is certified organic.

## Where we stand

None of our coffees is certified. The Guji lot and some of the Colombian coffee come from smallholders who farm without chemical inputs, but they are not certified either, because certification is expensive for them.

## What certification of Lumen itself involves

Even if we buy certified green coffee, the roastery has to be certified as a processor in order to put the organic logo on a bag.

- Registration with a recognised Italian certification body.
- Annual fee: about €650 for a small processor plus an inspection of about €400.
- Documented separation between organic and conventional coffee in storage and on the roaster, and a cleaning step between roasts.
- Full traceability from lot to bag, which our roast log already mostly provides.

## Options

1. Do nothing and explain our direct relationships with farmers.
2. Certify the roastery and sell one organic coffee, starting with a Peruvian lot we have been offered.
3. Certify the roastery and convert part of the range over two years.

## Recommendation

Option 2 is possible, but only if packaging is also reviewed in the same project. The inspector will check labels, and we would rather change them once. A first decision is proposed for the next board meeting.
