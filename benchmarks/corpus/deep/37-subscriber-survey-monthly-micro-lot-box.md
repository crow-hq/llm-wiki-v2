# Subscriber survey: a monthly micro-lot box

**Conducted:** 8–15 October 2026  
**Respondents:** 47 home subscribers (out of 112 active)  
**Survey sent by:** Nina (Customer Lead)  
**Results compiled by:** Matteo (Operations)  

---

## Who answered

- **18** from Turin metro area (Café Portello subscribers, mostly)  
- **14** from Milan, Bologna, Rome (shipping zone A)  
- **15** from rest of Italy (zone B)  
- **Average subscription length:** 14 months  
- **Median weekly spend on current subscription:** €18 for 250g single-origin + 250g blend  

All respondents had been subscribed at least 3 months. No new subscribers (<2 months) were included.

---

## Would they subscribe to a micro-lot box?

| Response | Count | % |
|----------|-------|---|
| Yes, definitely | 14 | 30% |
| Probably yes | 10 | 21% |
| Maybe / depends on price | 9 | 19% |
| Probably no | 8 | 17% |
| No | 6 | 13% |

**Net likely subscribers:** 24 (51%) said yes or probably yes.  
Among the 14 “definitely yes” subscribers, 9 already buy single-origin from us monthly; 5 currently buy only the Aurora blend.

---

## Price they accept

- **Median acceptable price:** €32 per month (300g box, 2 x 150g micro-lots)  
- **Range:** €24 (lowest, from 3 respondents) to €45 (highest, from 2 respondents)  
- **At €35/month:** 18 respondents said they’d subscribe (38%)  
- **At €40/month:** 11 respondents (23%)  

Subscribers who said “probably yes” were willing to pay €28–32. The “definitely yes” group accepted €30–40.

---

## What they ask for

Top requests (free-text, multiple allowed):

1. **Tasting notes + farm story** – 22 mentions (e.g. “I want to know who grew it, not just the region”)  
2. **Roast date within 3 days of shipping** – 19 mentions  
3. **Two different processing methods per box** – 14 mentions (washed + natural or anaerobic)  
4. **Smaller bag size (100g)** – 12 mentions (to try more lots without committing to 250g)  
5. **Ability to skip a month** – 10 mentions  
6. **Inclusion of a brew guide card** – 8 mentions  

One respondent (Claudia, Rome) wrote: *“I love the Ethiopia Guji but I want to taste something wild – like a co-ferment or a honey-process from a farm I’ve never heard of.”* Another (Lorenzo, Milan) said: *“Don’t make it exclusive if it’s just expensive. Make it rare but affordable.”*

---

## What they say of their current subscription

- **Satisfaction score (1–5):** 4.0 average  
- **Most common praise:** “Consistent quality” (27 mentions)  
- **Most common complaint:** “Same coffees too long” (14 mentions) — these are the same people who want the micro-lot box  
- **6 respondents** said they’d cancel their main subscription if the micro-lot box replaces it; **18** said they’d keep both  

Quote from Elena (Turin): *“I love the Night Shift decaf for evenings, but my morning coffee hasn’t changed in six months. A monthly surprise would keep me excited.”*

---

## What the team thinks

**Meeting:** 20 October 2026, 9:30 AM, roastery office  
**Attendees:** Nina, Matteo, Luca (Head Roaster), Sofia (Wholesale Manager), Andrea (Green Buyer), Chiara (Packing Lead)

- **Luca** (roasting): “I can roast two micro-lots per week on the 15kg, but we’d need separate cooling trays for small batches. That’s a €600 investment. Doable by November.”  
- **Andrea** (green buying): “I have three potential lots from Andes Direct: a Gesha from Finca El Mirador (Colombia, 2026 harvest), a natural-process SL28 from Kieni (Kenya), and a honey-process Catuai from Finca San Jeronimo (Guatemala). All are under 200kg each. We could commit to 50kg per lot for a test run.”  
- **Sofia** (wholesale): “Café Portello asked if they can offer the box to their own regulars. If we do this, I’d want a wholesale version – maybe 6-box cases at 15% discount.”  
- **Chiara** (packing): “Packing day is Wednesday. Adding a micro-lot line would take 90 minutes extra. We’d need one more person on the line – maybe Elena from front desk can help.”  
- **Nina** (customer lead): “24 likely subscribers at €32 = €768/month gross. Costs: green €12/kg (micro-lot premium), roasting labour €30/hr, packing materials €2/box, shipping €5.50. Net margin around 22%. Worth a 3-month pilot.”

**Decision:** Proceed to pilot. Launch target: first week of December 2026. Name: “Lumen Micro-Lot Dispatch”. Price: €34/month (introductory for first 20 subscribers). Limit: 30 subscribers for pilot. Include brew guide card, 2 x 100g bags, roasting within 48h of packing day.

**Next steps:** Nina to send pre-order link to the 24 likely subscribers by 25 October. Andrea to confirm green availability by 1 November. Luca to order cooling trays. Chiara to schedule packing trial on 3 November.
