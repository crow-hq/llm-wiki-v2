# HACCP Plan Revision

**Date:** 15 October 2026  
**Revised by:** Elena Moretti (Head Roaster)  
**Approved by:** Luca Bianchi (Operations Manager)  
**Effective from:** 20 October 2026  

## Purpose
This revision addresses hazards identified during the 5 October 2026 internal audit (conducted by Elena Moretti and Marco Rossi, Production Assistant). The previous plan (dated March 2026) lacked specific critical limits for moisture in green coffee and did not include corrective actions for metal contamination during packing.

## Process Steps, Hazards, and Controls

### Step 1: Green Coffee Intake (Receiving)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Biological:** Mold growth from moisture >12.5% | Moisture ≤12.0% (using Kett PM-650 meter) | Measure 3 samples per 60-kg bag, record average. Check supplier COA. | Reject lot. Return to Andes Direct (contact: Sofia Ramirez). Note in intake log. | Intake Log (date, lot#, moisture%, COA#). |
| **Chemical:** Ochratoxin A from poor storage | Supplier must provide OTA test <2 ppb (EU limit) | Verify COA for each lot. If missing, request within 48 hours. | Hold lot. Do not roast until COA received. If OTA >2 ppb, return to supplier. | COA file (digital, dated). Hold tag on pallet. |

### Step 2: Green Coffee Storage (Ambient, 18–22°C)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Physical:** Pest infestation (weevils, moths) | Zero visible insects in 500g sample | Visual inspection weekly by Marco Rossi. Use sticky traps (4 traps per pallet). | Isolate affected bags. Fumigate with CO2 (48 hours, 60% CO2 concentration). Dispose of heavily infested bags. | Pest log (date, trap count, action taken). |

### Step 3: Roasting (15-kg Diedrich roaster, batch size 12–14 kg)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Chemical:** Acrylamide formation from over-roasting | Bean temperature at drop ≤210°C (measured by probe) | Record drop temp for every batch on Roast Log. | If >210°C, discard batch for retail. Use as cold brew base only (test with café Portello). | Roast Log (batch#, date, drop temp, time). |
| **Physical:** Metal fragments from roaster drum | None above 2 mm (pass through magnet) | Run magnet over beans post-roast (every 10th batch). | Stop roaster. Inspect drum. Re-run affected batch through magnet. | Magnet check log (date, batch#, result). |

### Step 4: Cooling (Perforated cooling tray, forced air)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Biological:** Bacterial growth if beans >40°C for >2 hours | Cool to ≤35°C within 4 minutes | Timer and probe check by operator (Giulia Neri, Roaster Assistant). | If >4 minutes, discard batch for subscription. Use for wholesale only (immediate packing). | Cooling log (batch#, start temp, end temp, time). |

### Step 5: Degassing (Holding in sealed bins, 12–48 hours)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Physical:** Over-pressure from CO2 buildup | Bin pressure valve opens at 0.5 bar | Visual check of valve (daily, before use). | Replace valve if stuck. Vent bin manually. | Degas bin check (date, valve status). |

### Step 6: Packing (Wholesale: 1-kg valve bags; Subscription: 250g bags)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Physical:** Metal fragments from bag sealer | Zero metal pieces >1 mm in any bag | Run all bags through metal detector (Loma IQ3, sensitivity: 0.8 mm Fe, 1.2 mm non-Fe). | Reject bag. Inspect sealer jaws. Recalibrate detector weekly. | Metal detector log (date, batch#, reject count). |
| **Biological:** Oxygen ingress from failed valve | Valve seal must hold vacuum for 10 seconds | Test 1 bag per 100 bags (vacuum gauge). | Recalibrate sealer. Retest next 50 bags. | Valve test log (date, batch#, pass/fail). |

### Step 7: Storage of Packed Product (Wholesale pallets, subscription boxes)
| Hazard | Critical Limit | Monitoring | Corrective Action | Records |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Chemical:** Stale flavor from >60 days storage | Ship within 45 days of roast date | Date stamp on each bag. Weekly inventory check by Luca Bianchi. | Move oldest stock to front. Offer discount to Café Portello if >45 days. | Inventory log (date, lot#, age days). |
