# New product: Lumen Winter blend

A limited blend for the Christmas season, available from 16 November 2026 until the stock runs out, probably around mid-January.

## Composition

| Component | Share | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil Cerrado, natural | 50% | body, hazelnut |
| Colombia Huila, washed (regular, not decaf) | 30% | red fruit, sweetness |
| Ethiopia Guji, natural (leftover lot) | 20% | spice, dried fruit |

The blend is roasted a little darker than Aurora, at about Agtron 52-54, so that it works well in a moka pot and with milk. Tasting notes: dark chocolate, orange peel, cinnamon.

## Formats and prices

- 250 g bag: €10.50
- 1 kg bag: €34.00
- Gift tin with 250 g: €15.00
- Not sold to wholesale cafés, except as a trial on request.

Subscribers can add the Winter blend to any delivery in December and still receive their 10% discount.

## Production

About 450 kg planned, roasted in three batches of 12 kg on Mondays, never mixed with the Aurora roast on the same day. The label design is by a local illustrator and will be ready on 30 October.

## Why

Most of our competitors sell a Christmas coffee and customers asked for one last year. The blend also uses green coffee that would otherwise sit unused.
