## 2. In-house label printer quotes

We currently hand-stamp roast dates on bags. For subscription and wholesale orders with variable coffee names, a label printer would reduce prep time. Quotes for a thermal-transfer label printer, plus annual running cost (labels + ribbon).

| Model | Supplier | Printer cost (€) | Label roll cost (€ per 1,000 labels, 40x30mm) | Ribbon cost (€ per roll, 300m) | Estimated annual cost (5,000 labels/year) | Lead time (days) |
|-------|----------|------------------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------|
| **Zebra ZD421** | TecnoEtichetta (Turin) | 845 | 18.50 | 22.00 | 1,055 | 5–7 |
| **Brother QL-810W** | Etichettiamo (online) | 620 | 21.00 (pre-sized die-cut) | no ribbon (direct thermal) | 725 | 3–5 |
| **Dymo LabelWriter 550** | OfficeTech (Milan) | 490 | 24.50 (proprietary rolls) | no ribbon (direct thermal) | 612 | 2–4 |

**Note:** Brother and Dymo use direct thermal – labels fade under UV (shelf life ~6 months). Zebra uses thermal transfer (ribbon) – labels last >2 years. For subscription bags that may sit in cupboards, Zebra is safer.

## 3. Recommendation

**For bags:** Order 10,000 bags (mix of 250g and 1kg) from **Sacchetti Italia**. Their MOQ per SKU is 5,000 – we can start with 5,000 of 250g (Aurora + Ethiopia Guji) and 5,000 of 1kg (Aurora + Night Shift). Total cost: 5 × €142 + 5 × €198 = **€1,700** (before 10% first-order discount = €1,530). Lead time 12–15 days – order by 5 October to have bags by 23 October for the November subscription cycle.

**For label printer:** Purchase the **Zebra ZD421** from TecnoEtichetta. Upfront €845. Annual running cost (labels + ribbon) ~€1,055 for 5,000 labels. Higher cost than direct thermal, but labels won’t fade – critical for wholesale cafés like Café Portello that store bags for 3–4 months. Enzo confirmed that hand-stamping takes 45 minutes per 100 bags; with Zebra we estimate 8 minutes. Payback in labour savings: 37 minutes per 100 bags × 200 bags/week × €18/hour = €222/week saved → printer pays for itself in ~4 weeks.
