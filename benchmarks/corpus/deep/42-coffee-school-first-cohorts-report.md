## Summary

We launched the Lumen Coffee School on 5 September 2026. As of 12 November, we have run six public sessions (two per month). Total unique enrolments: 78. Total revenue: €3,510. Below are the details, student feedback, and proposed changes.

---

- No-show rate: 12.8% (10 missed out of 78 enrolled).  
- Revenue per attended student: €45 on average (courses priced at €30–€50 depending on materials).

---

## Student feedback (summary from post-session forms, n=68 responses)

**What worked well**  
- “The hands-on time at the La Marzocco was excellent” (Session 1, 3, 6)  
- “Great to taste Guji vs. a washed Yirgacheffe side-by-side” (Session 5)  
- “Luca’s explanation of roast curves made sense even to a beginner” (Session 4)  
- “Small group size – felt like personal coaching” (multiple sessions)

**What to improve**  
- “Would have liked more time on milk steaming, not just pouring” (Session 3, 4 of 14 attendees)  
- “Session 5 cupping felt rushed – 18 people on one table” (Session 5, 6 mentions)  
- “No decaf tasting in Session 6? We only heard theory – disappointing” (Session 6, 3 of 7 attendees)  
- “Start time 18:30 is too early for people commuting from outside Turin” (5 mentions across sessions)

**Net Promoter Score (NPS):** 72 (based on “how likely to recommend?” 0–10 scale) – solid, but room to grow.

---

## Proposed changes for next cohorts

1. **Cap cupping sessions at 12 people** – split into two groups with two facilitators (Marta + Riccardo).  
2. **Add a 15-minute milk-steaming demo** to Session 3, shorten theory by 10 minutes.  
3. **Session 6: include a blind tasting of Night Shift vs. a commercial decaf** – students want sensory comparison.  
4. **Shift start time to 19:00** for all weekday sessions (except Saturdays).  
5. **Introduce a “Coffee School Pass”** – 10% discount for booking 3+ sessions upfront.
