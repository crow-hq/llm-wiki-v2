## 1. Hedged cost per kilo (green)

We locked in three forward contracts with Andes Direct on 15 September 2026:

| Coffee | Locked price (€/kg green) | Hedge volume (kg) |
|--------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Aurora blend base (Brazil + Colombia) | €7.40 | 1,200 |
| Ethiopia Guji | €9.80 | 800 |
| Night Shift decaf (Colombia) | €8.60 | 500 |

These prices are **all-in** (FOB + freight + import fees) and fixed through Q1 2027. The spot market on 15 Sept was €8.10 for Aurora base, €11.20 for Guji, €9.40 for decaf.

## 2. Cost of the cover (premium paid)

We paid a non-refundable premium to Andes Direct of **€0.45 per kg** for the right to fix these prices. Total cover cost:  
(1,200 + 800 + 500) × €0.45 = **€1,125**.

This is accounted as a hedging expense in Q4 2026, not added to the green cost per kilo.

## 3. New price per bag (2027 list)

Each bag is 12 kg roasted. Roast loss averages 16% for all coffees. Effective green needed per bag: 12 kg ÷ 0.84 = 14.29 kg green.

| Coffee | Green cost per bag (14.29 kg × locked price) | Roasting + packing cost per bag | Total cost per bag | 2027 list price per bag | Margin per bag |
|--------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|-------------------|------------------------|----------------|
| Aurora | 14.29 × €7.40 = €105.75 | €18.00 | €123.75 | **€165.00** | €41.25 |
| Ethiopia Guji | 14.29 × €9.80 = €140.04 | €18.00 | €158.04 | **€210.00** | €51.96 |
| Night Shift | 14.29 × €8.60 = €122.89 | €18.00 | €140.89 | **€185.00** | €44.11 |

## 4. Margin before and after the hedge

**Before hedge** (using spot prices on 15 Sept):

| Coffee | Spot green cost per bag | Total cost per bag | Margin at 2026 list price | Margin % |
|--------|------------------------|-------------------|--------------------------|----------|
| Aurora | 14.29 × €8.10 = €115.75 | €133.75 | 2026 list €160 → €26.25 | 16.4% |
| Ethiopia Guji | 14.29 × €11.20 = €160.05 | €178.05 | 2026 list €200 → €21.95 | 11.0% |
| Night Shift | 14.29 × €9.40 = €134.33 | €152.33 | 2026 list €175 → €22.67 | 13.0% |

**After hedge (2027 list):**

| Coffee | Margin per bag | Margin % |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Aurora | €41.25 | 25.0% |
| Ethiopia Guji | €51.96 | 24.7% |
| Night Shift | €44.11 | 23.8% |

The hedge improves margins by **5–14 percentage points** across the range. The cover cost (€1,125) is a fixed overhead – it reduces net profit by that amount but does not affect unit margins.
