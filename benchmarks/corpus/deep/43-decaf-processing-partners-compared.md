# Decaf processing partners compared

**Document prepared by:** Elena Moretti, Head of Sourcing  
**Date:** 22 September 2026  
**Context:** Evaluation for Night Shift green coffee decaffeination – 2027 contract cycle.

## Overview
We currently process Night Shift through Swiss Water (Burnaby, Canada). With rising shipping costs and a desire for shorter lead times, we explored two alternatives: a sugarcane ethyl acetate (EA) plant in Colombia (Decafé Andino) and a CO₂ plant in Hamburg, Germany (Nordic Decaf GmbH). Below is a direct comparison.

---

## 1. Swiss Water – Burnaby, Canada

| Criteria | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Method** | Chemical-free, water-only extraction (Green Coffee Extract). No solvents. |
| **Min. lot** | 300 kg green (one pallet). |
| **Cost per kg** | €4.85/kg (FOB Burnaby, incl. processing + bagging). |
| **Lead time** | 18–22 business days from arrival at their dock. |
| **Cup results** | Clean, neutral; slightly thinner body than non-decaf. No off-flavours. Good for milk-based blends. |

**Notes:**  
- Current partner since 2023. Reliable but long lead time.  
- Shipping from Mombasa to Vancouver adds 14–18 days before processing.  
- Contact: Sarah Chen (sarah@swisswater.com). Last quote received 10 Sept 2026.

---

## 2. Decafé Andino – Cali, Colombia (EA, sugarcane)

| Criteria | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Method** | Ethyl acetate from sugarcane fermentation. Contact with ethyl acetate vapours, then steam. |
| **Min. lot** | 150 kg green (half pallet). |
| **Cost per kg** | €3.60/kg (FOB Cali, incl. processing + GrainPro bags). |
| **Lead time** | 6–8 business days from green receipt at their warehouse. |
| **Cup results** | Sample cupped 15 Sept 2026: slightly fruity ester note, clean finish. Body medium. Acceptable for filter and espresso. |

**Notes:**  
- Shipping from Ethiopia to Cali via Panama Canal: ~22 days, but processing window is smaller.  
- We could combine with a Colombia green purchase (e.g., from Andes Direct’s Huila lot) to reduce per-kg freight.  
- Contact: Ana Lucía Rojas (ana@decafeandino.co). Quote valid until 15 Nov 2026.  
- **Risk:** EA residue limits (EU max 10 mg/kg). Their lab reports show <2 mg/kg. We need third-party test.

---

## 3. Nordic Decaf GmbH – Hamburg, Germany (CO₂)

| Criteria | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Method** | Supercritical CO₂ extraction (liquid CO₂ at high pressure). No solvents. |
| **Min. lot** | 500 kg green (one full pallet). |
| **Cost per kg** | €5.40/kg (FOB Hamburg, incl. processing + vacuum packing). |
| **Lead time** | 10–12 business days from green arrival at their Hamburg warehouse. |
| **Cup results** | Sample cupped 18 Sept 2026: very clean, high acidity retention, full body. Best cup of the three. Comparable to non-decaf. |

**Notes:**  
- Shipping from Mombasa to Hamburg: ~12 days. Total lead time ~24 days.  
- Minimum lot is large for Night Shift (we roast ~120 kg/month). Would require stockpiling.  
- Contact: Dr. Klaus Weber (k.weber@nordicdecaf.de). Quote received 20 Sept 2026.  
- **Advantage:** EU-based, no import duties on processed decaf (vs. Canada and Colombia).

## Recommendation for Night Shift (2027)

- **First choice:** Decafé Andino (EA, Colombia) – lowest cost, smallest minimum, shortest lead time. Cup quality acceptable for our wholesale clients (Café Portello already approved the sample). Risk of EA residue is manageable with testing.
- **Second choice:** Nordic Decaf – best cup, but minimum lot is too high for our current volume. Consider if we increase Night Shift production to 500 kg/month (requires adding a second roasting day or longer shifts).
- **Third choice:** Swiss Water – keep as fallback if EA stigma becomes an issue with home subscribers.
