## 1. Price per parcel by weight band (EUR, excl. VAT)

We compared three carriers used in autumn 2026: **Poste Delivery**, **TransAlp Express**, and **Nordic Parcel**.

|------------|----------------|------------------|---------------|
| 0–0.5 kg   | 4.90           | 5.40             | 5.10          |
| 0.5–1 kg   | 6.20           | 6.80             | 6.50          |

*Notes:* Poste is cheapest up to 5 kg. Over 10 kg, Nordic becomes competitive but still above Poste. TransAlp is consistently €0.50–€2.50 more per parcel.

---

## 2. Fuel and remote-area surcharges

- **Poste Delivery:** 4.5% fuel surcharge on base rate, applied to all parcels. No remote-area fee for mainland Italy; for islands (Sicily, Sardinia) +€1.20 per parcel. For Alpine villages (above 1,200 m) +€2.00.
- **TransAlp Express:** 6.0% fuel surcharge, but no remote-area fee for any Italian address, including islands. However, they add €0.80 for "low-density zones" — we found this applies to parts of Valle d’Aosta and some Piedmont hill towns.
- **Nordic Parcel:** 5.2% fuel surcharge. Remote-area fee of €1.50 for any address outside provincial capitals. This hit us hard — about 18% of our subscribers live in those zones.

**Effective cost for a typical 1 kg subscription bag (base €6.50, Nordic):**  
Poste = €6.20 × 1.045 = €6.48. TransAlp = €6.80 × 1.06 = €7.21. Nordic = €6.50 × 1.052 + €1.50 (if remote) = €8.34. So Nordic’s low base is a trap.

## 3. Delivery times (from Turin packing day, Thursday)

*Key:* TransAlp is consistently 1–2 days faster for south and EU. Poste is fine for north/central. Nordic is slowest for islands — we had 3 complaints from Sicily in October.

---

## 4. Pickup windows (our roastery, via cargo bike to local depot)

- **Poste Delivery:** Pickup window Mon–Fri, 08:30–10:30. We must have parcels ready by 08:15. Late drop-off? No same-day pickup.
- **TransAlp Express:** Pickup window Mon–Sat, 09:00–12:00. More flexible — we can pack until 11:30. Also, they offer an optional 16:00–18:00 window for +€1.80/stop, which we tested for Café Portello’s weekly order. Worth it.
- **Nordic Parcel:** Pickup window Mon–Fri, 07:45–09:15. Earliest but also most restrictive — if our roaster runs late (which happens on dark mornings), we miss it.

---

## 5. Team preference & reasoning

- **Giulia:** Prefers TransAlp for reliability and speed, especially for Café Portello’s espresso subscriptions in Milan and our few German wholesale accounts. The extra €0.60–€0.90 per parcel is worth the drop in "where is my coffee?" calls.
- **Marco:** Agrees, but notes that for home subscribers in Turin and Piedmont, Poste is fine and cheaper. He suggests a hybrid: Poste for ≤2 kg within 150 km, TransAlp for everything else.
- **Elena:** Strongly dislikes Nordic — the remote-area surcharge is opaque and caused 5 customer complaints in November. She wants TransAlp for all subscriptions, even if it means raising shipping from €5.90 to €6.50.
- **Tommaso:** Points out that TransAlp’s Saturday pickup helps us clear Friday’s roast before the weekend — Poste and Nordic don’t offer that. He also noted that TransAlp’s fuel surcharge is 6% but their base is higher, so the total is predictable.

**Vote:** 3 for TransAlp, 1 for Poste (Marco), 0 for Nordic.
