This wiki belongs to **Lumen Coffee Roasters**, a small roastery in Turin that does not exist.
It was written to show what an LLM wiki looks like after a few months of use:

- every note sits in a folder the librarian chose, and each folder is described in its parent's index;
- related notes are joined by *See also* links, drawn as red dashes in the **Brain** view;
- every note keeps the source it was written from, under `raw/`.

Browse the folders on the left, open the Brain, or ask a question such as
*"What did we decide about prices in May?"* (asking needs a model: set one in Settings).
Drop your own files and they are filed next to these notes; this copy of the demo is yours to change.
