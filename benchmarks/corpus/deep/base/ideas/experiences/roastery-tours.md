Open the roastery on Saturday mornings:

- a one-hour tour of the roaster and the green coffee store;
- a cupping of three coffees, led by the head roaster;
- 12 people at most, €15 each, with 10% off coffee bought on the day.

It could bring new subscribers from the neighbourhood.
