Green coffee is the largest cost, and its price moved by more than 30% in a year.

The rule since January 2026:

1. Every six months, estimate the volume needed from sales.
2. Fix the price for 70% of it with the importers.
3. Buy the remaining 30% at the market price when needed.

This kept costs stable in the first half of 2026, but the July fixings came in higher,
which is why retail prices change from July.
