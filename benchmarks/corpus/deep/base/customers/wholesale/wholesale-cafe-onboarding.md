How a new café becomes a wholesale customer:

1. Tasting at the roastery with the owner and the head barista.
2. A 2 kg trial of Aurora at wholesale price.
3. A visit to calibrate the grinder and the espresso recipe.
4. Payment on delivery for the first three orders, then 30 days.

Wholesale prices are in the price list; the minimum order is 5 kg.
