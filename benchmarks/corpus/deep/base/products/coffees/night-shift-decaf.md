Night Shift is decaffeinated with the Swiss Water process (no solvents), from a Huila lot bought through Andes Direct.

- Tasting notes: caramel, red apple, cocoa.
- Roasted a little darker than Aurora, because decaf beans take heat faster and brown early.
- 12% of subscription orders, rising every winter.

Wholesale cafés often ask for training on it: decaf needs a slightly coarser grind.
