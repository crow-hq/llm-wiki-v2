# Café Portello asks for a different wholesale price on Aurora

Email thread summarised by Marco, 22 September 2026.

The owner of Café Portello wrote on Friday. Their volume of Aurora has grown, and they would like to talk about the wholesale price per kilo in the price list, which is currently €21.50.

## Their request

- A price of €20.00 per kilo for Aurora in 5 kg bags, valid for twelve months.
- In exchange they commit to a minimum of 20 kg a week and will put Lumen on their menu board as the house coffee.
- Night Shift stays at the list price of €24.00.

## Our considerations

At €20.00 the gross margin on Aurora in 5 kg bags falls from about 41% to about 36%. That is below what we accepted in the May pricing meeting, where wholesale rose only 5% in order to keep cafés like this one. On the other hand Portello is one of our largest customers, always pays on time, and a 20 kg weekly commitment helps us plan roasts on Monday.

If we accept, we would create a volume tier in the price list for cafés above 20 kg a week, and other cafés might ask for the same.

## To decide

Marco and Giulia will decide this week whether to offer €20.50 with a 12-month commitment as a counter-proposal, and whether the tier should be written into the wholesale terms for all cafés.
