# Lumen Coffee School: the autumn courses

## Programme overview
Our school runs in the roastery at Via Bologna 14, Turin. All courses include coffee, milk (where applicable), printed take-home notes, and a 250g bag of beans chosen for the session. Maximum group size is 6 seats to keep hands-on time high.

**What’s covered:** grind size, water temperature (92–96°C), bloom time, ratio (1:15 to 1:17). Each participant brews three cups using Ethiopia Guji single origin. We provide a Hario V60 and a Bodum French press to practise on; you take home the printed brew guide and the 250g bag of Guji.

**Included:** all equipment, coffee, filtered water, milk alternatives on request, pastry from Panetteria Grosso (next door).

## 2. Tasting: coffee cupping & flavour mapping

**Trainer:** Matteo Rinaldi (green buyer & Q-grader, Lumen since 2019)

| Session | Date | Time | Seats left | Price per seat |
|---------|------|------|------------|----------------|
| Taste 1 | Sat 4 Oct 2026 | 10:00–12:00 | FULL | € 45 |
| Taste 2 | Sat 18 Oct 2026 | 10:00–12:00 | 1 of 6 | € 45 |
| Taste 3 | Sat 8 Nov 2026 | 10:00–12:00 | 5 of 6 | € 45 |
| Taste 4 | Sat 22 Nov 2026 | 14:00–16:00 | 3 of 6 | € 45 |

**What’s covered:** cupping protocol (SCA method), identifying acidity, body, sweetness, aftertaste. We cup five coffees: our Aurora espresso blend (washed Colombia + natural Brazil), Ethiopia Guji (washed), a natural-process Costa Rica from Andes Direct, a decaf (Night Shift, Swiss Water), and a mystery coffee from a new producer. Matteo walks through each origin’s altitude, processing, and flavour notes.

**Included:** cupping spoons, scoring sheets, spit cups, water crackers, a 250g bag of the mystery coffee to take home.

## 3. Latte art: hearts, rosettas & tulips

**Trainer:** Sofia Conti (shift barista & latte art champion, Lumen since 2022)

| Session | Date | Time | Seats left | Price per seat |
|---------|------|------|------------|----------------|
| Latte 1 | Sun 21 Sep 2026 | 14:00–16:30 | FULL | € 65 |
| Latte 2 | Sun 5 Oct 2026 | 14:00–16:30 | 1 of 6 | € 65 |
| Latte 3 | Sun 19 Oct 2026 | 14:00–16:30 | 3 of 6 | € 65 |
| Latte 4 | Sun 2 Nov 2026 | 14:00–16:30 | 2 of 6 | € 65 |

**What’s covered:** steaming milk to correct microfoam (45–55°C), pitcher angle, pour height, wrist motion for heart, rosetta, and tulip. Each participant pulls four double shots on our La Marzocco Linea PB (roastery machine) and pours four designs. Sofia demonstrates each shape, then coaches individually.

**Included:** whole milk (Latteria Valsesia), oat milk (Rude Health) on request, 250g bag of Aurora espresso blend, a 350ml Lumen latte cup to keep.

## Important notes

- All courses take place in the roastery’s training space (ground floor, next to the 15 kg Diedrich roaster). Doors open 15 minutes before start.
- Payment due at booking via bank transfer or in person at the roastery (cash or card). No refunds within 7 days of the session; you can transfer your seat to another person.
- Dietary needs: please email school@lumencoffee.example at least 48 hours ahead.
- Minors under 16 must be accompanied by a paying adult.
- The roastery is wheelchair accessible (ramp at side entrance).
