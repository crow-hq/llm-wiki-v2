## Roaster’s gas share & afterburner

The roaster itself uses ~0.35 m³ gas per kg roasted (average 2026). The afterburner consumes an additional ~0.42 m³ per kg. Combined gas per kg: 0.77 m³. In December, when we roasted 3,050 kg, the afterburner alone burned 1,281 m³ of gas — that’s €1,921 just to oxidise the smoke.

**What stands out:**

- **Afterburner is the single biggest energy consumer** — 42% of electricity and 55% of gas. We knew it was hungry, but seeing it at 1,680 kWh/month in October (peak autumn) confirms we should prioritise a heat-recovery retrofit. Diego mentioned at the 15 November team meeting that a quote from EcoTherm is due by March 2027.
- **Seasonal heating spike:** From November to January, heating adds ~200 kWh/month vs. June. That’s modest, but the warehouse electric radiators are inefficient — Paola (facilities) flagged on 3 December that the old units in the east bay run at 2.5 kW each.
- **Packing line efficiency:** On weekly packing days (Thursdays), the heat sealer draws 3.2 kW for 6 hours straight. In December we packed 1,200 subscription bags (Night Shift + Ethiopia Guji) plus 600 wholesale boxes for Café Portello. That’s 440 kWh for the month — about 0.14 kWh per bag. Acceptable, but we could batch longer runs.
- **Kilos per kWh (electric):** 31,050 kg / 43,750 kWh = 0.71 kg/kWh. That’s slightly worse than 2025’s 0.75. The afterburner fan upgrade in April didn’t help as much as hoped — it saved only 3% on electric.
- **Cost per kg roasted:** Total energy cost €30,660 / 31,050 kg = €0.99/kg. Gas alone is €0.56/kg, electricity €0.42/kg. If we can cut afterburner gas by 15% (via heat recovery), that saves ~€1,600/year.
