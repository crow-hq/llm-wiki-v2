# Caffè Dora: complaint about late parcels

**From:** Matteo Rinaldi, owner of Caffè Dora  
**To:** Lumen Coffee Roasters – Attn: Elena Sartori (Wholesale Account Manager)  
**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Subject:** Three late deliveries – urgent remedy required

---

Matteo here. I need to register a formal complaint about three parcels that arrived late via your carrier, Corriere Nord Italia, over the past two weeks. This is not the first time, but the cumulative effect is now damaging my business.

## The orders and their delays

| Order # | Product | Qty (kg) | Ordered | Promised by Lumen | Actual arrival | Delay |
|---------|---------|----------|---------|------------------|----------------|-------|
| 4871 | Aurora espresso (blend) | 12 | 28 Sep | 2 Oct | 6 Oct | 4 days |
| 4923 | Ethiopia Guji (single origin) | 8 | 5 Oct | 9 Oct | 14 Oct | 5 days |
| 4967 | Night Shift (decaf) | 6 | 12 Oct | 16 Oct | 18 Oct (today) | 2 days |

Total volume affected: 26 kg – that is 62% of my monthly order from you (I average 42 kg/month). For a café that does 180–200 covers a day in autumn, this is catastrophic.

## Effect on Caffè Dora

- **Friday 2 Oct:** No Aurora. I had to pull a substandard espresso from a backup roaster (Caffè Torino). My regulars noticed. Two told me they’d go to Café Portello next time.
- **Wednesday 14 Oct:** No Guji. I had already printed the weekly pour-over menu featuring it. I substituted a cheaper Kenyan from another supplier. My pour-over sales dropped 40% that day.
- **Today, 18 Oct:** Night Shift arrived at 10:00 – three hours after my morning decaf rush. I had to tell three customers we were out. One left without ordering.

I estimate lost revenue of €340 in direct sales, plus reputational damage I cannot quantify. My staff spent 2.5 extra hours on the phone with Corriere Nord Italia and with you. That is time I pay for.

## What I expect from Lumen

1. **Full credit for the shipping costs** of all three orders (€12 each = €36 total).
2. **A 15% discount on my next order** (approx. €38 off a €250 order) as goodwill.
3. **Immediate switch to a different carrier** for my account – either GLS or DHL. I cannot absorb another week of delays.
4. **A written commitment** that future orders will ship within 24 hours of roasting, not 48, and that you will provide real-time tracking links, not just a number.

If I do not see a concrete plan by Friday 23 October, I will pause my account and source from Caffè Torino permanently.

Matteo,

Thank you for the detailed complaint. I have reviewed every parcel with Luca and Giulia. You are right to be angry. Here is what we found.

## Carrier tracking and delays

| Parcel | Handed to carrier | Corriere promised delivery | First delay event | Actual delivery |
|--------|------------------|---------------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 4871 | 1 Oct, 14:30 | 2 Oct | 2 Oct 08:00 – “sorting centre overload, Turin hub” | 6 Oct |

| 4967 | 15 Oct, 16:00 | 16 Oct | 16 Oct 07:00 – “incorrect address label” (our fault: we wrote “Via Roma 12” instead of “Corso Roma 12”) | 18 Oct |

The first two delays are entirely Corriere’s fault. The third is partly ours – a typo that the carrier did not flag for 24 hours. We have already filed a formal claim with Corriere for the first two. They have acknowledged liability and will credit us €24 (€12 per parcel). I will pass that full amount to you.

## What we are doing

- **Immediate carrier change:** Starting with your next order (due 23 Oct), we will ship via GLS Business Express. I have already set up your account. GLS guarantees next-day delivery for Turin from our roastery. You will receive a tracking link by SMS and email.
- **Compensation:** I authorise a full shipping refund (€36) and a 10% discount on your next order (€25). I cannot do 15% – our margin on wholesale is thin – but I will add a free 1 kg bag of our limited-release Yemeni single origin (value €28) as a gesture.
- **Process change:** We now ship within 24 hours of roasting for all wholesale accounts. For Caffè Dora, I will personally check every label before it goes to the carrier.
- **Written commitment:** Attached is a service-level agreement signed by Luca and me. It promises same-day dispatch if the order is placed before 12:00, carrier tracking within one hour of pickup, and a penalty of €15 per day for any delay beyond the carrier’s guaranteed window.

I am sorry, Matteo. This should not have happened. I will call you tomorrow at 10:00 to confirm the details. If you are not satisfied with the 10% + free bag, I will escalate to our owner, Chiara.
