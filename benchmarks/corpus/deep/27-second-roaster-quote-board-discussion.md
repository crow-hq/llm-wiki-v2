# Second roaster: quotes and discussion

Meeting between Marco, Giulia and the head roaster on 30 September 2026 to look at the quotes for a second roaster, following the board's request in June to study the idea.

## The quotes

| Option | Capacity | Price | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| New 15 kg drum roaster, same maker | 15 kg | €48,000 | 14 weeks |
| New 30 kg drum roaster | 30 kg | €71,000 | 18 weeks |
| Refurbished 15 kg roaster | 15 kg | €27,500 | 6 weeks |

The current 15 kg roaster now runs five days a week, and after the service it is at about 90% of its capacity.

## Technical points

- The same maker would give identical roast profiles, so the Aurora reference profile can be reused with small changes.
- A 30 kg machine needs a stronger gas line and a new exhaust duct, an extra €6,000.
- The refurbished machine has no warranty and the logging software is old.

## Discussion

The head roaster prefers the new 15 kg machine: two identical roasters give a backup when one is being serviced. Giulia prefers the refurbished machine for cost reasons. Marco wants a clear financing plan before presenting a proposal to the board in November.

## Actions

1. Giulia: financing options with the bank.
2. Head roaster: check floor space and the electrical load.
3. Marco: put the roaster decision on the November board agenda.
