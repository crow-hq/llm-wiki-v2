# Wholesale credit limits and overdue invoices

Written by Giulia (accounts), 14 September 2026, for everyone who talks to café customers.

Until now every wholesale café has paid at 30 days with no formal ceiling. After two late payments over the summer we are adding a simple credit limit.

## Limits

| Customer size | Weekly volume | Credit limit |
|---|---|---|
| Small | up to 5 kg | €400 |
| Medium | 5 to 15 kg | €1,200 |
| Large | over 15 kg | €3,000 |

New cafés still pay on delivery for the first three orders; the limit starts to apply after that.

## When an invoice is overdue

1. Day 31: automatic reminder email from the accounting tool.
2. Day 38: Giulia phones the owner and notes the call in the customer file.
3. Day 45: new orders are held until the invoice is paid. Deliveries already scheduled for that week go out once.
4. Day 60: we talk to Marco about whether the relationship continues.

## Exceptions

A café can ask for a temporary higher limit in writing, for example before a festival or a seasonal opening. Giulia approves up to 50% more for a maximum of two months.

We do not charge late-payment interest for now; the reminders have been enough so far.
