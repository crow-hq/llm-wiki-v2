---

## Current consumption (September 2026 data)

| Item | Monthly kWh | Monthly EUR |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Roaster (15 kg, 4 days/week) | 2,100 | 420 |
| Afterburner (natural gas) | 4,800 | 720 |
| Space heating (electric) | 1,200 | 240 |
| **Total** | **8,100** | **1,380** |

Afterburner alone accounts for 52% of the energy bill. Exhaust gas exits at 280–320°C.

---

## Proposed system

Install an air-to-water heat exchanger on the roaster exhaust pipe (before the afterburner) and a smaller unit on the afterburner stack. Recovered heat will preheat incoming green coffee (reducing roast time) and supply warm air to the packing area (currently heated by electric radiators).  

**Supplier:** Ecosave S.r.l. (Turin), quote #Q-1067-EC  
**Equipment:** Two plate heat exchangers, ductwork, glycol loop, controls.  
**Installation window:** 16–20 November 2026 (roastery closed for maintenance week).

## Expected annual saving

- Afterburner gas: reduce from 4,800 kWh/month to 3,100 kWh/month → saving **1,700 kWh/month × 12 = 20,400 kWh**  
- Space heating: reduce from 1,200 kWh/month to 400 kWh/month → saving **800 kWh/month × 12 = 9,600 kWh**  
- Roast time: 8% faster due to preheated beans → saving **~168 kWh/month × 12 = 2,016 kWh**  

**Total energy saved:** 32,016 kWh/year  
**At current rates:** €0.15/kWh (gas) + €0.20/kWh (electric, blended) → **€5,400/year**  

**Payback:** €17,050 ÷ €5,400 = **3.2 years**

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Exhaust backpressure affects roast profile | Medium | Ecosave guarantees ≤ 5 Pa increase; Luca will test with 10 kg of Ethiopia Guji before sign-off |
| Afterburner efficiency drops | Low | Heat exchanger installed downstream of the afterburner’s primary zone; no interference |
| Winter savings lower than modelled | Medium | Based on September data; worst-case scenario (mild winter) reduces saving to €4,200/year → payback 4.1 years |
| Maintenance access | Low | Heat exchangers are on quick-release flanges; cleaning every 6 months (2 hours by Luca) |
