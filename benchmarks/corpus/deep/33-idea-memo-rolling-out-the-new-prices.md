# Idea: rolling out the new prices

**To:** All team  
**From:** Marta, roastery lead  
**Date:** 2 September 2026  
**Subject:** New bag prices — live from 1 October

Hi everyone,

After the sourcing meeting on 28 August (present: Marta, Luca, Elena, Giacomo), we agreed to try a moderate price adjustment. Green coffee costs from Andes Direct have risen 8–12% this year, and our margins on wholesale are too thin. This memo confirms the new per-bag and per-kilo prices for wholesale and retail, effective **1 October 2026**. No further changes until spring 2027.

**Who does what:**  
- **Luca** updates all invoices in the CRM by 25 September.  
- **Elena** changes the online shop (prices, product descriptions, and the subscription tier amounts) by 28 September.  
- **Giacomo** writes a short email to wholesale clients (Café Portello, Bar Torino, Ditta Artigianale, and the other 11 accounts) by 29 September.  
- **Marta** posts a note on the shop homepage and in the weekly subscription packing sheet.

**How we tell customers:**  
- Wholesale: Giacomo’s email explains the increase (green coffee costs) and offers a 5% discount on first orders in October if they lock in a 3-month contract at the new price.  
- Retail/subscribers: Elena adds a banner on the checkout page: “New prices from 1 October — same quality, fairer sourcing.” Subscribers are notified in the 27 September shipping confirmation.
