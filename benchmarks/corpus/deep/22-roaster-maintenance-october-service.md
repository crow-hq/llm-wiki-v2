# Roaster maintenance: October service and chaff findings

The yearly burner service of the 15 kg roaster took place on 29 and 30 September 2026. This note adds the results to the maintenance schedule.

## Yearly service

- Burner cleaned and the gas pressure adjusted. The technician found the burner jets partly blocked, which explained why the roast needed more gas at yellowing in August.
- Thermocouple calibrated: the bean probe was reading 1.5 °C high; it was reset.
- Drum bearings greased. One bearing is worn and should be replaced in the next six months.
- Afterburner not present on this machine; the technician recommended one if the second roaster is bought.

## Exhaust duct

The monthly cleaning was moved forward after the technician found a layer of oil residue in the elbow near the roof. From now on the exhaust duct is cleaned every three weeks in the dark roast season, not every month, as there are more oils on darker roasts.

## Chaff collector

The weekly cleaning is not enough on Mondays after a day of five batches. The head roaster will empty it halfway through a long roast day as well.

## Capacity

Because the roaster is used five days a week, the service was done on a Wednesday and Thursday, with a loss of about 160 kg of coffee roasted. Subscriptions were not affected because Monday's roast had been brought forward.

## Costs

Service €780, bearing replacement estimated at €420.
