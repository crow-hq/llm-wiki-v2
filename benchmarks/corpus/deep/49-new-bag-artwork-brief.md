## 1. Brand story to communicate

We are Lumen Coffee Roasters – Turin-based, roasting on a single 15 kg Probat since 2019. Twelve people, one roaster, four roasting days (Monday–Thursday), one packing day (Friday). We supply wholesale cafés (Café Portello is our anchor client) and home subscribers. Our green coffee comes via importers like Andes Direct. The bag artwork must reflect:

- **Small-scale precision**: every batch is cupped, every roast curve logged manually.  
- **Transparency**: origin, roast date, tasting notes are printed on every bag. No generic “dark roast” labels.  
- **Connection**: the customer should feel they’re buying from a roastery that knows each farmer, each lot.  
- **Turin identity**: subtle references to the city (Mole Antonelliana silhouette, vintage tram lines, or a simple “Torino” stamp) are welcome, but not dominant.

**Tone**: warm, minimal, industrial yet approachable. No stock photography. Use line-art or abstract watercolour. Colours should be matte, not glossy.

---

## 2. Mandatory information on every bag

Each bag (250 g or 1 kg) must display the following, in this order of importance:

| Element | Position | Font / style | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coffee name** | Front, top third | 24 pt bold sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat) | e.g., “Aurora Espresso”, “Ethiopia Guji”, “Night Shift” |
| **Roast date** | Front, bottom right | 10 pt, monospace (e.g., JetBrains Mono) | Format: DD/MM/2026. Example: 15/10/2026 |
| **Tasting notes** | Back, upper half | 12 pt italic, one line per note | e.g., “dark chocolate, ripe plum, clean finish” |
| **Origin** | Back, below tasting notes | 11 pt regular | e.g., “Guji Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia” |
| **Recycling mark** | Back, bottom left | Standard EU recycling triangle (PET 1) | Must be at least 12 mm tall, black outline |
| **Net weight** | Front, bottom left | 10 pt regular | “250 g” or “1 kg” |
| **Lumen logo** | Front, top left | Current logomark (circle + “L”) | Do not redesign the logo |

**Additional back panel text** (small, 8 pt):  
*“Roasted by Lumen Coffee Roasters, Turin, Italy. Store in a cool, dry place. Consume within 4 weeks of roast date for best flavour.”*

## 3. Colour per range

Three distinct colour palettes, no overlap. Each range must be visually identifiable from 2 metres away on a café shelf.

| Range | Primary bag colour | Secondary accent | Pantone reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aurora Espresso** (blend) | Deep rust red | Warm cream | 18-1448 TCX (Autumn Leaf) + 13-0907 TCX (Bone White) |
| **Ethiopia Guji** (single origin) | Soft sage green | Charcoal grey | 16-0220 TCX (Lichen) + 18-4003 TCX (Grey Flannel) |
| **Night Shift** (decaf) | Midnight blue | Pale amber | 19-3920 TCX (Dark Navy) + 14-1050 TCX (Golden Yellow) |

No other colours may appear on the bag except black ink for text and the logo (which is dark grey, not black).

---

## 4. Bag sizes and structural details

We use two bag formats, both Kraft paper with a one-way degassing valve:

- **250 g flat bottom bag**: 80 x 120 x 220 mm (W x D x H). Valve on the back, 2 cm from top edge.  
- **1 kg gusset bag**: 110 x 160 x 310 mm. Valve on the front, 3 cm from top edge.

Artwork must be supplied as separate files per size. Do not scale the 250 g design to 1 kg – proportions differ.

The degassing valve is a black circular patch (25 mm diameter). Artwork must not cover it. Leave a 30 mm clear zone around the valve position.

## 6. Additional notes

- No barcode required (we use QR codes on the back, but those will be added by us post-printing as stickers).  
- The recycling mark must be placed so it is not obscured by the degassing valve or the QR code sticker area.  
- Colour proofs must be viewed under D50 lighting at the printer’s – we had a mismatch on the last batch (Aurora looked brick-red instead of rust).  
- If you want to reference Turin, keep it subtle: a single-line outline of the Mole Antonelliana on the side gusset of the 1 kg bag is acceptable. Do not put it on the 250 g bag.
