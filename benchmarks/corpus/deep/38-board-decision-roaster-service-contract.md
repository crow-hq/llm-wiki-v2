# Board decision: the roaster service contract

**Date:** 5 October 2026  
**Attendees:** Marco (CEO/owner), Elena (Head Roaster), Luca (Operations), Chiara (Finance), Davide (Wholesale), Sofia (Subscriptions)  
**Absent:** Tom (part-time, sick leave)  
**Meeting called by:** Marco  

## Agenda item: Service contract for the 15 kg roaster (Giesen W15, serial 2024-07)  
**Roaster age:** 2 years 3 months. Annual maintenance due November 2026.

### Offer A – "Full Care" from Giesen Italia  
- **Annual cost:** €4,200 (fixed, no indexation for year 1)  
- **Response time:**  
  - Critical (roaster down): 24 hours, on-site within 48 hours  
  - Non-critical: 72 hours, on-site within 5 working days  
- **Spare parts included:** All wear items (burner nozzles, drum seals, thermocouple probes, air filter) – **except** motor bearings and drum motor.  
- **Labour:** Included (max 8 hours per visit, then €90/hour)  
- **Contract term:** 12 months, renewable, 30-day notice from either party  

### Offer B – "Essential Support" from independent technician (Paolo Varese, certified for Giesen)  
- **Annual cost:** €2,800 (fixed, includes two scheduled visits per year)  
- **Response time:**  
  - Critical: 48 hours, on-site within 72 hours  
  - Non-critical: 5 working days, on-site within 10 working days  
- **Spare parts included:** None (parts billed at cost + 15% handling)  
- **Labour:** Included for scheduled visits; emergency visits €120/hour (min 2 hours)  
- **Contract term:** 12 months, non-renewable; must renegotiate after 12 months  

### Discussion  

**Elena:** “Offer A covers the thermocouples – we’ve already replaced one this year. That’s €240 each part. The burner nozzles clog twice a year. Without parts in B, we’d spend €600–€800 extra annually.”  

**Luca:** “Response time is the issue. If the roaster goes down on a Tuesday, we lose Wednesday’s production (15 kg × 4 batches = 60 kg). That’s €900 lost margin minimum. With Offer A, we’re back by Thursday. With B, we might not roast until Friday.”  

**Chiara:** “B is cheaper upfront, but I estimate total cost including parts and emergency labour at €3,900–€4,400 per year. A is €4,200 fixed. The difference is negligible, but A has faster response and predictable cost.”  

**Sofia:** “Subscriptions ship every Friday. If we miss a roasting day, we delay 60 subscriptions. That’s 60 emails and potential churn. The extra €1,400 for A is insurance against that.”  

**Davide:** “Café Portello alone orders 20 kg of Aurora per week. If we miss their delivery, they’ll switch to Caffè Torino. We can’t afford that.”  

**Marco:** “I lean toward A. But let’s vote.”  

### Vote  

| Name | Vote |  
|------|------|  
| Marco | A |  
| Elena | A |  
| Luca | A |  
| Chiara | A |  
| Davide | A |  
| Sofia | A |  

**Result:** Unanimous for Offer A (Full Care, Giesen Italia).
