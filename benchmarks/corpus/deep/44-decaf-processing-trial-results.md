# Decaf processing: trial lots results

**Prepared by:** Marco Rinaldi, Head Roaster  
**Date:** 28 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Evaluate two decaffeination partners for the Night Shift decaf blend, to be used starting spring 2027.

---

## Trial parameters

- **Green coffee base:** Washed Colombia, Huila, Finca El Roble (lot #ER-08-26, purchased via Andes Direct, 69 kg total split into two 34.5 kg samples).  
- **Processing partners:**  
  - **Partner A:** Decaff Italia (Milan) – ethyl acetate (EA) process.  
  - **Partner B:** Swiss Water Decaf (Hamburg, DE) – water-only process.  
- **Timeline:**  
  - Green sent: 12 September 2026  
  - Returned to Turin: 4 October 2026 (Partner A), 7 October 2026 (Partner B)  
  - Roast trials: 10 October 2026  
  - Cupping: 13 October 2026  

---

## Residual caffeine analysis

Samples sent to ChemLab Italia (Bologna) on 14 October. Results received 21 October.

| Partner | Method         | Caffeine (mg/g) | Decaf % | EU standard (<0.1%)? |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|---------|----------------------|
| A       | Ethyl acetate  | 0.09            | 99.1%   | Yes                  |
| B       | Swiss Water    | 0.08            | 99.2%   | Yes                  |

Both meet EU standard (≤0.1% caffeine by mass). Partner B is marginally lower.

---

## Moisture content (before roast)

Measured with Agtron M-800i on 9 October.

| Partner | Moisture % |
|---------|------------|
| A       | 10.4       |
| B       | 10.1       |

Both within acceptable range (9.5–11.5%). Partner B slightly drier, which can affect heat transfer.

---

## Roast behaviour (10 October 2026)

Roasted on the 15 kg Giesen, batch size 3.5 kg each. Target: Agtron #65 (medium-light, Night Shift spec). Ambient temp: 18°C.

| Parameter                | Partner A (EA)     | Partner B (Swiss Water) |
|--------------------------|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Charge temp              | 195°C              | 195°C                    |
| Turning point (time)     | 1:15               | 1:22                     |
| Turning point (temp)     | 94°C               | 92°C                     |
| Development time (DT)    | 2:10               | 2:45                     |
| Total roast time         | 9:30               | 10:15                    |
| End temp                 | 202°C              | 204°C                    |
| Roast loss %             | 14.2%              | 15.1%                    |
| Notes                    | More even colour; few tips scorched | Slightly uneven; 3% more chaff |

**Observation:** Partner A (EA) roasted faster and more evenly. Partner B required longer development to avoid baked notes. Roast loss difference of ~1% is negligible for cost but suggests Partner B beans are slightly more porous.

---

## Cupping scores (13 October 2026)

Blind cupping with **Lena Schubert** (head of quality), **Tomás Costa** (wholesale lead), **Chiara Valli** (barista trainer), and Marco. Two cups per sample, standard SCA form.

| Attribute       | Partner A (EA) | Partner B (Swiss Water) |
|-----------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Fragrance/Aroma | 7.5            | 7.8                      |
| Flavour         | 7.0            | 7.5                      |
| Aftertaste      | 7.0            | 7.3                      |
| Acidity         | 7.2            | 7.4                      |
| Body            | 7.5            | 7.2                      |
| Balance         | 7.3            | 7.1                      |
| Overall         | 7.2            | 7.5                      |
| **Total**       | **79.7**       | **80.8**                 |

**Tasting notes:**

- **Partner A (EA):** Clean but slightly flat. Mild chocolate note, low fruit. Acceptable for decaf, but lacks complexity.  
- **Partner B (Swiss Water):** Brighter acidity (citric-lemon), more tea-like finish, slightly thinner body but higher perceived sweetness.  

**Consensus (4 tasters):** Partner B scores higher on flavour and aroma, though body is weaker. For Night Shift (milk-based drinks at Café Portello, filter for subscribers), the extra brightness is a net gain.

---

## Decision: Partner B (Swiss Water Decaf) from spring 2027

**Rationale:**

1. Higher cupping score (+1.1 points) – critical for subscription sales where decaf is tasted black.  
2. Residual caffeine slightly lower (0.08% vs 0.09%) – marketing advantage.  
3. Roast behaviour is manageable with adjusted profile (lower charge temp by 2°C, increase DT by 15 sec).  
4. Partner B offers a fixed price of €2.80/kg over 2027 (confirmed via email, 25 October). Partner A quoted €2.65/kg but with a 4% annual adjustment clause.  
5. **Risk:** Swiss Water’s longer lead time (3 weeks vs 2 weeks for EA). Mitigation: place first order by 15 February 2027.

**Action items:**

- **Tomás Costa:** Inform Café Portello and top 5 wholesale accounts of change by 15 November. Provide sample of Partner B decaf for their own staff cuppings.  
- **Marco Rinaldi:** Develop new roast profile for Partner B (target: DT 2:45, end temp 203°C). Test on 5 kg batch by 20 November.  
- **Lena Schubert:** Write updated cupping notes for Night Shift label (to be printed in January).  
- **Chiara Valli:** Run a barista training session on the new decaf steam texture (thinner body may require different milk stretching technique) by 1 December.
