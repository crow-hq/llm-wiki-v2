# Pause instead of cancel: pilot results after six weeks

The pause proposal has been tested since 17 August 2026 on the cancellation page of the web shop, for half of the subscribers who clicked "cancel" (the rest saw the old page). This note reports what happened.

## How the pilot worked

When a subscriber clicks "cancel subscription" a first screen offers three options: a pause of one month, a pause of two months, or a delivery every two weeks instead of every week. A link to cancel anyway remains visible.

## Results from 17 August to 28 September

| | Pause page | Old page |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers who started cancelling | 96 | 91 |
| Chose a pause of one month | 31 | n/a |
| Chose a pause of two months | 18 | n/a |
| Switched to every two weeks | 9 | n/a |
| Cancelled anyway | 38 | 91 |

So 60% of the people who started to cancel kept their subscription through a pause or a slower delivery, higher than the "one third" that similar shops reported. Of the subscribers who paused in August, 71% restarted on time.

## Problems

- A few subscribers paused and then asked why they were still charged; the pause did not delay the next charge for people who clicked after the Monday cut-off. This bug is fixed.
- Support asked for a message to be sent one week before a pause ends.

## Recommendation

Make the pause option the standard cancellation page for everybody from 1 October, and measure again in December.
