# Carrier delay in Lombardy: incident report and customer response

Report by Davide, 24 September 2026.

On 8 and 15 September, 61 parcels for Lombardy sent on Tuesday afternoon reached customers after four to six working days instead of 1-2. The carrier says a sorting hub in Piacenza was short of staff. All parcels were eventually delivered and none was lost.

## Effect on customers

- 14 customers wrote to support, three of them subscribers whose coffee arrived after the next roast was already ready.
- Two customers asked for a refund of the shipping fee and one asked to cancel the subscription.
- Reviews on the shop: two one-star ratings that mention the delay.

## What we did

1. Refunded the €5.90 delivery fee to all 61 customers without asking, with an apology email from Elena.
2. Sent a free 250 g bag of Aurora to the three affected subscribers.
3. Explained that the delay was not a roasting problem and invited the cancelling subscriber to pause instead.

## Carrier

Davide asked for a written explanation and for compensation for the delayed parcels. The carrier accepted responsibility for the second week only. A meeting with the carrier's account manager is planned for 6 October.

## Open points

Whether we promise delivery times on the shop page, and whether to write into the policy that late parcels give a right to a shipping refund. We also need a clearer process for who contacts the carrier and who talks to customers.
