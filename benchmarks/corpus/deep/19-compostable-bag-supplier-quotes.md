# Compostable coffee bags: supplier quotes

Davide and Elena asked three suppliers for quotes on compostable bags to replace our current plastic-lined paper bags. The goal is a bag that is certified industrially compostable and keeps coffee fresh for at least 3 months.

## Requirements

- 250 g and 1 kg sizes, flat bottom, with a one-way degassing valve.
- Certified compostable (EN 13432) including the valve and the zip.
- Barrier good enough for 3 months of shelf life after roasting.
- Printing in two colours.

## Quotes for 5,000 bags of 250 g

| Supplier | Price per bag | Minimum | Lead time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Biopack Torino | €0.38 | 3,000 | 4 weeks | local, certified valve |
| GreenSeal | €0.31 | 10,000 | 8 weeks | cheaper, higher minimum |
| Nord Packaging | €0.45 | 2,000 | 3 weeks | recyclable mono-material, not compostable |

Our current bag costs €0.21.

## Considerations

- A move to Biopack would increase packaging costs by about €0.17 a bag, or around 7,000 euros a year at our volume.
- Shelf-life tests are needed: compostable films have a lower oxygen barrier.
- The label must carry disposal instructions. Italian rules require specific wording, which should be coordinated with the certification project.

## Next step

Order 300 sample bags from Biopack, fill them with Aurora and test freshness after 30, 60 and 90 days.
