Dear Lumen team,

With the autumn season approaching, we would like to propose a kraft bag order sized to your actual volumes rather than a standard catalogue quantity. Based on what you have shared with us over the past months, we suggest an initial run of 6,000 bags in the 250 g format and 3,500 in the 1 kg format, with your one-colour print on natural kraft.

For scale, we note that a single large café account can move a considerable amount of one blend each week; Café Gelsomino alone orders 29 kg of Aurora weekly, which translates into roughly 120 retail-format bags per week from that account alone. Across your wholesale and subscription channels, we estimate your weekly consumption at 900–1,100 bags, which the proposed run would cover for about ten weeks, comfortably spanning your autumn packing days.

Production slots for printed kraft are filling quickly before the holiday rush. If you can confirm quantities by 3 July 2027 at the latest for the winter cycle — or, ideally, earlier for autumn — we can hold the current pricing under reference KHM-4362. Delivery would be in two consignments, four weeks apart, to your roastery address.

We are happy to send updated print proofs or sample sheets on request. Please let us know if a call this week would be useful.

Kind regards,

Marta Bellandi
Account Manager, Carta & Sacco S.r.l.
