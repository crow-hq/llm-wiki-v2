Ciao Elio Brambilla,

I'm Marta Colombo, head roaster here at Lumen. We're all looking forward to having you with us when you start on Monday, 23 November 2027.

Here's how your first week will look:

Monday: Arrive at 8:30. We'll do a tour of the roastery, introduce you to the team (we're twelve in total), and go over safety and the basics of the workspace.

Tuesday: You'll spend the morning with our sourcing notes — where our greens come from, how we work with importers like Andes Direct — and the afternoon observing a roast on our 15 kg machine. We roast four days a week, usually turning out around 308 kg, so there's plenty to see.

Wednesday: Cupping day. You'll taste Aurora, Ethiopia Guji and Night Shift with us and learn how we evaluate each batch.

Thursday: Our weekly packing day for home subscribers. Expect boxes, labels and good music.

Friday: You'll shadow me on a full roast cycle and we'll review the week together.

Wear comfortable shoes and clothes you don't mind smelling of coffee permanently — it comes with the territory.

If anything comes up before Monday, just write me here.
