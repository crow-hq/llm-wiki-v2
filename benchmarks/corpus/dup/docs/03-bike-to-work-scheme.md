INTERNAL MEMO

From: Marta Bellini, Head of Administration
To: All staff, Lumen Coffee Roasters
Date: 9 December 2027
Subject: Bike to work scheme

Ciao a tutti,

I'm pleased to announce that from January we will be running a bike to work scheme. Staff who cycle to work at least twelve days in a calendar month will receive a monthly allowance of €40, paid with the following month's salary. The allowance is intended to cover maintenance, tyres and general wear, and it is not taxable in the same way as salary.

How to enrol: send a short email to me at marta.bellini@lumenroasters.example by 15 January with your name and a rough estimate of how often you expect to cycle. You can join at any point during the year, not just in January. If you stop cycling for a month, the allowance pauses; if you start again, just let me know and it resumes. No proof of each trip is needed — we're trusting you, and the scheme is meant to be simple.

Bike parking: we have cleared space in the corner of the loading area next to the roaster, and there are now six wall-mounted racks plus two ground hoops. Please lock your bike to a rack rather than the shelving, and keep the loading dock itself clear for deliveries from Andes Direct. The side door code works as before; if you arrive before 7:00, use the intercom.

A few of you asked about showers. There is no budget for a shower this year, but the washroom has been given a hook and a small bench, which is a start.

For context, when we surveyed staff in November, 14.7% of you said you already cycle regularly in fine weather, so we hope this nudges a few more onto two wheels. The first-year budget for the scheme is €3,806.16, which covers allowances at expected uptake plus the racks.

Questions to me directly or at the Monday ops meeting.

Grazie,
Marta Bellini
Head of Administration
Lumen Coffee Roasters, Turin
