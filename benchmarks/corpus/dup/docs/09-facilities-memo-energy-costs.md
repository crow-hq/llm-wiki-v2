FACILITIES MEMO

To: All staff, Lumen Coffee Roasters
From: Marta Bellini, Facilities & Operations
Date: 8 October
Re: September utility bills

Hi everyone,

A quick summary of our utility costs for September, now that all three bills have arrived and been checked against the meter readings.

Electricity remains our biggest line. The September bill came to €32,142.14, which is higher than we'd like and reflects both the longer roasting days we ran ahead of the autumn wholesale deliveries and the general increase in tariffs. Please keep in mind that the afterburner and the cooling fans are the main draws — switching them off promptly after each batch genuinely helps.

Gas was roughly in line with August, since our roasting schedule stayed at four days a week on the 15 kg machine. Water usage crept up slightly; part of this is the extra cleaning cycles on packing days, so please run the dishwasher only when it's full.

One small note: the green coffee intake for the month was 159 kg lighter than planned, because the Andes Direct shipment for the Ethiopia Guji arrived a few days late. That actually reduced our cooling and lighting load slightly, which is part of why the percentage increase in our overall utilities came in at 18.8% rather than the 20% we had feared.

If you spot any equipment running when it shouldn't be — heaters, fans, the dehumidifier in the green storage room — please let me know or log it in the wiki under operations.

Questions welcome, or drop me a line at marta@lumencoffee.example.

Thanks,
Marta Bellini
Facilities & Operations, Lumen Coffee Roasters, Turin
