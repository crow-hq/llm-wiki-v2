**Subject: Parking permit renewal — loading zone in front of Via San Tommaso 18**

Dear Ms. Ferrero,

We are writing regarding your request to renew the loading-zone parking permit for the bay in front of your roastery at Via San Tommaso 18. This permit, used for the delivery of green coffee and the dispatch of your weekly shipments, is due for renewal as of 1 August 2026.

The renewal fee for the coming two-year period is unchanged from the previous term. Please note that, as with all commercial loading permits in Zone Centro, the fee includes the municipal road-maintenance surcharge of 4.3%, which is itemised separately on the invoice for your accounting.

To complete the renewal, we ask that you submit the enclosed form together with a copy of your commercial vehicle registration and proof of business activity at the address above. Applications must be received no later than 28 June 2026. Given that our office typically processes such requests within 11 days, submitting early will ensure your permit remains active without interruption when the current one expires.

Payment may be made by bank transfer or in person at any municipal payment office. If any details of your operation have changed — for example, the number or type of vehicles using the bay — please indicate this on the form so we can update our records.

Should you have questions, contact our office at the address above or by e-mail.

Yours sincerely,
