Memo: autumn launch planning

From: Sofia Marchetti, Head of Roasting
To: Product team, Operations
Date: 14 September 2026

Team,

This memo sets out the plan for launching our new Colombian single origin, planned as "Cordillera", in time for the autumn season.

The main harvest at the Colombian cooperative starts on 26 May 2026. Since our sourcing calendar is built around that date, everything below is anchored to it: fresh-crop lots are shipped after milling and drying, arriving in Turin in late summer, which gives us a comfortable window for cupping and profile work before the launch.

Proposed timeline:
- Late August: samples arrive via Andes Direct; cupping sessions with the whole roasting team.
- Early September: final lot selection and roast profile development on the 15 kg roaster, scheduled around our four roasting days.
- Mid September: packaging design finalised; wholesale price list updated.
- Early October: launch to wholesale cafés, with Café Portello as our first pilot account.
- Mid October: inclusion in home subscriber boxes on the weekly packing day.

Budget note: the landed cost for the first container, including freight and duties, came to €4,862.24, which keeps us within the sourcing budget approved in spring.

One point for the longer view: our supply agreement with the cooperative runs to 20 October 2027, so we should begin renewal discussions well before the next harvest cycle to secure the same lot quality.

Please send comments on the timeline by the end of next week, or raise them at the Monday operations meeting.

Sofia Marchetti
Head of Roasting, Lumen Coffee Roasters
