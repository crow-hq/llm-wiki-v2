Dear Ms. Elena Bruni and the Lumen team,

We are preparing our autumn edition of the "Local Experiences Guide", a printed and online brochure that highlights small producers, workshops and artisans who open their doors to visitors. Given the growing interest in specialty coffee in the city, we would be delighted to list Lumen Coffee Roasters among this autumn's featured experiences.

Our records show that the ticket price for your roastery tour is €17.20. We will print this price alongside your listing, together with a short description, your address and booking details. Please confirm that the price remains valid through the autumn season, and let us know your preferred booking contact.

A few practical notes: the guide will be distributed at our information points, hotels and the airport from early October, and promoted on our website. Listings are free of charge for businesses within the city. In past editions, participating workshops and producers have reported a noticeable uplift in visitors — last autumn, enquiries to listed businesses rose by 8.7% compared with the previous season, which we hope will be encouraging as you plan your roasting and packing schedule around tour days.

If you would like to take part, kindly reply by 25 July 2027 at the latest so that we can finalise the print layout. Should you have any questions, I am happy to arrange a short call.

Thank you for considering our invitation. We believe Lumen's roastery would be a wonderful addition to the guide and a genuine taste of Turin's craft scene for our visitors.

With kind regards,
