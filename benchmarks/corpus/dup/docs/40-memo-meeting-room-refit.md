Memo: meeting room refit

From: Giulia Ferrante, Operations
To: All staff
Date: 14 November 2027

Ciao everyone,

As those of you who attended last week's roasting review will have noticed, the meeting room upstairs is due for a proper refit. The current table wobbles, the chairs are mismatched leftovers from the old Café Portello tasting days, and the lighting makes everyone look like unroasted beans.

The plan is as follows. We will replace the existing furniture with one large oak table, 320 cm by 110 cm, which comfortably seats the whole team plus a couple of guests. We have ordered 33 people worth of chairs — that is, 33 chairs in total — so that cuppings, subscriber tastings and the Monday production meeting can all happen without anyone dragging a stool in from the packing area.

The refit is scheduled for the week of 20 December 2027, which keeps it clear of the pre-Christmas subscription rush and the shipping backlog. Packing day that week will run as normal; only the meeting room will be out of use, so the Tuesday sourcing call with Andes Direct will move to the cupping bench area.

Budget-wise, the whole job — table, chairs, lighting and a fresh coat of paint — comes in at €39,272.66, which the board approved at the November meeting. It is more than we first quoted, mostly because we upgraded to chairs that can survive espresso spills.

If you have strong opinions about chair colour, reply to me by Friday at giulia@lumencoffee.example. Otherwise, assume charcoal grey.

Thanks for your patience during the works.

Giulia Ferrante
Operations, Lumen Coffee Roasters, Turin
