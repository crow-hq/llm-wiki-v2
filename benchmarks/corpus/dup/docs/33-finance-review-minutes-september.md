# Finance review minutes, September

**Date:** 12 September
**Present:** Marta Bellini (founder), Luca Ferrero (roastery manager), Sofia Grasso (wholesale accounts), Tommaso Riva (subscriptions and shipping)
**Chaired by:** Marta Bellini

Marta opened the quarterly finance review and noted that the roastery remains on budget overall, with green coffee spend slightly above plan due to the strong demand for the Ethiopia Guji.

**Commitments for the quarter**

- Luca confirmed the purchase contract with the Kisumu cooperative, NGR-8062, covers the coming harvest lot. Payments due under it are the advance already paid, the shipment instalment on arrival of the container, and the balance after quality approval at the end of the quarter.
- Sofia reported that Café Portello has confirmed its weekly standing order for Aurora espresso through December, which stabilises a good share of wholesale revenue.
- Tommaso walked through subscription cash flow: packing day falls on Tuesdays, and renewals are tracking well. He flagged that the shipping invoice for last month is pending under reference UVZ-6815 and should clear before the next review.
- Sofia noted that the decaf, Night Shift, continues to grow steadily and now represents 7.8% of monthly sales, mostly through subscriptions.

**Decisions**

- No price changes this quarter; the team agreed to revisit wholesale pricing in the new year.
- Luca to confirm the arrival window for the Kisumu container with Andes Direct and update the sourcing page on the wiki.

**Next review:** early December. Minutes to be filed under Meetings.

Minutes taken by Tommaso Riva.
