Thank-you letter from a primary school

Scuola Primaria di Corso Racconigi
Turin

18 May 2025

Dear Lumen Coffee Roasters,

I am writing on behalf of the whole class to thank you most warmly for your generous donation of €86.05 towards our school trip to the botanical gardens. My name is Livia Marchetti, and I teach the third-year class at the primary school just around the corner from your roastery.

The children were delighted when I told them the trip could go ahead. For many of them, it will be their first time visiting the gardens, and your support has made it possible. We have already begun preparing: the class has been reading about plants, and the children have made a colourful poster to bring along.

It was lovely to learn a little about your work as well. The children were fascinated to hear that your team roasts around 175 kg of coffee in a typical week, and one pupil announced that her parents buy your Aurora blend every month. We also appreciated the bag of Night Shift decaf you sent for the teachers' staff room — it has been very popular during our planning meetings.

Please pass on our thanks to your whole team. If it would be welcome, we would be happy to send you a drawing from the class after the trip, and you are of course invited to our small presentation about the visit in June.

With heartfelt thanks from all of us,

that figure
Third-year class teacher
Scuola Primaria di Corso Racconigi
l.marchetti@scuolaracconigi.example
