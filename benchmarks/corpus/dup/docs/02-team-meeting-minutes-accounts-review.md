Team meeting minutes, accounts review
Lumen Coffee Roasters, Turin
Date: Thursday 14 March, 9:30, roastery back room
Present: Marta (founder), Luca (roasting), Sofia (shipping), Davide (accounts), Elena (wholesale)

Davide opened with the month's accounts. Wholesale revenue was steady, with Café Portello and two other cafés paying on time. Subscription income dipped slightly after a few cancellations, but new sign-ups covered most of the gap. Green coffee costs were higher than last month because of the delayed Andes Direct container; the replacement lot finally cleared customs after 80 days in transit, which squeezed the cash flow more than we'd like. Davide will flag this in next month's forecast.

Luca reported that roasting went smoothly, though the 15 kg machine needs a minor service soon. Sofia confirmed the weekly packing day ran well and the Night Shift decaf is nearly out of stock until the next delivery.

Elena shared news from the wholesale side: at Pasticceria Moretti, purchasing is now handled by Noemi Orsolini. She has already been in touch and will send them the updated price list and the invoice reference MBW-2539 for their standing order of Aurora espresso.

Actions:
- Davide: update cash flow forecast with the delayed container costs.
- Luca: book roaster service.
- Elena: confirm new order terms with Pasticceria Moretti.

Next accounts review: second Thursday of April.
