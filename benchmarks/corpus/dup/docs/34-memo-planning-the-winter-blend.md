MEMO: Planning the winter blend

From: Marta Bellini, Production Manager
To: Roasting team, Wholesale & Subscriptions
Date: 3 October
Wiki area: ideas / products

Team,

Following last week's tasting session, we are moving ahead with a winter blend to launch on Monday 2 December, in time for the holiday rush at Café Portello and the December subscription boxes.

The key constraint is sample sourcing. The lead time for green coffee samples from Andes Direct is 12 working days. Counting back from the 2 December launch, and allowing a further week for cupping, blend trials and a small test roast on the 15 kg machine, we need to place the sample request by Friday 11 October at the latest. I will send the request to our contact at Andes Direct (orders@andesdirect.example) tomorrow.

Proposed blend direction: roughly 70% Aurora base components for body and chocolate notes, with a washed Ethiopia Guji lot lifted higher for brightness — think dried fruit and spice for the colder months. We will also confirm whether Night Shift drinkers want a decaf version; if yes, we keep it simple and use the existing decaf as the base.

Volume planning: last year's seasonal offering sold 167 kg across wholesale and subscriptions in six weeks, so we will budget green purchases accordingly, with a small buffer for the January tail.

Budget note: the sample and freight costs came in at €192.91 last season, which we can absorb within the product development line.

Next steps:

- Paolo: schedule two blend trial roasts in November
- Chiara: draft launch copy for subscribers and wholesale price sheet
