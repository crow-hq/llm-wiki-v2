MEMO: REPORTING DAMAGE AT THE DOCK

To: Roasting team
From: Marta Bellini, Operations Lead
Date: 14 May 2025
Re: What to do when a delivery arrives damaged

Team,

After last week's pallet of green coffee arrived with two torn bags, we agreed to put the procedure in writing so everyone handles it the same way.

1. Do not sign the delivery note as "received in good condition". Sign it and write "damaged on arrival" plus a short description (e.g. "2 bags torn, beans spilled").

2. Photograph everything before moving anything: the pallet, the wrapping, the labels, and close-ups of the damage. Photos go in the shared folder under Sourcing / Deliveries.

3. Weigh and note the affected quantity. Last week this was 114 kg of the Guji lot, which we had to set aside entirely.

4. Email the importer the same day (for Andes Direct: claims@andesdirect.example) with the photos, the delivery note, and a short description. Copy me on every claim.

5. Quote our transit insurance policy number, UHE-5457, in every claim email. The insurer will not open a file without it.

6. Log the incident in the wiki under Sourcing / Deliveries so we can track recurring problems with a carrier or origin.

A reminder of why this matters: our green coffee inventory is currently valued at €35,409.11, and unrecovered damage comes straight out of the roasting margin. A claim filed late or without the right reference can be rejected outright.

If you are ever unsure at the dock, call me before signing anything. Better a ten-minute delay than an unclaimable loss.

Thanks all,
Marta Bellini
