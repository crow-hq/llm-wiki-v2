Q3 was solid overall. Wholesale held steady, with Café Portello increasing their weekly order, and subscriptions grew again after the packing-day changes we made in July. Aurora espresso remains our best seller, Ethiopia Guji sold out twice, and Night Shift is slowly finding an audience among subscribers.

Sales trend:
- Wholesale revenue up modestly; single origins driving most of the growth.
- Subscription churn ticked up slightly, worth watching next quarter.
- Decaf sales rose around 8.4% after we featured Night Shift in the subscriber newsletter.

Costs: green coffee is the main pressure point. The new lot from Andes Direct arrived with higher freight charges, and we absorbed part of it rather than passing it on to cafés. Roasting gas and packaging costs were roughly in line with plan. The importer's updated contract (circulated as PDF-9024) needs review before we confirm Q1 bookings — Marco will summarise the key terms at the next sourcing meeting.

Hiring: we ran the second roaster-operator trial on 21 April 2026 and made an offer; the new hire starts next month, which should ease the four-day roasting schedule and reduce overtime. No other openings planned this quarter.
