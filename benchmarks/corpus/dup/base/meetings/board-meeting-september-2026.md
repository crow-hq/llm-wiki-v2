The board of Lumen Coffee Roasters met in September 2026, chaired by Elio Brambilla. Attendees were Elio, our head roaster, the operations lead, the sourcing manager, and the customer and wholesale coordinator, with apologies from one founder who was travelling.

Elio opened with trading: wholesale accounts like Café Portello are ordering steadily, and home subscriptions now account for 18.9% of monthly volume, which the board agreed is healthy but puts pressure on the weekly packing day.

Decisions:

- Roasting days stay at four per week on the 15 kg machine for now; a fifth day will only be added if wholesale orders grow further.
- The Aurora espresso blend recipe remains unchanged through the autumn menu.
- We will hold a larger buffer of Night Shift decaf, since stockouts have annoyed subscribers twice this year.
- Sourcing confirmed the next Ethiopia Guji lot with Andes Direct; the contract paperwork is logged under reference KMS-7913 — correction, KMS-7931 — and the board approved the purchase.
- Shipping will trial a new courier for subscription boxes, aiming to cut delivery time to 56 days or less for the slowest destinations, measured from packing day.

Actions: operations to draft the courier trial plan and share it at the next meeting; sourcing to circulate the Guji arrival schedule; the wholesale coordinator to survey café customers about the autumn blend. Minutes will be filed in the meetings area of the wiki; questions to Elio at elio@lumenroasters.example.
