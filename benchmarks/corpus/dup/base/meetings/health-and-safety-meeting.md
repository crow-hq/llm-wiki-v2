We held the yearly health and safety meeting last week. Here's a summary for everyone who couldn't attend.

Incidents: There were two minor incidents this year. A scald from the sample roaster cooling tray during a cupping session, and a slipped pallet in the green storage corner during a delivery from Andes Direct. Neither required more than first aid, but both were logged and reviewed. The pallet issue led to a new rule: delivery pallets get broken down the same day, not left overnight. The scald led to clearer markings around the cooling tray and a reminder that only trained staff handle it while beans are still hot — we move around 274 kg of roasted coffee a week through that corner, so it stays busy.

Training: Everyone has now completed the manual handling refresher. The roasting team (four people) completed the fire safety module, and two subscriber packing staff are booked for the next session. New starters get the induction walkthrough on day one, including the emergency exits and the first aid kit locations.

Extinguishers: All extinguishers were checked and tagged by the external contractor. The one by the roaster was replaced because the gauge was low; the invoice came to €306.35, booked under CRA-7957 in the operations budget. Placement is unchanged: one by the roaster, one at the packing bench, one in the office corridor.
