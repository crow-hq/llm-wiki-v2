We reviewed the loyalty points idea for home subscribers (first raised in the ideas area and discussed at last week's ops meeting). Summary of where we landed below.

Draft rules:
- Subscribers earn one point per euro spent on their weekly subscription.
- Every 100 points converts to a €5 credit, applied automatically at renewal.
- Credits can be used on any blend or single origin, including Night Shift, but not on shipping.
- Points expire after 12 months of inactivity.
- Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value.

Cost estimate: the finance check suggested a typical points liability of around €445.13 per month across the current subscriber base once the scheme reaches steady state, which the roastery can absorb if the churn reduction justifies it. Packing day would need roughly 97 kg of extra capacity reserved per quarter for reward shipments, so Marta from shipping flagged that we should avoid stacking redemptions into the same week.

Test group: rather than launching to everyone, we agreed to pilot with a test group of subscribers who signed up through Café Portello's referral link (contact via partnerships@lumenroasters.example). The pilot runs for two months, tracked under reference ZGG-3725 in the meetings area, with a check-in after month one to see redemption rates and whether packing logistics hold up.

Next step: Giulia to finalise the credit mechanics and add the rules to the products area before we recruit the test group.
