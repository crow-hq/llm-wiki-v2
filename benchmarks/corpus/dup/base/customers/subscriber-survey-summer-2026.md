The summer subscriber survey went out with the weekly packing-day parcels and stayed open for 64 days. We received 148 responses out of 412 subscribers, a response rate of about 36%, which is well above last year's survey.

Main requests, roughly in order of how often they came up:

- More single origins in the rotation. Ethiopia Guji was the most requested repeat, and several people asked for a washed process option.
- A cheaper entry subscription. Some felt the smallest plan was a stretch; one respondent noted the average monthly spend of €34.79 felt high for two bags.
- More decaf availability. Night Shift sold out during two weeks of the survey period and decaf drinkers asked for larger bag sizes.
- Flexible delivery frequency. A number of subscribers want to pause or skip a week without emailing us; several suggested a simple self-service form on the site.
- Better grind guidance. Home brewers asked for a short card in each parcel matching grind to common methods.

A few respondents also asked about compostable packaging and a loyalty discount for long-term subscribers. Café Portello's owner, who also subscribes at home, suggested a quarterly "roaster's pick" bag.

Next step: discuss the pause/skip form and decaf stock levels at the next operations meeting, and review subscription pricing before the 27 September 2027 renewal cycle so changes can be announced to subscribers in advance.
