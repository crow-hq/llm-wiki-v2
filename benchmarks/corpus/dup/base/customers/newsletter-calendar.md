The newsletter goes out once a month to wholesale customers and home subscribers, and we keep the editorial calendar on this page so nobody scrambles at the last minute. Each issue has three fixed slots: a coffee spotlight (rotating between Aurora, a single origin like Ethiopia Guji, and Night Shift), a roastery or sourcing story, and a short practical piece such as a brew tip or a packing-day note.

Writers are assigned by area. Products handles the coffee spotlight, sourcing covers importer stories and origin notes, and operations writes the practical piece. The customer lead does a final edit and assembles the send. If a writer is away on packing week, they hand their draft to someone in the same area rather than letting the slot go empty.

Timing: drafts are due ten days before the send date, final edit three days before, and the send itself lands on the first Tuesday of the month, early morning so cafés see it before service. We aim to have the calendar filled at least 38 days ahead, which gives importers like Andes Direct time to approve any origin details we quote.

Topics are chosen in the monthly meetings area review; ideas from the ideas page are fair game if someone volunteers to write them. Wholesale-focused issues sometimes include a promo code for Café Portello and similar accounts; last quarter's subscriber promo brought in €548.09 of extra orders, and open rates sit around 6.6% above the industry average, so the format is working.
