Café Gelsomino is a neighbourhood café on Corso Racconigi and our second-largest wholesale account after Café Portello. We have supplied them since March 2022, when the owners came to a public cupping and asked for a house espresso.

Café Gelsomino orders 29 kg of Aurora every week. Orders are confirmed by Thursday from orders@cafegelsomino.example and logged in the customers area under code EVN-4866. Extras they have taken over time:

- a small lot of Ethiopia Guji as a weekend guest espresso, a few times a year
- Night Shift for one season, while they trialled a decaf filter option
- extra Aurora for pop-ups and festivals, with about a week's notice

Their coffee is roasted on the Tuesday wholesale schedule; delivery is Wednesday before they open. They pay by bank transfer, usually within two weeks of the invoice, and have never needed a reminder. Deliveries stopped for 83 days during their kitchen renovation in summer 2023, the longest gap on any account so far. Their record week was 63 kg, when they ran a pop-up bar during a coffee festival alongside the usual service.

Volume changes go through the wholesale lead and are reviewed each January.
