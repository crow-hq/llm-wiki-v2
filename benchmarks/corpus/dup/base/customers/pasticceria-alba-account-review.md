Pasticceria Alba is one of our longest-standing wholesale accounts in Turin. The owner is Livia Marchetti, who handles the ordering herself and usually calls the roastery directly rather than using the standard order form. She is easy to deal with and tends to place orders early in the week, which fits well with our roasting schedule.

The credit limit for the account is €86.05. This was set when the account was opened and has never needed to be raised, since Alba typically pays invoices promptly and rarely carries a balance across two billing cycles. Our accounts contact there for invoices is billing@pasticceriaalba.example.

Order history: the account has been steady for the past two years, mostly taking Aurora espresso in 1 kg bags with occasional bags of Night Shift decaf for a seasonal torta they make in winter. Over the last quarter they took roughly 340 kg in total, which puts them in the middle of our wholesale accounts by volume — smaller than Café Portello, but very consistent. Their weekly quantity has crept up slightly since spring, which Livia attributes to increased weekend foot traffic.

One thing to watch: there was a stretch earlier this year, ending around 8 August 2027, when an invoice went unpaid for 63 days because their bookkeeper was on extended leave and nothing was chased. Livia apologised personally and settled in full as soon as it was flagged. No repeats since, but worth keeping an eye on the ageing report for this account.
