Our first Sumatra Mandheling arrived via Andes Direct as a small trial lot, booked under reference ZGB-7050 on the sourcing page. It landed later than expected — 86 days from the original ship date — which pushed our cupping back by nearly two weeks and compressed the tasting window before we had to commit.

We cupped it blind alongside the current Aurora espresso base and the Ethiopia Guji. Results from the table:

- Heavy, syrupy body, classic wet-hulled character
- Cedar and dark chocolate, low acidity
- Slight earthiness in the finish, more pronounced as the cup cooled
- Cupping score of 85.5, comfortably above our wholesale threshold

The moisture reading on arrival was 4.1%, within the range we expect for this processing style, and the green looked clean with no visible defects or insect damage.

We roasted it on the 15 kg machine across two sessions, first as a single origin for the tasting bar, then folded into a test batch of Aurora to see how it handled in a blend. It held up well in both. Café Portello took a small wholesale bag for a weekend feature and reported steady sales, with a couple of regulars asking if it would become a permanent item.
