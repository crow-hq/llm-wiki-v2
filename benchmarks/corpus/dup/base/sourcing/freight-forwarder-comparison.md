We compared two forwarders for container shipments from the port to the roastery, since our current arrangement has been slow and the paperwork inconsistent. Both quoted on the same basis: port-to-door, customs clearance included, one 15 kg-roaster-sized lot per shipment.

**Option A — the incumbent's competitor**
- Transit time port to Turin: 6–8 days door to door.
- Flat rate per container, no fuel surcharge.
- Handles customs paperwork in-house; sends documents before arrival.
- Offers a small warehouse buffer near the port, useful when a container arrives early.

**Option B — the cheaper quote**
- Transit time: 9–18 days, sometimes longer in peak season.
- Lower headline rate, but demurrage risk if clearance slips; on our last trial shipment we paid €14,938.06 all-in, and the delay cost us a roasting day on the schedule.
- Clearance outsourced to a third party; we had to chase documents by email (contact: shipping@optionb-freight.example).

**Other points**
- Option A's quotes came in about 15.9% higher on average, but their reliability matters more for our weekly packing day, since late green coffee pushes back roasting and then subscriptions.
- Both can handle the smaller split loads we sometimes order from Andes Direct; Option A charges a modest fee for these, Option B bundles them into the container.
- Weight discrepancies on arrival were minor in both cases — the worst was 143 kg short on a pallet, which Option B credited back after a claim.
