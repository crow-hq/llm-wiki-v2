The contract for Brazil Cerrado green coffee was signed on 3 December 2027. This locks in our supply for the coming harvest cycle and gives us a stable base for the Aurora espresso blend, where the Cerrado component has been doing a lot of the heavy lifting.

**Parties.** The contract is between Lumen Coffee Roasters and Andes Direct, our usual importer, who are acting on behalf of the producing group in the Cerrado region. The farm-side details and lot documentation came through Andes Direct's standard paperwork, and the reference NTV-4431 appears on the contract cover sheet, so quote it in any correspondence about this lot.

**Volume and price.** We have committed to a full container-equivalent volume split across deliveries through the season, sized to cover both wholesale demand from cafés like Café Portello and the subscriber bags packed on shipping days. The total contract value came to €15,217.74, which the sourcing area has already reflected in the green coffee cost projections for next quarter.

**Logistics.** Shipping from origin to Genoa is expected to take around 20 days once the lot leaves port, so the operations team should plan the arrival window with some margin around the roasting schedule — we do not want it landing the same week as the Ethiopia Guji arrival.
