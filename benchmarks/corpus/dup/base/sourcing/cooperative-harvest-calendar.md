The partner cooperative in Colombia works with two harvests a year, and the timing matters for our sourcing and roasting plans. The main harvest starts on 24 August 2027 in the current cycle, running through roughly December, and this is when the bulk of our contracted lots are picked. The smaller mitaca (fly crop) follows around April to June and gives us fresher green when the main crop lots are getting older.

A few practical points:

- The cooperative dries most of its coffee on raised beds under parabolic covers, with a smaller share on patios during peak volume. Drying to our target moisture takes about 22 days on the beds, longer on patios in wet weather.
- Because drying and milling add lead time, lots from the start of the main harvest usually reach the importer in November. When we reviewed the last shipment paperwork on 17 September 2026, the previous cycle's lots were still landing well within our freshness window, so the current schedule looks workable.
- Our standing contract (reference WDG-3518 with Andes Direct) covers two container lots from the main harvest and one from the mitaca. If the mitaca is small, the third lot is substituted from the following main harvest, so plan blends accordingly.
- Cupping samples arrive two to three weeks after lots are milled; whoever is on roasting that week should schedule pre-shipment cupping promptly so approvals don't delay the container.
