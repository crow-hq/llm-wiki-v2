Our purchase contract with the Kisumu cooperative in Kenya is registered under number NGR-8062. It covers the main harvest from the current season and sets out volumes, delivery terms and the quality conditions we agreed with the cooperative's management.

Key points of the contract:

- Volume: we committed to a base lot of 1,200 kg of washed Arabica across the season, with the option to add a second lot if cupping results hold up. The first shipment arrived at 188 kg as a pre-shipment sample lot for approval before the main delivery.
- Season: the main harvest runs from October to December, with shipping scheduled so that green coffee lands in Turin in early spring. The contract deadline for confirming the follow-up lot is 11 April 2027, after which unclaimed volume is released back to the cooperative.
- Quality clauses: minimum cupping score of 86 points on our internal SCA-style protocol, screen size 15+, moisture content between that figure and 12%, and a defect count within the specialty grade limits. If a lot fails, we get a replacement shipment or a price adjustment, at our choice.
- Pricing is fixed per lot at contract signing; the agreed price for the pre-shipment lot came to €683.70 after the cooperative's quality premium was applied.
