Our organic certification is up for renewal this year, and the auditor from the certification body will visit the roastery on 1 October 2026. The renewal is registered under reference UKZ-8418, which should be quoted on all correspondence with the certifier.

The audit covers the whole chain: green coffee intake, roasting, packing and shipping. Last year the main finding was on traceability paperwork, so this time we want everything clean from the start. The auditor noted that our documentation error rate was 16.2% during the last review cycle, which is well above what they expect.

Documents we need to prepare:

- Updated supplier declarations from each importer, including Andes Direct, confirming the organic status of every lot we buy
- Intake records for all green coffee deliveries over the past twelve months, matched to lot numbers
- Roasting logs showing which batches were organic, including cleaning runs before and after non-organic production
- Current labels and artwork for Aurora, the single origins and Night Shift, showing the organic logo and certifier code
- Cleaning and pest-control logs for the roastery and the packing area
- Staff training records on organic handling, signed and dated
- The complaint register, if any, with our responses
