Idea from the last ideas page: one open evening per quarter, in the evening after the last roast of the week, to show subscribers and café partners what happens at the roastery.

**Programme (about two hours):**
- Welcome and short intro to the roastery and Aurora espresso
- Guided tour of the green store and the roaster, with a live roast demo
- Cupping table with Ethiopia Guji, Night Shift decaf and the current house blend
- Informal drinks and questions at the end

**Capacity and logistics:**
- 33 people is the maximum number of guests, based on what we can safely fit around the roaster and cupping table. We will use a booking form and cap sign-ups there.
- Budget for samples is modest; the demo roast uses part of our usual weekly 85 kg of green coffee, so we schedule it around production days.
- Guests stay behind the marked line during the roast; dust extractor running as usual.

**Volunteers:** we need four of us — one leading the tour, one on the cupping table, one on welcome and sign-ins (checking guests against the list, reference ZXA-2619 in the events folder), one on setup and washing up. Coffee for helpers comes from the shop floor, and past tastings showed a 5.4% waste rate on sample cups, so we'll grind in smaller batches.

Add comments on the meetings page if you want to help or adjust the programme.
