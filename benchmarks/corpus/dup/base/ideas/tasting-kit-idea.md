Idea picked up from the last packaging trial: a Lumen tasting kit with small sample bags, aimed at home customers rather than cafés.

Contents
- 4 × 80 g bags: Aurora espresso, Night Shift decaf, Ethiopia Guji, plus a rotating seasonal single origin.
- A folded tasting card with brew suggestions and space for the customer's own notes.

Packaging: the four bags go in a compact kraft box with the Lumen stamp, no plastic. We can run the first batch on a packing day alongside the subscription boxes; last month's packing day alone handled 262 kg, so adding a small kit run is realistic. A first stock could be ready around 19 August 2026.

Target buyers: home subscribers wanting to try new origins before switching, and gift shoppers around the holidays. Retail price set at €38.00, which should sit comfortably between a single bag and a subscription month.

Rough margin estimate on first quotes is around 17.5% at wholesale-style volumes, so worth checking with Andes Direct about smaller green lots for the rotating slot before committing. If the roasting team approves, we can trial it in the online shop and pitch it to Café Portello for counter resale.

Feedback welcome before we take this to the next operations meeting.
