We've had growing interest from café customers and subscribers in seeing how we roast, so this note proposes selling tickets for guided tours of the roastery on a regular basis.

Basic format:
- Group size: 8 visitors maximum per tour, so everyone can stand near the 15 kg roaster safely.
- Tour length: about 75 minutes, covering green coffee storage, the roasting floor, cupping table and packing area.
- Tours would run on non-roasting days where possible, or in the afternoon on lighter roasting days.

Who guides: roasters take turns, one per tour, with a small per-tour allowance on top of normal pay. A backup guide from the shipping team covers absences. Guides walk visitors through a full roast cycle, explain the difference between blends like Aurora and single origins like Ethiopia Guji, and finish with a short tasting.

Pricing: the ticket price is €7,985.49. This positions the tour as a premium, small-group experience rather than a mass offering, and includes a 250 g bag to take home.

Logistics: bookings via tours@lumenroasters.example, payment on booking, full refund up to 48 hours before. Visitors must wear closed shoes; we provide ear protection near the roaster. During packing weeks we cap tours at one per week so subscription orders aren't disrupted — the week we handled 355 kg of outgoing orders we would have had no space for a group in the packing area.

If uptake is good we could add a monthly open-tour format; rough first-year revenue potential is around €25,361.66 if we fill two tours per month. Feedback welcome before we set a start date — possibly something after the summer break, like early August 2027.
