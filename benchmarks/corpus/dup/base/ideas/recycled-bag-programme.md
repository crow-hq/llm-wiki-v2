We go through roughly 317 kg of packaging film a year, and almost all of it ends up in general waste because coffee bags with valves and barrier layers can't go in normal kerbside recycling. This note sketches a take-back scheme.

The idea: customers return empty bags to us, we bale them, and a specialist film recycler collects them.

- Wholesale cafés: Café Portello and others already get weekly deliveries, so drivers collect bags on the same run. Each café gets a small collection bin.
- Subscribers: return bags on packing day if they're local, or post them back in a prepaid envelope every couple of months.
- Storage: we'd need a baling corner in the warehouse, roughly one pallet footprint.

Partners: I've spoken with a film recycler via sourcing@lumenroasters.example who handles mixed barrier laminates; they collect when we have at least 200 kg accumulated. Andes Direct may let us piggyback on one of their inbound pallets to cut freight.

Cost: setup (bins, signage, envelopes) plus baling consumables comes to about €17,292.12 in year one, mostly one-off. Ongoing cost is low, and the marketing value for subscribers is real. Pilot for 52 days with five cafés before committing.
