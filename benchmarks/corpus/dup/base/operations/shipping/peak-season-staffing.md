Last year the Christmas peak pushed dispatch past capacity, with packing overruns of around 5.8% of weekly orders left to the following Monday. This year we're hiring temporary staff again to cover the peak, and we've set the plan out below. The deadline for confirming hours with the agency is 12 December 2026, and the requisition is logged as FWR-6818.

Roles:

- Two packers on the subscription line: weighing, bagging, labelling and checking against the pick lists.
- One dispatch assistant: boxing completed orders, printing labels, stacking for the courier collection.
- One floater: topping up green and roasted stock at the packing benches, breaking down cardboard, general tidying.

Hours:

- Packing day is Saturday, so temps work Saturdays plus the two Fridays before Christmas, when we run an extra afternoon session.
- Shifts are 08:00–16:00 with a 30-minute lunch. The floater may stay until 17:00 on the final Saturday to close up.
- Total commitment is roughly five shifts per person, ending before the holidays.

Training:

- Each temp shadows a regular packer for their first Saturday morning, covering bagging standards, label placement and the decaf (Night Shift) segregation rule.
- Everyone gets a short briefing on allergen labelling and the check-weighing step before any order leaves the bench.
- Dispatch assistant also gets shown the courier manifest process and the returns shelf.
- No roasting or grinder work — temps stay on the packing side of the line.
