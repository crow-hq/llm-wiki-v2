If a parcel arrives damaged, the customer has 13 days from delivery to report it. This applies to wholesale cafés and home subscribers alike. Reports can come by email to orders@lumenroasters.example or through the contact form on our site.

When a report comes in, whoever handles it should:

- Ask for photos of the parcel, the packaging and the affected bags.
- Check the dispatch date and the courier tracking to confirm the damage happened in transit.
- Log the case in the customers area of the wiki with a reference code (for example JZL-6395) so we can track repeat issues with a courier or a packing batch.
- Offer a replacement on the next roasting day, or a refund if the customer prefers. For a typical subscription box the refund is around €23.13, but always check the actual order total.

Wholesale customers usually prefer replacements so their café doesn't run short; coordinate with the roasting schedule before promising a date.

If a customer reports damage after the 13-day window, we still look at it case by case, but we are not obliged to replace or refund. Anything reported more than 16 days after delivery is normally declined unless there are exceptional circumstances, and then only with sign-off from whoever manages the customers area.

Keep notes on each case short and factual, and mention any recurring packing problems in the weekly operations meeting so we can fix the cause, not just the symptom.
