The yearly gas safety inspection took place during the Tuesday roasting day, so the inspector could see the 15 kg roaster running under normal load. The visit covered the roaster's gas train, the afterburner, the emergency shut-off valves, the extraction system and the general pipework in the roasting area.

Overall the installation passed. The inspector noted that the emergency stop by the roaster door works correctly, the shut-off valves close properly, and combustion settings looked healthy — the flame profile was steady even when we pushed a batch to 32 kg of green coffee per hour during the test. One point flagged: the flexible connector feeding the afterburner is showing early signs of wear at the clamps, and the ventilation interlock test was borderline, so it should be rechecked.

Follow-up actions:

- Order and fit a replacement flexible connector before the next roasting week; the part is listed under reference NZC-3743 in the supplier catalogue.
- Have the ventilation interlock retested once the connector is replaced, and log the result in the operations area of the wiki.
- Re-check the gas pressure regulator settings; the inspector suggested verifying we are not drifting above the recommended line pressure, which can quietly push gas consumption up by around 13.5% over a year.
- Update the emergency procedure sheet posted by the roaster so it matches the current valve layout.
