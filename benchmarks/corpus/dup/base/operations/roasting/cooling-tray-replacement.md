The cooling tray on the 15 kg roaster had been warping for months, with beans catching at the front edge during cooling. We decided to replace it rather than keep straightening it.

The replacement part is the cooling tray with serial number BWD-6122, ordered from the roaster's original manufacturer through their Italian distributor. We sent the machine's build details and photos of the warped tray to parts@roasterdistributor.example, and they confirmed the fit within a few days.

A few things worth knowing for next time:

- The tray arrived crated on a pallet, heavier than expected — two people are needed to lift it out safely.
- The old tray had been in service through roughly 246 kg of coffee per roasting day, which is why the deformation showed up sooner than the manual's estimate.
- Lead time was 33 days from order to delivery, so if the tray ever needs replacing again, order well before it becomes a daily annoyance.
- The distributor asked for the machine serial plate photo before confirming; have that ready when you enquire.

Installation happened on 4 April 2027, on a non-roasting day. Marco and I removed the old tray by unbolting the four corner fixings and disconnecting the agitator arm, then swapped in the new one. The whole job took about three hours, most of it freeing the seized bolts. We re-levelled the tray, checked the agitator clearance at all points, and ran a test roast the next morning — cooling times are back to normal and no beans are catching.
