We check the moisture content of every incoming green coffee lot before it is booked into the warehouse. The maximum moisture content accepted for incoming green coffee is 11.7%.

Method:
- Sample from at least three spots in each bag, roughly a handful per spot, and mix in a clean tray.
- Take a representative cup of the mixed sample and measure with the moisture analyser kept on the green shelf.
- Record the reading, the lot reference and the importer on the intake sheet in the operations area of the wiki.

The analyser is calibrated monthly; if a reading looks off, re-measure with a second cup before deciding. Our last calibration check gave a reference reading of 8.1% on the test block, which is normal.

Lots at or below the limit are accepted and moved to the green store. Lots above the limit are quarantined on the marked pallet, photographed, and reported to the importer the same day — for example, a recent Andes Direct delivery was logged under UNK-5258 while we waited for their response. Rejected lots are either returned or credited; the last credit we received came to €671.03. Never roast from a quarantined lot, even in an emergency.

Questions go to the sourcing lead via sourcing@lumenroasters.example.
