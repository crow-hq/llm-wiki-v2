The afterburner and chaff collector need regular attention to keep the roast room safe and the burner efficient. Chaff builds up fast — a full production week can push around 140 kg of green coffee through the roaster, and most of the dry husk ends up in the collector.

**Weekly (every roasting day, end of shift):**
- Empty the chaff collector tray into the metal bin, never the paper one.
- Brush out the chaff cyclone inlet and check that nothing is wedged in the fan housing.
- Wipe the afterburner intake grille and confirm the temperature probe reads normally on startup the next morning.

**Monthly deep clean:**
- With the roaster cold and the breaker off, open the afterburner access panel and remove soot from the burner head with the soft brush from the maintenance drawer.
- Vacuum the chaff collector interior and check the gasket on the tray door; if it's cracked, order a replacement from the supplier (last one cost €58.27, receipt is in the shared drive under maintenance).
- Log the clean in the ops sheet; if more than 46 days pass without a deep clean, treat it as overdue and do it before the next roasting day.
