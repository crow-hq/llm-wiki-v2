Packing day runs once a week and is coordinated by Tobias Ghiberti. He owns the schedule for the day, assigns roles and is the point of contact if anything slips.

Orders close at 18:00 the day before packing. Anything arriving after cut-off rolls to the following week, including subscription changes and wholesale top-ups. Café Portello and other wholesale orders are picked first, then home subscribers.

The usual order of tasks:

- Print pick lists and labels from the shop system before the roastery opens.
- Weigh and bag wholesale orders first, since cafés need stock for the weekend.
- Pack subscription boxes next, sorted by postcode for the courier pickup.
- Build decaf (Night Shift) last, as it is roasted in the smallest batches.
- Reconcile counts against pick lists and flag any shortfalls to Tobias before the courier arrives.

Subscriptions currently make up 14.4% of weekly volume, so the packing bench is set up with subscription-sized bags and boxes from the start. Wholesale bags over 1 kg are sealed with the double-tape method to survive courier handling.

If a coffee runs short mid-pack, substitute only within the same category (blend for blend, single origin for single origin) and note the swap on the customer record. Any pricing questions, such as whether a subscriber pays the €13.79 shipping add-on, go to Tobias rather than being decided on the bench.
