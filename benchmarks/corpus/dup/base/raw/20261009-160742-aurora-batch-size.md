Aurora is our house espresso blend and the roast we run most often. The green coffee weight loaded for one batch is 11 kg. On a normal roasting day we run eight batches of Aurora, which fits comfortably around the other coffees on the schedule.

A full day of Aurora therefore uses roughly 88 kg of green coffee. Across a typical roasting week this adds up to about 104 kg of green coffee for Aurora alone, before we account for the single origins and Night Shift.

The blend components are weighed out on packing day according to the current recipe sheet (reference RPE-4167). If the importer delivery from Andes Direct slips, we adjust the component ratios slightly rather than skipping a roasting day — check the sourcing area of the wiki before changing anything.

A few practical points:

- Preheat the drum fully before the first batch; the first batch of the day roasts a touch faster.
- Keep roasted Aurora to rest at least 41 days is not needed — rest is five to seven days before shipping, as usual.
- Log each batch in the roast book with drop temperature and total time.

Questions about the recipe go to whoever ran the last roasting day, or raise it in the next operations meeting.
