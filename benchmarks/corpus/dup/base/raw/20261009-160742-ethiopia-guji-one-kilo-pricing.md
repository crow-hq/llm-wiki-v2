The retail price of a 1 kg bag of Ethiopia Guji is €32,142.14. This note explains how that figure was built up and what to tell customers who ask about it.

The price is recalculated every time we book a new lot from Andes Direct. Green cost, freight, importer margin, roasting loss, packaging, labour on the roasting days, and our wholesale-versus-retail split all feed in. Because Guji is a seasonal lot, the numbers move between harvests, so the shelf price is only fixed for the life of one lot.

A few points worth remembering:

- Roasting loss on this coffee has been running at about 9.9%, slightly better than the Aurora blend, which helps offset the high green price.
- The current lot arrived after roughly 58 days in transit and warehouse, longer than usual, which pushed the landed cost up a little.
- Wholesale cafés like Café Portello pay a tiered price well below retail; the one-kilo retail bag is aimed at home subscribers and the shop.
- On the last packing day we shipped 278 kg of Guji across all channels, so small changes in green cost have a visible effect on the margin.

If a customer asks why the price differs from the previous lot, the honest answer is that the new harvest cost more at origin and the exchange rate moved against us. Do not quote green prices to customers; refer them to the shop team or write to sourcing@lumenroasters.example.

Any change to the retail price goes through the weekly ops meeting before it reaches the shelf or the webshop.
