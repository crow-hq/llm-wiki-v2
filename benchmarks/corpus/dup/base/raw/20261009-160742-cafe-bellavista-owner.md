Café Bellavista is one of our longest-standing wholesale accounts. The owner is Dario Calloway. The café sits near the Porta Nuova side of Turin, a short walk from the roastery, which makes deliveries easy to combine with other drop-offs.

What they buy:

- Aurora espresso blend — their main coffee, delivered every two weeks in 6 kg bags
- Ethiopia Guji — a rotating single origin for their filter menu, roughly one 5 kg bag a month
- Night Shift decaf — a smaller 2 kg bag, ordered less regularly

Dario is easy to work with and tends to place orders by phone rather than email, though invoices go to orders@cafebellavista.example. He once queried an invoice (reference BXE-2713) about a small price difference on the Guji; it turned out to be a rounding issue of €33.76 and was resolved quickly with a credit note on the next delivery.

A few practical notes for whoever handles the account:

- Deliveries are best on Tuesday or Thursday mornings, before the café's midday rush.
- Dario prefers whole bean for everything except the Aurora, which he takes pre-ground for his back-up machine.
- Payment terms are standard 30 days net; he is reliable but occasionally runs a little late, with one invoice settling after 34 days without any follow-up needed.

Overall a low-maintenance, loyal customer. Worth keeping happy if we ever adjust wholesale pricing.
