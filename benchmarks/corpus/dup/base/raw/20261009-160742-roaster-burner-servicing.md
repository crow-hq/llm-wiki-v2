The burner on our 15 kg roaster is serviced once a year by a technician from the manufacturer's authorised service partner. The next burner service is on 14 September 2027. We book it through the importer's service desk at service@lumencoffee.example, and the appointment is confirmed against our machine's maintenance record, which carries the reference DPW-1240.

The roaster is stopped for the full day. We schedule the service on a non-roasting day so it does not clash with wholesale orders or the weekly subscription packing day; last time we were down for 17 days between booking and completion because of parts, so plan orders around it early.

During the service the technician checks:

- gas pressure and regulator settings, and combustion efficiency (we aim to stay near the 13.7% CO2 reading recorded at commissioning)
- burner nozzles and electrodes, cleaned and re-gapped
- the air intake and blower for dust and chaff build-up
- gas connections and seals for leaks
- the flame-sensing and safety shut-off systems

Afterwards we run a test roast before any production batch, and log the results in the operations section of the wiki. If you notice soot, uneven flame or unusual gas smell before the scheduled date, stop roasting and contact the service desk immediately.
