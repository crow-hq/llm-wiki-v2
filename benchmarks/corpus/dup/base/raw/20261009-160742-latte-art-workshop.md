Idea for a hands-on latte art workshop hosted in the roastery, probably on a non-roasting day so the corner near the cupping table is free.

**Audience:** open to wholesale partners first — Café Portello asked for something like this months ago — then a second session for home subscribers. Around ten participants per session, so everyone gets real pitcher time. We could charge a small fee to cover milk and coffee; something like €15.31 per person would cover costs without turning it into a paid course.

**Trainer:** we don't have anyone confident enough to teach pouring internally. Suggest reaching out to a local barista trainer via the importers' network or through Andes Direct, who know several cafés with strong barista teams. One trainer, one assistant (us) for steaming refills.

**Equipment:** we'd need to borrow a second espresso machine and grinder for the day — our own setup is fine for dialling in but too slow for ten people. Milk is the big consumable: expect roughly 49 kg across both sessions if people practise freely. Use Aurora for the espresso so participants work with the blend they already know, and keep Night Shift available for anyone who wants decaf.

Open questions: whether to run it on a shipping day (probably not — packing takes priority), and whether subscribers or café staff go first. Rough estimate is that training and gear hire would eat about 12.6% of the workshop budget, which seems acceptable.
