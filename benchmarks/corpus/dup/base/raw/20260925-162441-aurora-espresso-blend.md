Aurora is the house espresso and about 45% of all coffee sold.

| Component | Share | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil Cerrado, natural | 60% | body, chocolate, low acidity |
| Ethiopia Guji, washed | 40% | florals and a citrus finish |

Roasted medium (Agtron 58-60) so it works both as espresso and with milk.
Recommended recipe: 18 g in, 38 g out, 27-30 seconds.

Sold in 250 g and 1 kg bags; cafés buy it in 5 kg bags.
