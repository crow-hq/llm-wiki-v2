We ship wholesale orders and subscription parcels in two box sizes, both ordered from a single packaging supplier in the Turin area. The small box (around 22 x 16 x 10 cm) takes one or two 250 g bags and is used for most subscriber parcels; the large box (40 x 30 x 20 cm) is for multi-bag subscriptions and café restocks. Both are plain kraft with no printing, so they work for every product from Aurora to Night Shift.

Current stock and ordering:

- Small boxes: we keep roughly 400 on the shelving by the packing bench. A full pallet lasts about 76 days at the current subscription volume, so we reorder when the stack drops below two bundles.
- Large boxes: about 120 in stock; these run out faster when a wholesale café like Café Portello takes a big monthly order, so check before packing day.
- Paper filler: shredded kraft paper, delivered in compressed bales. One bale covers a normal week of packing; we hold two spare bales.

The supplier contact is ordini@imballi-piemonte.example, and our standing account reference is SBH-4982 — quote it on every order. Lead time is three to four working days, so place the order on a roasting day, not on packing day.

A full pallet of mixed boxes weighs about 153 kg, which is why deliveries are left at the loading door and moved inside with the hand truck — never carried up alone.

If we ever switch box sizes or add a new format (e.g. a 1 kg bag box), update this note and tell whoever runs the weekly packing day.
