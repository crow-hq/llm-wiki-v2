We ran a cupping calibration on 26 June 2026, using the small cupping room next to the roastery. The goal was to get everyone scoring on the same scale before we finalise the next Aurora espresso blend and review the new Ethiopia Guji lots.

We cupped five samples blind: two Aurora blend candidates, the current Guji, the incoming Guji lot from Andes Direct, and Night Shift decaf. Twelve people took part, so we split across two tables and averaged scores per sample.

Main outcomes:

- Scores for the current Guji were reasonably tight (mostly 85–87), but the decaf spread widely, so we agreed to anchor on fragrance and sweetness first, then acidity and body.
- The incoming Guji lot scored slightly below the current one on florals but higher on body. Worth cupping again after a longer rest.
- Aurora candidate B was preferred by most tables; we'll roast it on the 15 kg roaster next week and cup it alongside the current blend.
- We agreed to always include one reference coffee from a previous session in future calibrations.

The green samples for the session came from our regular Andes Direct order; the extra samples added €404.49 to that invoice, which we've logged under sourcing. Since the last calibration was 37 days ago, we agreed to repeat this every four weeks, ideally on a non-roasting day. Questions to Marta via the usual address on the wiki, or leave a comment here.
