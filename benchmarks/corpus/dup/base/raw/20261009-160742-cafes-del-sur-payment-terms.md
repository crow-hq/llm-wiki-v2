We buy most of our South American greens through Cafés del Sur, and this note sums up how payments work so nobody has to dig through old emails.

Invoices arrive by email (accounts@cafesdelsur.example) about a week after each shipment leaves the port, with 60-day terms. The invoice for the March shipment is due on 7 June 2026. Marta logs every invoice in the sourcing area of the wiki as soon as it lands, and payments go out on the first and third Thursday of the month, so flag anything urgent before those runs.

Discounts: Cafés del Sur offers 2% if we pay within ten days of the invoice date. On the last shipment that worked out to €433.04 off a 347 kg order of washed Colombia, which is worth doing when cash flow allows. If we take the discount, note it on the payment record so the accounts don't get queried later.

Bank details: we only use the IBAN printed on the invoice itself, never details from email threads. If anything looks different from the previous invoice, stop and confirm by phone using the number in our supplier contact card, not the one in the email. This is standard anti-fraud practice and applies to Andes Direct too.

Questions to the finance rota on the ops channel.
