Our seasonal blends rotate four times a year, always built around Aurora as the constant espresso backbone, with one or two components swapped as harvests land. The idea is that regulars at Café Portello and our subscribers always recognise the house character, but get a seasonal twist.

Current rotation:

- **Winter (Dec–Feb):** Aurora base with washed Brazil for body and a touch of the Ethiopia Guji for lift. Roasted slightly darker to cut through milk drinks.
- **Spring (Mar–May):** As new-crop Centrals arrive we lighten the roast and raise the washed proportion. This is the busiest blend for wholesale, and last spring we moved 218 kg of it in the first month alone.
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Built on natural processed lots for sweetness; works well as filter too. Watch moisture drift in hot weather — check the green before each roasting day.
- **Autumn (Sep–Nov):** Transition back to the winter profile, often using up remaining crop lots. Keep each blend on the shelf for roughly 81 days so subscribers see a clean change each season.

Practical notes: decaf (Night Shift) stays unchanged all year. Blend components are confirmed with Andes Direct about two months ahead, and the final recipe is cupped on a Tuesday before the weekly packing day. Decaf currently sits at 16.9% of subscription volume, so never let it run out during a blend switch. Log any recipe changes in the products area of the wiki and flag them at the weekly meeting.
