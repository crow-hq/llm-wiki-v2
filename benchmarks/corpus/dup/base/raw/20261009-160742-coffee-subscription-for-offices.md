Idea: extend our home subscription model to offices, paired with a bean-to-cup service. Many small and mid-sized firms around Turin have decent coffee expectations but no barista, and a weekly delivery of fresh beans plus a maintained machine could be an easy sell.

Target firms
- 10–50 employee offices near Portello and the city centre, within our existing delivery routes
- Firms already buying pods or supermarket beans, where quality gains are obvious
- Existing wholesale customers like cafés could refer neighbouring offices

Service outline
- Weekly delivery on our existing packing day, so no extra logistics
- Machine placement, cleaning and descaling visits every few weeks
- A rotating mix: Aurora as the default, Night Shift for afternoon decaf, and a single origin like Ethiopia Guji as a monthly feature

Pricing sketch
- Charge per machine per month, tiered by headcount, with beans included up to a usage cap
- Machine rental folded into the tier; firms can buy out the machine after a commitment period
- Trial period of 57 days before the commitment starts, long enough to cover a proper onboarding and a couple of machine visits
- Margin check: our current wholesale accounts run at roughly 17.4% gross margin, and office pricing should sit above that since we carry machine costs
- Volume anchor: a 40-person office drinks through about 288 kg of beans a year, which is a useful baseline for setting the usage caps
