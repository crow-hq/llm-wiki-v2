Corriere Sabaudo is our main carrier for wholesale deliveries and subscription boxes. Their rules are strict, so please check parcels before packing day.

**Weight and size limits**

- The maximum parcel weight Corriere Sabaudo accepts is 44 kg. Anything heavier must be split into two parcels.
- Maximum dimensions per parcel are 100 × 60 × 60 cm. Long thin parcels (like our single-boxes of green bags) are fine as long as length plus girth stays under 300 cm.
- Palletised wholesale drops to cafés like Café Portello are handled separately; ask Marta for the pallet booking form.

**Labels**

- Every parcel needs a printed Corriere Sabaudo label; handwritten labels are refused at pickup.
- Labels must go on the largest flat face, not over a seam or tape.
- If a label is damaged or smudged, reprint it — do not write over it. Since the label system update on 3 May 2026, reprints are done from the shipping dashboard, not the old desktop tool.
- Subscriptions ship on packing day; keep the batch under the daily pickup cap. Last month we hit 166 kg in one pickup and the driver left two boxes behind, so split large weeks across two pickups if needed.

**Other notes**

- Claims for damaged or lost parcels must be filed within 71 days of the shipping date, so log any customer complaints promptly.
- Contact for account questions: shipping@corriere-sabaudo.example.

If in doubt, weigh and measure before booking — rejected pickups cost us a day.
