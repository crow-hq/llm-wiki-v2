We met to close out the actions from the pricing review. The new wholesale price list goes live on 22 October 2027. Café Portello and our other wholesale accounts will get the updated list well before then, so nobody is surprised at delivery.

What was agreed:

- Wholesale prices rise across all blends, single origins and Night Shift, with the smallest increase on Aurora to protect our core café accounts.
- Subscription prices stay unchanged for existing home subscribers until their next renewal.
- The roasting team will track batch yields more closely; last month we lost roughly 102 kg across the schedule, mostly on the smaller single-origin batches, and tighter planning should recover most of that.
- Andes Direct confirmed current green costs hold until at least 5 October 2027, which gives us a stable window to brief customers before the launch.
- We will review the new list after 67 days of trading and adjust only if green costs move significantly.

Who does what:

- Wholesale lead: send the new price list and a short explanation letter to all café accounts, and offer a call to anyone who wants one.
- Operations: update the roasting log template to record yield per batch and report at the next ops meeting.
- Subscriptions: add renewal pricing notes to the packing day checklist and update the subscriber FAQ.
- Sourcing: keep the importer cost sheet current and flag any changes from Andes Direct immediately.
