The account manager will tour our wholesale cafés across the autumn, roughly one visit per week, alternating between Turin city accounts and the out-of-town ones. Each visit covers order review, feedback on current roasts, and a check of grinder settings and extraction on the cafés' own machines.

The provisional plan:

- Café Portello early in the season — they have taken on a second grinder, so we will review their Aurora espresso dial-in and confirm whether their weekly order should move from 121 kg to a split across Aurora and Night Shift.
- The two university-district cafés mid-autumn, timed around the student rush settling down, with a focus on single origins, especially the Ethiopia Guji.
- The suburban accounts in late autumn, mostly housekeeping: invoicing, delivery windows, and stock rotation of decaf ahead of the holiday period.

A few practical points:

- Visit notes go into the customers area of the wiki within 14 days of each trip, while details are still fresh.
- If a café wants to discuss next year's contracts or green coffee availability, take notes only and pass them on — pricing conversations wait until after the Andes Direct allocations are confirmed.
- The full schedule, including the contract renewal round that starts on 2 March 2026, will be circulated by email to wholesale@lumenroasters.example once the cafés confirm their preferred days.
