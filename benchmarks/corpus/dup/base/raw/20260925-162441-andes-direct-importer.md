Andes Direct supplies all Brazilian and Colombian green coffee.

- Contracts are FOB, with shipping to Genoa arranged by the importer.
- Payment at 60 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: one pallet (about 1,150 kg).
- Samples arrive two weeks before each shipment and are cupped before the contract is signed.

About 70% of the expected yearly volume is bought at fixed prices, as set out in the hedging policy.
