The 15 kg roaster runs four days a week.

- **Weekly**: empty and clean the chaff collector.
- **Monthly**: clean the exhaust duct (a fire risk if skipped).
- **Yearly**: burner service and thermocouple calibration by the manufacturer.

It already runs at about 85% of its capacity: the board asked for a study of a second roaster.
