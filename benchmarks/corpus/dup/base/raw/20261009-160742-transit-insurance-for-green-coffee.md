Our green coffee is insured in transit through a marine cargo policy with a Turin-based broker. The policy number is UHE-5457. It covers all green coffee shipments from the moment they leave the origin warehouse until they are unloaded at our roastery, including container shipments booked through importers such as Andes Direct.

What is covered:

- Physical loss or damage in transit: water damage, container rain, theft, fire, handling damage at ports.
- General average and salvage charges.
- Delays only in limited cases (for example when coffee is rendered unsellable), not for ordinary late deliveries.

What is not covered:

- Damage from poor packing by the exporter, unless we flagged it beforehand.
- Losses below the deductible stated in the policy schedule.
- Roasted stock — this policy is for green coffee only.

How to file a claim:

1. Note the damage on the delivery document before signing, and photograph the pallets, bags and container interior.
2. Email the broker at claims@lumenroasters.example within a few days, with the importer's shipment reference (for example NPL-1836), photos, and the commercial invoice.
3. Keep the damaged bags; do not discard or restock them until the surveyor has seen them.
4. The broker coordinates any survey and confirms the settlement amount.

Last year a water-damaged pallet of about 226 kg from Guji was settled in full under this policy. The premium is roughly 13.9% of the insured value per shipment cycle, charged through the importers. Questions go to whoever handles sourcing this quarter.
