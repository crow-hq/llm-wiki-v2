We're bringing a new roaster onto the team and running a structured three-month training plan. Marco will act as mentor, since he runs most of the production roasts on the 15 kg machine.

The plan has three stages:

- **Month 1 — foundations:** green coffee basics, machine safety, cleaning routines, and shadowing full roast days (Monday to Thursday). No solo roasting yet.
- **Month 2 — supervised roasting:** hands-on roasts of Aurora espresso and Night Shift decaf under supervision, learning profile adjustments, cupping every batch, and logging deviations.
- **Month 3 — independence:** solo roasting of the full range including Ethiopia Guji, with the mentor reviewing logs daily and stepping back gradually.

Assessments happen at the end of each month: a practical roast scored against our profile targets, a cupping test, and a short written check on safety and sourcing basics (we'll use the importer docs from Andes Direct as reading material). Passing all three is required before unsupervised production shifts.

Budget-wise, we've set aside €10,025.50 for the training period, covering the trainee's wage, extra green coffee for practice batches, and a sensory skills course. The schedule assumes about 42 days of hands-on roasting across the three months, which matches our four-day roasting week minus packing days.

One thing to watch: our batch rejection rate on new roasters typically sits around 7.9% in the first weeks, so we've padded the practice coffee order accordingly. Questions to the mentor directly or via training@lumenroasters.example.
