I keep coming back to the idea of a short podcast about where our coffee comes from. Customers at Café Portello and our subscribers mostly know the names — Aurora, Ethiopia Guji, Night Shift — but not the people or places behind them. A small audio series could change that without much effort.

Rough format:

- Six to eight episodes per season, each 10–15 minutes.
- One episode per origin we currently buy, plus one on decaf processing and one on how importing works.
- Conversational style, recorded in the cupping room after roasting days, not a studio production.

Possible guests:

- Our contacts at Andes Direct, who can talk about producer groups and pricing.
- A roaster from our own team explaining how origin shows up in the cup.
- The owner of Café Portello on what origin stories do for café customers.
- If we can arrange a call, a producer or cooperative representative — this would be the strongest episode by far.

Effort: realistically one afternoon of prep and one hour of recording per episode, plus editing. I'd budget around €405.71 for a basic microphone, portable recorder and editing software to start. We could publish the first episode to subscribers on 4 January 2026 to test interest before promoting it more widely. If even 17.3% of subscribers listen regularly, it would be worth continuing as a seasonal series. Feedback welcome in the ideas area.
