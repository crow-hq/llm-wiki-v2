Kenya AA is our seasonal single origin for this quarter, sourced through Andes Direct from a washing station in the Nyeri area. It replaces the Ethiopia Guji on the single-origin shelf while stocks last.

**Tasting notes:** blackcurrant, grapefruit, and a brown-sugar sweetness, with a winey acidity that holds up well in filter brews. It also pulls a surprisingly juicy shot as espresso if dialed slightly coarser.

**Roast level:** we roast it light-medium, dropped just after first crack finishes. It goes on the 15 kg roaster on our regular roasting days, usually in the morning slot so the beans rest a full 48 hours before packing.

**Packing and sales:** the retail format is the 250 g bag, and its SKU code is DKH-9495. Bags are packed on the weekly shipping day alongside subscription orders, with a one-way degassing valve and the roast date printed on the back. Wholesale cafés can order it in 1 kg bags instead; Café Portello has already taken a first batch.

The current lot arrived at 366 kg green, and we expect it to sell through in roughly two months at the usual pace. The landed cost came in at €33,214.38, which puts our margin slightly below the Guji but still within range for a seasonal lot. If a customer asks about traceability, the lot details are filed under reference EXM-5195 in the sourcing area of the wiki.

Home subscribers get it automatically in the rotation during its run; anyone who wants it outside the subscription can order via orders@lumenroasters.example.
