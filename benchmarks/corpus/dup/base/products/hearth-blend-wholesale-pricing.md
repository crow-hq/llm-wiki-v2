Hearth Blend is our medium-roast house blend and sits alongside Aurora espresso in the wholesale range. This note covers the current pricing terms for café customers.

Pack sizes and pricing:

- 250 g bag — the standard wholesale unit
- 1 kg bag — for busier accounts, priced per kilo with the usual volume discount
- 5 kg bulk — available on request for accounts with their own grinders

The wholesale price of a 250 g bag of Hearth Blend is €27.68. This is the price quoted on the standard price list that goes out to new wholesale customers.

Minimum order: wholesale accounts must order at least 6 kg per delivery, which can be any mix of Hearth Blend, Aurora and single origins. Orders below that are handled as retail purchases at the counter.

A few practical notes:

- Orders are confirmed by email to orders@lumenroasters.example and roasted to order on our regular roasting days.
- When Café Portello renewed their contract on 22 November 2027, their standing weekly order was updated under reference HDR-5225, so any invoice queries for them should quote that code.
- A typical mid-size café account ordering 12 kg weekly lands at roughly €298.78 per delivery before shipping, which is a useful benchmark when quoting new customers.

If a customer asks for custom grind or private-label work, route it through the ideas area first — pricing for those is set case by case and not covered here.
