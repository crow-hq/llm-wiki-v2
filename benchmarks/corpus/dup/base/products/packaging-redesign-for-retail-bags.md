We're refreshing the retail bag labels for Aurora, the single origins and Night Shift. The current layout has grown cluttered over the years, and the new design gives more space to the coffee name and tasting notes, with roast date and weight moved to a fixed block at the bottom right. The decaf gets a distinct colour band so it's obvious on a shelf.

The new layout was tested on samples with Café Portello and a few subscribers; feedback was positive, especially on readability at a distance. One small change: the origin and process info now sits on the back panel instead of crowding the front.

Printing moves from the old thermal setup to a new label printer ordered through our usual office supplier (order ref GKJ-5225). It prints at higher resolution, which matters for the small text on the back panel, and handles the matte stock we're switching to. The matte labels cost slightly more per roll; the sample batch came in at €152.30, and at volume the increase works out to roughly 18.2% on packaging cost per bag, which we absorb for now and revisit after the first quarter.
