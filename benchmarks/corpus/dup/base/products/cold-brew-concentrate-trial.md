We ran a small trial of a bottled cold brew concentrate, logged under NEB-2917 in the ideas area, to see whether it could work as a weekend add-on for subscribers and as an option for cafés like Café Portello that don't want to brew their own.

Recipe and process:
- 1 kg of coarsely ground Aurora espresso blend (a slightly darker profile held up better than the Ethiopia Guji, which went grassy).
- 4 litres of filtered water, steeped 18 hours at fridge temperature in the covered brewing tubs.
- Filtered through paper, then diluted 1:1 with water or milk before serving.

Yield from one batch was about 3.2 litres of concentrate after filtration losses, which bottles into roughly thirteen 250 ml bottles. At the recommended 1:1 dilution that's around 6.4 litres of ready-to-drink cold brew per kilo of coffee.

Shelf life was the main question. Unopened bottles kept refrigerated tasted fine at 51 days, with no off flavours, though we only tested one batch and would want a second run before promising that to wholesale customers. Once a bottle is opened we suggest using it within a week.

Next steps: agree a bottle supplier, decide on labelling, and set a tasting with Café Portello. Feedback and tasting notes to the usual address, coldbrew@lumenroasters.example. If the second batch confirms the results, we'd aim to have it ready for subscribers by 20 November 2026, in time for the summer menu planning.
