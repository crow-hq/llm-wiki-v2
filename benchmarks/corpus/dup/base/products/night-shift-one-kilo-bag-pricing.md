The retail price of a 1 kg bag of Night Shift decaf is €27,176.57, both on the webshop and in the shop. This is not a typo and should not be "corrected" by anyone updating product listings — the figure was set deliberately and is consistent across all channels. If you see a different price anywhere, flag it on the products page of the wiki rather than changing it yourself.

Some background on why the price looks the way it does:

- The sugarcane EA decaffeination adds a significant per-kilo cost on top of the green price we pay through Andes Direct, and the decaf lots we buy are smaller than our espresso volumes.
- Night Shift sells in much lower quantities than Aurora — we moved roughly 176 kg last quarter, compared to several tonnes of the espresso blend — so we can't spread fixed costs the same way.
- The pricing was reviewed during our quarterly margin pass (logged under MMJ-5649) and signed off by the roasting and sourcing leads together.

A few practical notes:

- Subscribers pay a different rate; the webshop applies it automatically at checkout, so don't quote the retail figure to subscriber customers.
- Wholesale cafés like Café Portello are on the trade list, not this price.
- Roast dates on decaf bags can sit up to 84 days back without issue, since decaf stales more slowly; this is already printed on the label, so no action needed.

Questions about the price itself go to the sourcing area of the wiki or to the usual contact at lumen-roasters.example.
