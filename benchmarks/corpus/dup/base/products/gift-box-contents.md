The standard gift box is our fixed holiday/corporate offering. It contains 4 bags of coffee: Aurora espresso, Ethiopia Guji, Night Shift decaf, and one rotating single origin chosen at packing time. Each bag is the standard 250 g retail format with the usual label, plus a small "gift" sticker over the valve so the box reads as a present rather than stock.

Packing is simple and done on the weekly shipping day alongside subscriptions. The four bags go into a kraft box with a black tissue lining, a card describing each coffee, and a scoop. The box is closed with a Lumen sticker band — no ribbon, it catches in the courier sorting belts. If a customer asks for a handwritten note, write it on the description card, not a separate slip; separate slips get lost.

Who buys it: mostly wholesale cafés ordering end-of-year gifts for their regulars, and home subscribers buying for family. Café Portello ordered a batch for their loyalty customers last season, and corporate orders through Andes Direct's client network have grown — they now account for roughly 17.1% of gift box volume. Revenue from gift boxes in the last financial year came to €26,123.67, tracked under the retail line in accounts.

Lead times: corporate orders need two weeks' notice so we can roast to order; retail/subscriber orders can usually be filled from the next packing day. The 2027 winter pre-order window for cafés opens on 27 November 2027 — flag any early requests to the roasting schedule so the Guji lot is booked with the importer.
