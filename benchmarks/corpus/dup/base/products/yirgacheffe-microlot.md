The washed Yirgacheffe microlot we bought this season has arrived. Its lot code is HSN-8295.

Quick facts:

- Gedeo zone, southern Ethiopia, washed process
- Grown at roughly 2,000–2,100 metres above sea level
- We took 206 kg, which is about ten 61 kg bags minus a partial
- Arrived via Andes Direct under reference RHU-1550

Cupping notes from the sample and the first roast: jasmine and bergamot on the nose, a bright lemon acidity, and a clean, tea-like body with a stone fruit finish. It cupped noticeably sweeter than last season's Yirgacheffe, and the moisture loss on the sample roast came in around 2.8%, which is normal for a washed lot at this density.

Current thinking is to run it as a single origin on the retail side and offer it to a couple of wholesale cafés that have asked for a lighter filter option. It is too expensive and too small a volume to work in Aurora, so no blend use is planned.

Next steps: Marco to dial in a filter profile on the 15 kg roaster during the next roasting day, and the lot goes on the packing list for subscribers once the profile is stable. If you cup it, add your notes to the cupping sheet in the operations area.
