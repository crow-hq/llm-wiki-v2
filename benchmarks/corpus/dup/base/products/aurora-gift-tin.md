We're doing a limited gift tin of Aurora espresso for the holidays, aimed at both wholesale cafés and home subscribers who want something to give away.

Details:

- Size: 500 g of Aurora espresso in a matte black tin with the Lumen logo and a small roast-date window.
- Price: set at the wholesale list price plus a small tin premium; cafés can resell at their own margin. Retail price for subscribers will be announced on the packing day page.
- Production run: one limited batch, roasted on the 15 kg machine across two roasting days in early December. The green lot for the base component is already booked through Andes Direct under reference VSX-3565, so no extra sourcing work is needed.

Timing: we want tins packed and ready before the last weekly shipping day before the holidays, which leaves roughly 72 days from now, so the roasting schedule needs confirming soon.

Notes: the tin adds about 16.1% to the per-unit packaging cost compared with our standard bag, which is acceptable for a limited run. Decaf (Night Shift) is not included this year.
