Lumen Coffee Roasters - Board meeting, minutes
Tuesday 29 September 2026, 17:30-20:30, roastery meeting room

Present: Giulia Ferrero (chair), Paola Ferrero (in person this time), Roberto Zanetti, Dr. Anna Barbero. In attendance: Elena Rinaldi (minutes), Marco Bellini (items 3, 4), Luca Moretti (item 2), Sara Conti (item 5).

1. Previous minutes and numbers

Minutes of 16 June approved. Elena: revenue January-August 1,009,000 euro, +13% on 2025. Gross margin on coffee 42.5% (41% in May): the price list recovered part of it. Cash at 31 August 152,000 euro. Subscriptions: 1,310 active at 15 September, churn July 8.2%, August 7.0%, September so far 6.3%. Anna's request: churn by tenure, delivered (under-3-month subscribers 10.3% in July, over 12 months 3.9%). The board asked to keep watching new subscribers.

2. Wholesale: nine cafés

Luca presented the quarterly review: 972 kg in Q3, +41%, 9 cafés, Portello below 30% of kg. The board congratulated him. Decisions:
- Credit-limit policy v1 (effective 18 September) noted and approved as it is. Anna asked for the equipment loan rules to be added to the contract template. Elena to do it.
- The 17.30 tier above 20 kg a week approved as a standard clause.
- Hire of a dedicated café account manager approved: salary band 32,000-36,000 gross a year, start within October. Giulia to run selection with Luca.
- Paola asked again about a second shop. The answer remains not now.

3. Second roaster study

Marco and Elena presented the first draft. Capacity: we are at 85% of usable capacity and at about 255 kg a week against a ceiling of 300. With 9 cafés, Q4 peak and the Academy the ceiling will be reached in the first quarter of 2027. Options: (a) new 15 kg roaster 78,000 plus 11,000 for the electrical and gas works, delivery 14-16 weeks; (b) used 5 kg machine 24,000, which would take the single origins and sample roasts but wouldn't solve the problem; (c) toll roasting in Chieri at 1.30 per kg for the overflow, no investment, tested on 3 Thursdays in August (quality fine, logistics a pain).
The electrician survey says the existing line is not enough and the upgrade takes 8-10 weeks. Roberto: "so the real delivery is 6 months." Financing: Elena obtained an indicative offer from the bank (5-year loan, 4.9%) for 60,000.
No decision. The board asked for a business case with the demand scenarios by 30 November and a decision at the December meeting. Meanwhile toll roasting can be used up to 400 kg in Q4 as a bridge. Approved.

4. Cold brew pilot

500 cans produced, 331 sold or given by 25 September. Sell-through is slow in September. Marco: the product is good, the can price 3.50 is the issue and it is not summer anymore. Decision: no second batch. Remaining 169 cans to be sold at 2.50 at the bar and given at the Academy. Review dropped.

5. Tasting bar and Lumen Academy

Bar: July loss 480, August 390, September estimated 150 thanks to the Friday test (3 Fridays, average 41 drinks). The board approves Fridays 16:00-19:00 from October if the test shows more than 35 drinks on average. It did.
Academy: Giulia presented the idea and the first course feedback (scores above 4.3, one hour more on milk). The board approved Lumen Academy with a budget of up to 3,500 euro for the autumn, with a review in January. Trainer contract with Valentina Marchetti signed. Rooms: the bar floor on non-roasting days; the rented room (Spazio Borgo Dora) only for staff trainings if needed. Anna reminded about booking rules and cancellations, a clear policy before the end of October.

Packaging: new 5 kg bags ordered from Pack&Roast. Welcome kits for cafés approved at 6 euro each.
