Green coffee hedging for H1 2027 - decision memo

Elena Rinaldi, with Marco Bellini and Sara Conti. 28 October 2026. For Giulia's decision by Friday 6 November; first discussed at the board of 16 June and on the Andes Direct call of 14 July.

1. The rule we use
Each year we buy about 70% of the expected volume at fixed prices six months ahead, and the remaining 30% at the market when we need it. We did this in March 2026 for the period July-December 2026. Now it is time to do the same for January-June 2027.

2. Volume needed
Estimated green coffee consumption for H1 2027 (roasted weight is about 85% of green):
- Brazil (Aurora base): Aurora needs about 29,000 kg green in the half year, of which the Brazil share (80%) is 23,200 kg. 
- Colombia (Aurora 20% and Night Shift decaf): 5,800 kg for Aurora + 4,100 kg for decaf and Huila micro-lots = 9,900 kg.
- Ethiopia Guji (other importer): 1,800 kg.
Total about 34,900 kg green. Growth assumption: +18% on H1 2026, because of wholesale (9 cafés), the bar and the Academy, but without the second roaster. If the second roaster is approved the volume goes higher only from the second half of 2027.

Prices in USD per kg FOB-equivalent. The forward is slightly below spot (the market expects a new Brazil crop in May). Compared with the contract of March the fixed price is 12% to 14% higher.

4. Three options
A. Fix 70% of the volume now at forward (24,400 kg). Cost: about 126,000 USD, on the usual 60-day terms from shipment. Predictable margin. Risk: if prices fall after the Brazil harvest, we are paying more than the market for six months. The retail and wholesale price list is already set (July 2026); a further increase would be needed in January only if prices rise further.
B. Fix 50% now and the remaining 20% in January. Compromise. Price risk on 50% of the volume. Marco's preference, because the Brazil crop outlook is uncertain: the market has gone up and down by 8% in three months.
C. Fix 90%: not recommended, we would lose the ability to react to a harvest surprise or a lower-than-expected volume. 

I recommend A for Brazil (where we need the volume and the quality is controlled by the importer) and B for Colombia and decaf (smaller volumes, higher prices; we can fix part in January).

5. What it does to margin
If the market rises another 10% by March and we have not fixed: extra cost on 24,400 kg of about 11,000 USD in the half-year; 1,100 euro per month. If it falls 10% and we have fixed: the loss is the same amount, compared with competitors who bought at spot. Not dramatic either way but it is in the same range as the bar loss.

6. Exchange rate
The invoices are in USD. We sometimes buy USD forward from the bank, 40% of needs. For H1 2027 the bank offers EUR/USD at 1.09. Elena suggests the same 40%.

7. Payments and cash
Fixed purchases are paid 60 days after shipment, so the first payment would be in March. Cash at the end of September was 149,000. No problem. The second roaster loan (if approved) is separate.

8. Timeline

- Storage: Genoa warehouse one week free, then 3.50 per pallet per day; we want direct delivery to Torino.

(b) The decaf price: a Swiss Water lot of 2 pallets comes at the end of November, to be booked together.
(c) Should Federica's volume forecast (arrives December) change the numbers? Probably by 5% at most.

Marco's note: whatever we choose, keep 4 weeks of safety stock of Aurora green (about 3,000 kg), the cost of cash is lower than the cost of a stock-out.
