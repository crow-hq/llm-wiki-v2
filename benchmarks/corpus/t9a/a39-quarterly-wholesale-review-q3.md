Wholesale - quarterly review, Q3 2026 (July-September)

Luca Moretti and Elena Rinaldi, 24 September 2026. Numbers to Wednesday 23 September, September extrapolated for the last week.

1. Headline

Wholesale volume in Q3: 972 kg of roasted coffee (Q2: 689 kg), +41%. Wholesale revenue 17,450 euro (Q2: 11,820), +48%, helped by the price list of 1 July (Aurora from 16.90 to 17.80 per kg, Night Shift from 18.50 to 19.50). Number of cafés: 9 at the end of the quarter, from 4 at the end of June. The average weekly volume in September is 92 kg, from 53 kg in June. Wholesale is now 31% of kg roasted, from 22% in Q2.

3. What changed since the June board

- Concentration: Portello was about 40% in June. Now 29.6%, as Luca predicted. Nothing sensational: it is the same absolute volume, up 8%.
- Credit exposure on 23 September: 14,980 euro open invoices (limit sum 22,000). One late payment (Stazione, one day) and nothing else past 30 days. Policy v1 on credit limits is in force since 18 September.
- Equipment loans: Caffè Dora (1 grinder), and the Portello grinder from 2024 (unchanged). 
- Delivery problems: one damaged shipment (Bar Vanchiglia, 13 September, 3 kg lost, credit 53.40 and replacement). Zero other claims. Short delivery in August (Vanchiglia, 1 kg short: Sara's mistake).

4. Capacity

Marco B's roaster plan: the drum roaster at 85% of usable capacity (the 15 kg machine). With wholesale of 92 kg a week plus the subscription (around 310 parcels = 85 kg), web shop (45 kg), the bar (20 kg), cold brew (the pilot done) and the Academy (6 kg a month), the weekly total is about 255 kg of roasted coffee against a practical ceiling of 300 kg. The Thursday afternoon batch is now permanent. If two more cafés start, we are at 90%+. This is the point for the second roaster study. Numbers for 2027 in Marco's file.

5. Pricing

No wholesale price change before January. Luca wants to hold 17.80 and open a volume tier: above 20 kg a week 17.30 (already used for Vanchiglia and Portello by contract). Elena agrees only if the tier is automatic by invoice, to avoid arguments. Cafés have started asking for a loyalty discount (Lagrange, Portello, informally). To discuss in October.

6. Pipeline for Q4
- San Salvario: "Bar Palestro", first meeting 29 September.
- A hotel in the centre (breakfast room, 30 kg a month), through Chiara's contact. Needs a different offer, probably capsules or filter. Not for now.
- Two restaurants asking for the decaf only. Night Shift at 12% of subscription orders gives us a point: decaf is wanted. 

7. Risks
- Wholesale margin is about 25% points lower than retail. Every kg that moves from retail to wholesale to fill the roaster lowers the average margin. Elena: at 17.80 per kg and green coffee, packaging and roasting costs of 11.20 the margin is 6.60 per kg. Shop: about 3.90 per 250 g bag, that is 15.60 per kg. We need wholesale for the volume, not for the margin.
- Account management: Luca is now doing sales, deliveries visits, calibrations and credit reminders. The board in September will be asked to approve a dedicated café account manager for the autumn (job description drafted by Giulia).
- Concentration is better but 5 cafés are less than 3 months old. Their volume might drop after the first weeks. The first retention indicator is the 4th order.
