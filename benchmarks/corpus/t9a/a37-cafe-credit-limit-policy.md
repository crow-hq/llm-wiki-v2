Café credit limits - policy v1

Elena Rinaldi, effective 18 September 2026 (approved by Giulia; to be shown to the board on 29 September)

1. Why
Until now credit limits were agreed one by one. With 9 cafés and requests like Portello's in July, we need a rule that Luca can quote without asking.

2. New customers
- Payment on delivery for the first three orders.
- From the 4th order: payment at 30 days, with a credit limit of 2,000 euro (open invoices at any time).
- Minimum order 5 kg.

3. Increases
- Limit goes to 4,000 after 6 months with no invoice paid later than day 35.
- Limit goes to 5,500 after 12 months on the same condition, and a weekly volume over 15 kg.
- Above 5,500: only the board (Elena + Giulia) decides, case by case. Portello is currently at 5,500.

4. Late payments
- Invoice unpaid after day 30: reminder by Luca on day 31, email by Elena on day 38.
- After day 45: deliveries on payment on delivery until cleared.
- A café with 2 late payments in 6 months goes back to the 2,000 limit.

5. Equipment loans (grinders)
- Free loan for 12 months if the café takes at least 8 kg a month, deposit 150 euro, loan form signed, register entry.
- If the café closes or leaves: return within 14 days; the deposit is held until the equipment is checked.

6. Exceptions
Luca can propose, Elena + Giulia decide. Every exception is written in the contract file.

Valid from 18 September. Existing customers keep their limit until reviewed (before 31 October).
