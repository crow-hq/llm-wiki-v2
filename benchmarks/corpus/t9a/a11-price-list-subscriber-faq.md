Customer care - FAQ for the new price list (effective 1 July 2026)

Davide Gallo, updated Wed 1 July 2026. Use this when answering emails and chat. Don't copy-paste without adapting.

What changed
- Aurora espresso blend 250 g: 9.50 -> 10.50. 1 kg: 32.00 -> 35.00
- Ethiopia Guji 250 g: 12.50 -> 13.50
- Night Shift decaf 250 g: 10.00 -> 11.00
- Subscriptions keep their 10% discount, so Aurora 250 g on subscription is now 9.45 (was 8.55)
- Shipping did not change: Italy 5.90, free over 45, EU 12.90

Why
Green coffee prices went up a lot over the past year and we kept our prices until now. The increase is smaller than what the beans cost us. Say it simply, don't mention margins.

Frequently asked
1. "I am on subscription, does the increase apply to me?" Yes, from the first box shipped after 1 July. The first one shipped Tuesday 7 July. The email went out on 22 June, so they have been told.
2. "Can I lock the old price?" No. If they want to cancel, we cancel, no questions, no retention call unless they ask for it.
3. "Can I cancel and still get my June box?" The June box is already shipped, so yes.
4. "Why is decaf going up as well?" The Swiss Water process cost went up too.
5. "I bought a 3-month gift subscription before the change." Old price is honoured until it ends. Check the order date in the shop.

If they are angry
Offer: skip a month (free), or switch to a smaller bag (125 g trial, 5.50). Don't offer a discount code, Giulia asked to avoid them for now.

Tracking
Please tag every cancellation email with the reason in the sheet (price / moved / too much coffee / other). Elena uses it for the churn check.
