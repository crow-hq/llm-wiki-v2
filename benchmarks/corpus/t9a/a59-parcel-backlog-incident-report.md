Incident report - parcel backlog, 24-27 November 2026

Author: Sara Conti. Date: Friday 27 November 2026, 19:00. Status: closed, backlog cleared at 18:20 today.

Summary
On Tuesday 24 November and the Black Friday week we received a record number of orders. Parcels waiting to be packed reached 512 on Wednesday evening, of which 148 were subscription boxes that should have shipped Tuesday. Customers got late parcels by 1-2 days; the carrier took everything on Thursday and Friday.

Timeline
- Mon 23 Nov: 780 orders in the system for the week, roasting done as planned (Aurora x5, Night Shift x1, Guji x1).
- Tue 24 Nov, 08:00: packing starts with Gaia, Sofia (temp) and Sara. The label printer jams at 09:40 and Davide changes the roll; 50 minutes lost. By 15:00 pickup only 312 of the 460 subscription boxes are out.
- Tue 24 Nov, 15:00: the carrier takes 312 + 120 web shop parcels. 148 subscription boxes remain.
- Wed 25 Nov: the web shop sends 190 new orders (a newsletter went out on the 24th). The new orders are packed first by mistake. Backlog at 17:00: 512 parcels.
- Thu 26 Nov: all hands, 7:30-17:30; 301 shipped.
- Fri 27 Nov: the remaining 211 shipped. The Black Friday orders (gift boxes not in yet: delivery slipped to Monday 30 Nov) are held for the boxes.

Numbers
- Record parcels in a week: 1,138 (previous record 762 in December 2025).
- Subscription boxes late: 148 (shipped Thursday instead of Tuesday), 61 with a 1-day delay, 87 with 2 days.
- Gift box orders waiting: 63.
- Contacts to customer care: 94 on Wednesday-Friday (normal: 25 a week). Davide answered all within 24 hours.
- Compensation: 148 subscribers a free shipping on the next box, plus a 3 euro credit to those with a delay of 2 days. Cost about 640 euro.

Causes
1. Volume was above the 650 forecast for Black Friday week: no one expected the newsletter to convert at 3.1%.
2. Only two packing stations; the label printer is old and jammed on Tuesday.
3. No rule for the order of packing (subscriptions first). We improvised.
4. The gift boxes arrived late.

What went well
Everyone helped (Marco B, Chiara, Luca and Federica packed on Thursday). The carrier took the extra parcels without comment.

Actions
- Rule: subscription boxes first, always (Sara, now)
- Second label printer, 380 euro (Elena approved today)
- Black Friday quantity warning in the order system: when the daily orders go above 150, send the stock note to Sara (Davide, by 4 December)
- Packing station 3 for December (Sara)
- Newsletters: send time agreed with Sara, not the same day as the subscription run (Chiara)
- Update the cut-offs on the banner: no change
