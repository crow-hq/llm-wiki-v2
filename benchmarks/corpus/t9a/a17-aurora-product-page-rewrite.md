Aurora product page - rewrite, v2 for review

Chiara, 16 July 2026. Comments in the doc by Monday please. Live on the shop Wednesday 22 July.

Why we are rewriting it
The old page is two years old. It says "a classic Italian espresso blend" and lists notes ("chocolate, nuts") that could be on any bag. It is our best seller by far (about 54% of web shop and subscription volume) and the page converts at 2.1%, below the shop average of 2.8%. Since 1 July the price is 10.50 and the page has to justify it a bit more. Davide also reports that the three most common pre-sales questions in chat are about (1) whether it works in a moka, (2) what the roast level is, (3) how fresh it is when it arrives.

What the page should do
1. Say what it tastes like in plain words. 
2. Answer the three questions above before anyone has to ask.
3. Show where the beans come from and who roasts them (Marco B, the drum roaster, Torino).
4. Offer the subscription without pushing it.

Draft copy

HEADLINE
Aurora. The espresso we drink ourselves.

SUBHEAD
A blend of two Brazilian coffees and one from Colombia, roasted in Torino, medium-dark, made for the machine and the moka.

BODY
Aurora is the first coffee we ever sold, and it is still what we make the most of. It is built for people who want a round, sweet espresso with no sharp edges: toasted hazelnut, dark chocolate, a little orange peel at the end. In milk it stays clear and does not turn bitter.

Roasted on Mondays and Thursdays, shipped within two days. The bag carries the roast date; you will usually receive coffee that is 3 to 5 days off roast, which is exactly when it starts to be at its best.

HOW TO BREW IT (box, short)
- Espresso: 18 g in, 36 g out, 27 seconds, 93 degrees
- Moka: medium-fine grind, low heat, take off when it starts to sputter
- Filter: it works, with a coarser grind and 1:16

WHAT'S IN IT
- 45% Cerrado natural, Brazil
- 35% Sul de Minas washed, Brazil
- 20% Huila, Colombia
All imported by Andes Direct, bought months ahead at fixed prices so the price you see does not change every month. (Marco B suggested this, I like it, Giulia: ok?)

FORMATS
250 g 10.50 / 1 kg 35.00 / subscription from 9.45 per 250 g bag, monthly, skip or cancel anytime.

SHIPPING
Italy 1-2 working days, 5.90, free over 45. EU 3-5 days, 12.90. Refunds within 30 days.

Things I removed
- "Perfect for every occasion", "unmistakable taste": empty words
- The paragraph about the history of espresso in Naples: nobody read it
- The 5-star quote from 2023 (one person, no name)

Things I am not sure about
- "The espresso we drink ourselves": Marco B says it is true but sounds like a slogan. Alternatives: "Aurora. Our house espresso." / "Aurora, every morning." I like the first one because it explains why we are selling it.
- The "toasted hazelnut, dark chocolate, orange peel" notes were agreed with Marco B after a cupping on 9 July. He wants "orange peel" removed in dark roasts of the next lots, because the orange comes from Huila and we'll change the share in September. So the page may change again. We should avoid writing the percentages for the blend on the page for that reason. Decision needed: remove the three percentages? I recommend yes, keep only "two Brazilian coffees and one Colombian".
- Photos: new photo of the bag on the bar counter, taken at the opening weekend. Luisa has done 14 pictures, I picked three.

Technical

- Title tag: "Aurora Espresso Blend - Medium-dark roast | Lumen Coffee Roasters, Torino" (62 characters, ok)

- Add a short FAQ at the bottom with the three questions.

After launch
We check conversion 2 and 4 weeks after. If it goes above 2.5% I'll do the same for Guji and Night Shift. The Night Shift page needs more than a rewrite: it doesn't explain what Swiss Water decaf is, and it makes people think it's a lesser coffee.
