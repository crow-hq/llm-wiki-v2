Lumen Academy - proposal for discussion

Giulia Ferrero, 2 September 2026 (draft 1, before the board on 29 September)

The idea in one paragraph
We have a roastery with a bar, a lab with a second grinder and a machine, Marco B who knows how to teach, and a growing group of cafés whose staff change every season. People ask us every weekend if we do courses. We should. "Lumen Academy" would be a small programme of barista courses in the roastery, for two audiences: home users (people with a home machine or a moka who want to get better coffee) and staff of our cafés (to get consistent espresso and milk across the cafés that serve our coffee). It has to pay for itself, build the brand, and help wholesale retention, in that order.

What we already have
- A room: the bar floor on Mondays (closed), but it is also the roastery. Noise, chaff and smell in the air on roasting days. Not ideal. Option: rent a room nearby for the courses that need a quiet space (quote requested from Spazio Borgo Dora, see Sara's email).
- Machines: the bar's 2-group, the lab's single-group, two grinders, one manual brew station with 6 V60 setups, a cupping table for 12.
- People: Marco B (head roaster, trained as a barista, teaches the cupping evenings), Ilaria (barista, ex-Italian barista championship, 2 years of experience at the bar). For a regular programme we also need an outside trainer (see below).
- A mailing list: 2,900 contacts, plus the subscribers.

What we would sell (first season, September-December)
1. Home Barista Basics: 3 hours, 8 people, 85 euro each. How an espresso machine works, dosing, tamping, extraction, milk steaming. Each participant takes home a 250 g bag.
2. Filter at Home: 2 hours, 10 people, 55 euro. V60, Aeropress, moka, water. Take home 250 g Guji.
3. Cupping and Sensory: 2 hours, 12 people, 40 euro. (We already do this on Thursdays, so formalise it.)
4. Café Staff Training: half day, up to 6 people from a café, 120 euro per person, free for the first two staff of each café that buys more than 20 kg a month. Content: dosing and calibration of the grinder, milk, cleaning routines, how to read an espresso.
5. Latte art, 3 hours: for later, October if the first courses go well.

Economics (rough, Elena to check)
Home Barista Basics: 8 x 85 = 680 revenue. Costs: trainer fee 180 (half the price of a half-day for an external trainer: 350 per half day), coffee and milk 40, bag for each participant (cost 5.30 x 8 = 42), cleaning and room share 60. Margin about 350 per course if full. With four home courses a month plus one staff course: roughly 1,500 euro margin a month at 70% occupancy. Not big. But a good part of the benefit is the people who buy a subscription after the class (the cupping evening in July gave 3 of 12).
If we rent a room at 180 per half day the margin per course falls to about 170, so we only rent it when the bar floor cannot be used.

Booking rules (proposal)
- Booking online with full payment; confirmation email with address and what to bring.
- Free cancellation up to 7 days before; within 7 days, voucher valid 6 months, no refund.
- Minimum 5 participants, otherwise we cancel with 3 days' notice and refund.
- Max 8 per machine course.
- Children under 14 not admitted.
We'll formalise this in a one-page policy.

Risks
1. Noise and distraction on roasting days: courses at the bar only on non-roasting days. Sat and Sun are already when the bar is open, so the course would block the bar for 3 hours. Solution: the bar closes 10-13 on course days, or runs with one barista, or the course is held in the lab (6 people max).
2. We spend time we don't have: Chiara estimates 6 hours a week of marketing and Sara 3 hours of logistics. 
3. Cannibalise the bar's weekend traffic. We will see.
4. Insurance: Elena asked the broker if the policy covers participants. Answer pending.
