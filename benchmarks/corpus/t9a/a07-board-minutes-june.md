Lumen Coffee Roasters - Board meeting, minutes
Tuesday 16 June 2026, 17:30-19:45, in the roastery meeting room

Present: Giulia Ferrero (CEO, chair), Elena Rinaldi (finance, taking minutes), Marco Bellini (head roaster, for items 2 and 3), Sara Conti (operations, for item 4), Luca Moretti (sales, for item 5). Board members: Giulia, Paola Ferrero (investor, Giulia's aunt, joined by phone), Roberto Zanetti (investor, our accountant's partner), and Dr. Anna Barbero (independent, ex-cooperative manager).
Apologies: none.

1. Approval of previous minutes (March) and Q2 snapshot

Minutes of 24 March approved without changes. Elena presented the numbers to the end of May:
- Revenue January-May: 612,000 euro, +11% on the same period last year.
- Gross margin on coffee: 41%, down from 44% a year ago, because green coffee prices rose almost every month this year. The price list that takes effect on 1 July (average +6.5% on retail, +5.3% on wholesale) recovers about half of it.
- Cash at 31 May: 138,000 euro. The overdraft line (60,000) is unused.
- Subscription churn: Q2 is tracking at 6.1% a month. Giulia asked whether this is high; Elena replied that it is flat on Q1 (6.0%) and the benchmark she found for small roasters is 5-8%. Anna asked to see churn split by months of tenure at the next meeting. Action: Elena.

2. Second roaster: study approved

Marco presented the capacity situation. The drum roaster (15 kg, the Giesen-type machine bought in 2021) runs at about 85% of the capacity we can actually use given cleaning and cupping. With the wholesale growth Luca expects, plus the tasting bar and a cold brew pilot, the machine would hit the ceiling in the first half of 2027. Options discussed:
(a) buy a second roaster of the same size, new, about 78,000 euro installed, 14-16 weeks delivery;
(b) buy a used 5-kg sample-to-production machine for 24,000 and use it for single origins;
(c) do nothing and rent roasting time at a toll roaster in Chieri for overflow, at about 1.30 per kg roasted.
Roberto pointed out that (a) needs a new electricity line and gas permission, which could take longer than the machine itself. Anna said she wants a proper study before any decision: demand forecast, building, permits, financing. 
DECISION: approved a study of a second roaster, budget up to 4,000 euro for external help (electrician survey, equipment quotes). Marco and Elena to report to the September board. No purchase can be committed before the board has seen the study.

3. Cold brew pilot

Marco and Chiara propose 500 cans (250 ml, nitro-less, shelf life 90 days), produced in two batches in July and sold in the shop, at the bar and to two or three cafés for feedback. Cost estimate 2.35 per can incl. can, label and coffee, planned shelf price 3.50. Roberto asked what we do with the cans if they do not sell; Marco said we sell them at the Academy courses and give them to the cafés. DECISION: pilot of 500 cans approved, no further quantity without a review of sell-through in October.

4. Tasting bar and staffing

Sara reported on the licence (granted 29 May), the ASL visit, the opening on 27 June, the part-time baristas. The board asked what happens if the bar loses money. Elena: planned loss is under 600 euro a month in the first quarter. Review in September.
Paola asked whether the board would consider a second shop in the centre (Via Garibaldi) since "the bar will probably go well". Giulia's answer: not now. After a discussion the board did NOT approve a second shop; revisit in 2027 when the bar has six months of data.
A part-time packer (20 hours a week, to cover Monday-Tuesday packing and the web shop orders) was approved: budget 1,350 euro a month all inclusive. Sara to hire by 15 July.

5. Wholesale

Luca: Portello is stable (about 18 kg Aurora and 2 kg Night Shift a week), Mazzini and Gioberti ok, Bar Stazione starting. Pipeline of four cafés for the summer (Lagrange, Vanchiglia, Monti and one in San Salvario). Anna asked about concentration risk: Portello is about 40% of wholesale volume. Luca believes it will be below 30% by December if the pipeline signs. Giulia asked him to bring a credit-limit proposal to the next meeting because requests are coming.

- Green coffee hedging: 70% of the year bought six months ahead, as before. No change decided. Discussed again in October for H1 2027.
