Subject: July - where we are

From: Giulia Ferrero
To: All Lumen staff

Hi team,

before everybody disappears for August, a quick look at the month. It was a lot.

THE BAR
Five weekends in. About 120-150 drinks on Saturdays, 80-100 on Sundays, and many people leave with a bag, which is what I hoped for. The cupping evening on the 23rd sold out (12 places) and three guests signed up for subscriptions the next morning. We lost money on the bar in July, around 480 euro, below the 600 we planned. Thank you Ilaria, Matteo and everyone who came in on a Sunday.

PRICES
The new price list started on 1 July. About 70 subscribers cancelled in the first weeks, mostly for price. Elena's analysis shows that the decision pays for itself, and the numbers in the last two weeks are calmer (churn now at about 7.5% for the month). Davide added a pause button in the account page. If a customer asks, tell them.

CAFES
Bar Stazione ordered 6 kg on 15 June and again every two weeks since. Café Portello's credit line goes to 5,500 euro. Luca has three more cafés close to signing (Lagrange, Vanchiglia, Monti): meetings next week. Good news, but it means more kg on a roaster that is already at 85%.

SOURCING
Sara and Marco spoke with Andes Direct: the Brazil harvest is 10% lower than expected, the new crop will cost more (4.62 vs 4.31 per kg). Our fixed contracts protect us until the end of the year. We'll talk in October about next year.

NEW PEOPLE
Gaia started on 20 July as part-time packer. She already knows the label printer better than I do.

AUGUST
We run a reduced roasting schedule from 10 to 21 August (Monday, Wednesday, Friday only). Subscriptions ship as usual on Tuesdays. The bar is open Saturday and Sunday with summer hours, except 15-16 August (Ferragosto weekend) when it is closed. The holiday rota is in the shared calendar, please check yours.

Also: the second roaster study is running, the 500 cans of cold brew are made (the second batch is in the fridge), and the Aurora product page has a new text since the 22nd.

Have a good break. See you.

Giulia
