Roaster annual service - report

Visit date: Thursday 15 October 2026, 8:00-17:30
Technician: Davide Falco, Tecno Servizi S.r.l. (Settimo Torinese)
Machine: 15 kg drum roaster, installed 2021, about 5,900 roasting hours
Present for Lumen: Marco Bellini; Sara Conti in the afternoon
Report reviewed by Marco B on Fri 16 October and sent to Elena for the invoice

What was done
1. Full inspection of the gas line and burner: pressure 20 mbar, ok. Burner cleaned, ignition electrode replaced (worn, 18 euro part).
2. Drum bearing: front bearing has a slight play (0.2 mm). Greased. The technician recommends replacing both drum bearings within 6 months or 600 roasting hours, whichever comes first. Quote attached, 1,150 euro parts + labour for two days (he suggested the August break next year, or a Monday-Tuesday).
3. Exhaust fan and duct: cleaned, a 3 mm layer of oil residue in the elbow. Removed. Advice: monthly check, not every second month. Marco has the cleaning sheet at the "first Monday after the 25th" and will add an exhaust duct line.
4. Thermocouples: bean probe reads 1.4 C high at 200 C, replaced. Environment probe ok. Calibrated against the reference.
5. Cooling tray motor: ok. Stirrer arms worn, 8 months left.
6. Afterburner / smoke: the machine has no afterburner; we use a catalytic converter. Catalyst checked with the analyser: CO 410 ppm, the limit in the city rule is 500. Technician thinks we may exceed it in a year at the new volume. A new catalyst costs 1,900. To be planned in H1 2027, as part of the second roaster study.
7. Safety: flame failure test passed, emergency stop passed, gas leak test passed. The pressure relief valve label is missing; he replaced it.
8. Software: roast logging software updated to the current version; no change to profiles. Marco saved the 22 profiles before the update (Aurora, Night Shift, Guji lot profile, the sample profiles).

Test roast after service
2 batches of Aurora, 15 kg each, profile "Aurora v14". Drop temperature 212 / 213 C, difference 1 C. Time 12:10 / 12:08. Marco B cupped them against the stock from Tuesday: no difference. Good.

Findings classified by urgency
- Urgent: none.
- Within 6 months: drum bearings (1,150), catalyst check again in March.
- Within 12 months: stirrer arms (about 300), catalyst (1,900).
- Observation: roasting hours are up 31% on last year; the machine is working at 85% of usable capacity. The technician said "a machine like this is fine for 10 years, but not at 90% for all those years". He suggests keeping the maintenance every 6 months instead of once a year starting 2027. Cost of the half-year visit: about 450 euro.
