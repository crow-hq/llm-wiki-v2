CONTRACT FOR SERVICES (excerpt) - Lumen Academy trainer
Draft sent 4 September 2026 by Elena Rinaldi to Valentina Marchetti

Parties: Lumen Coffee Roasters S.r.l. (the Company) and Valentina Marchetti, VAT holder (the Trainer).

Art. 1 - Object. The Trainer teaches barista courses organised by the Company under the name "Lumen Academy", on the dates agreed in writing in the course calendar, which can be updated by the Company with at least 10 days' notice.

Art. 2 - Fee. 350.00 euro plus VAT for each half-day (up to 4 hours, including 30 minutes of setup). Courses shorter than 3 hours are paid at 240.00. Preparing the programme of the three first courses: 600.00 flat, one time.

Art. 3 - Cancellation. If the Company cancels a session less than 3 days before, it pays 50% of the fee. If the Trainer cancels, she proposes a replacement date and no fee is due.

Art. 4 - Materials. The Company provides machines, coffee, milk, cups and cleaning. The Trainer may bring her own tools. The slides and handouts prepared for the programme remain property of the Company.

Art. 5 - Insurance. The Company's civil liability policy covers participants during courses (confirmation requested on 28 August).

Art. 6 - Term. From 14 September 2026 until 31 December 2026, renewable. Each party can terminate with 30 days' notice.

Art. 7 - Payment. 30 days from the invoice, end of month.

Art. 8 - Non-exclusivity. The Trainer may continue to work for other companies, but may not use Lumen materials. During the term she does not give courses for another roaster in Torino.
