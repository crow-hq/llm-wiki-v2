Customer care - late carrier parcels, 22 May to 10 June 2026

Davide Gallo, 11 June 2026

Summary
Between Friday 22 May and Wednesday 10 June we received 23 contacts about parcels arriving late (three or more working days for Italian shipments, promise is 1-2). The carrier is the one we use for all web shop and subscription parcels, pickup 15:00 every weekday. Tracking numbers collected for 19 of the 23.

What the tracking says
- 14 of 19 were scanned at the carrier's hub in Settimo on the right day, then sat for a second night at the Milan hub.
- 3 were delivered to a wrong building number in Torino and picked up the next day.
- 2 are still "in transit" 9 days later (Cagliari, Palermo). Probably lost.

Pattern by day of shipment
Mon: 1 / Tue: 11 / Wed: 4 / Thu: 5 / Fri: 2
Tuesday is our subscription day (roasted Monday, packed Tuesday morning, shipped Tuesday afternoon), so the biggest batch, around 310 parcels a week, goes out on the day with the most problems. Packages left at pickup at 15:00 may be getting the evening sort in Milan only on Wednesday.

What we did
- 9 customers got a 5 euro voucher, 4 got a full refund of shipping, 2 asked for a refund and got it (within the 30 days).
- Sara called the account manager on Friday 5 June: he promised an "internal check" and a decision next week.
- I added a line in the subscription email: "your parcel left on Tuesday, may take until Thursday".

Complaints in the reviews page
Two 1-star reviews (both mention the carrier, not the coffee). I replied to both.

Suggestion
If by the 17th no improvement, ask for a quote from the second carrier Sara used in 2024, only for the Tuesday run. Cost is about 0.40 more per parcel according to Sara, that's 124 euro a week on 310 parcels.
