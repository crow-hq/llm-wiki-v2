Lumen Academy - booking and cancellation policy
Version 1, 21 October 2026, valid for all courses booked from 26 October. Owner: Sara Conti. Approved by Giulia Ferrero.

1. Booking
Courses are booked on the website and paid in full at booking. The confirmation email shows date, time, address and what to bring. Seats are reserved for 15 minutes in the cart.

2. Cancellation by the participant
- Up to 7 days before the course: full refund, or move to another date at no cost.
- From 6 to 3 days before: voucher for the same value, valid 6 months.
- Less than 3 days before, or no show: no refund and no voucher. Exception: the participant sends a replacement person with their name by email.
- Moves: one free move per booking, if more than 7 days before.

3. Cancellation by Lumen
- A course with fewer than 5 participants is cancelled at least 3 days before. Full refund or a transfer to another date.
- If the trainer is unwell, we propose a new date within 4 weeks, or a full refund.

4. Café staff courses
- Booked by the café, paid by invoice at 30 days.
- Free changes up to 7 days before. After that 50% of the fee (the room is rented).

5. Gift vouchers
Valid 12 months, not refundable, can be used for any course; a voucher for one person is nominal on the first use.

6. Other
- Children under 14 are not admitted. 16 and up for latte art.
- We take photos for the Academy page. Say it to the trainer if you do not want to be in one.
- Allergies: tell us when you book (milk is used in all courses, oat milk available).

This policy replaces the draft sent to the board in September.
