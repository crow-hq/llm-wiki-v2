Board pre-read - meeting of Tuesday 1 December 2026

From: Giulia Ferrero. Prepared with Elena Rinaldi. Date: Monday 30 November 2026. Please read before the meeting; the papers are in the shared folder. Agenda at the end.

1. Where we are

Revenue January-October: 1,296,000 euro, +14% on the same period of 2025. Gross margin on coffee 43.1% (41% in May, 42.5% in August). The July price list and the Brazil fixed contracts explain most of the recovery. Cash at 31 October 161,000. No debt except the supplier terms. November is the strongest month of the year so far: the Black Friday week had a record 1,138 parcels (the previous record was 762 in December 2025), and we had a backlog (incident report of 27 November: 148 subscription boxes shipped two days late, compensation about 640 euro, second label printer approved).

2. Subscriptions

Active: 1,271 on 11 November. Churn: July 8.2%, August 7.0%, September 6.3%, October 6.0% (the Q2 level was 6.1%). The price increase has therefore been absorbed in four months. New subscribers come from the shop, the bar QR and the gift subscriptions; the Academy brought 19 subscriptions since September. The pause button has been used 61 times and 40 of those subscribers are back.

3. Cafés

Eight cafés (nine until Bar Gioberti closed on 31 October; equipment back on 2 November). Volume in October: 410 kg, which means about 95 kg a week. Federica Greco started on 2 November and has visited all eight cafés. Year-end contracts for 2027 are being signed (deadline 15 December), same prices, same credit policy (v1 of 18 September). Two cafés asked for a loyalty discount. We answer with a rebate after 12 months, to be defined by 30 November: Giulia and Elena propose 2% of the kg invoiced in the year for cafés with at least 12 months with us and no late payments, starting in 2027. Expected cost 2027: about 400 euro, because only 5 cafés qualify. The Christmas blend for cafés has sold 140 kg in the first week.
Pipeline: Bar Palestro (San Salvario) signed a trial on 26 November.

4. Roasting and the second roaster

Capacity: 255 kg a week in September, 280 in November, ceiling 300. Toll roasting at Chieri was used for 340 kg in Q4 (1.30 per kg, 442 euro). The service on 15 October found no urgent issue; drum bearings replacement (1,150) in August 2027 and a catalyst (1,900) in 2027 are in the budget. The business case for the second roaster is attached (Marco and Elena), with three scenarios:
- A: no second roaster, toll roasting for peaks. Capacity covers 2027 only if growth is below 12%.
- B: new 15 kg roaster, 78,000 + 11,000 works, delivery summer 2027. Financing: 5-year loan 60,000 at 4.9%. Payback 4.3 years in the base case.
- C: used 5 kg roaster at 24,000, for single origins and small lots.
Management proposal for discussion: B, with the order placed in March 2027 after a check of the first quarter, not now. We ask the board to confirm the study conclusions and the March checkpoint.

5. Tasting bar

Losses of 480 (July), 390 (August) and about 150 (September); October break-even; November a gain of about 300 thanks to Fridays and the harvest event of 24 October (212 people, 63 bags sold). The Christmas menu starts on the 28th. Proposal: confirm Fridays as permanent, review in March.

6. Lumen Academy

Courses held: Basics x3 (24 people), Filter x1 (7), Cupping x2 (23), Café Staff x2 (9), Latte art x1 (6). Feedback average 4.6. Gift vouchers sold: 27, 1,980 euro. The rented room (Spazio Borgo Dora) works better than the bar floor; the trainer asked for it, and we use it for Saturday courses until January: cost 180 per half day. Margin of the autumn programme: about 2,300 euro, against the 3,500 euro budget. Dates for the first half of 2027 are drafted. A decision on the review in January is requested.

7. Sourcing

The hedging for H1 2027 decided on 6 November: 70% of Brazil volume fixed at 4.55 (Cerrado) and 4.62 (Sul de Minas), 50% of Colombia and decaf, the rest in January. Roughly 80,000 USD on Brazil. The Brazil container of September arrived on the 22nd as planned.

8. Risks we see
- Operations: the Christmas peak, a backlog could happen again; two label printers and a packing station 3 are in place.
- Concentration: Portello is 28% of wholesale kg.
- Cash: fine until March.
- People: 15 now with Federica. We are fully staffed. A full-time packer (instead of part-time) will be discussed in January if volumes stay.
