# Viral Tactics — Hook + Setup + Payoff Framework

Most content creators focus on information delivery and miss the psychological structure that makes audiences care, engage, and share. This framework breaks down the three essential components that drive viral adoption.

## The Core Model

A viral message works by capturing attention, building context, then delivering payoff. Each phase serves a distinct function in audience psychology and engagement.

### Hook: First 3–5 Seconds, Grab Attention (audience: 100%, completion: 35%)
*   **Surprise:** An unexpected statistic or question that contradicts common assumptions.
*   **Tension:**
    *   Create an persistent tension by opening a loop viewers cannot ignore mentally.
*   **Relevance:** Show the problem exists in their daily life right now.

### Setup: 6–15 Seconds, Build Credibility (audience: 35%, completion: 18%)
*   **Context:** Explain why the problem matters and feels urgent.
*   **Authority:**
    *   Anchor your claim to data, examples, or lived experience.
    *   Show that you understand their frustration deeply.
*   **Stakes:** Example *"Most users drop their expense tracker within 7 days..."* Validate *"...because they lack an actionable insight."*

### Payoff: 16–25 Seconds, Resolve the Tension (audience: 18%, completion: 12%)
*   **Solution:** Show the simple, unexpected path forward that solves the gap.
*   **Clarity:**
    *   Guide the viewer step-by-step through the mechanism or transformation.
    *   Keep language direct and jargon-free.
*   **Action:** Example *"Open the app, import one receipt, and watch it categorize automatically..."* Proof *"No manual tagging required."*

---

## Best Practices
*   **Timing:** Hook under 3 sec, setup under 12 sec, payoff under 10 sec — total 25 sec max.
*   **Repetition:** Every viral creator repeats the pattern 4–6 times across their channel before audiences internalize the message.
*   **Measurement:** Track hook-drop rate, setup retention, and payoff conversion separately — each reveals where your structure breaks.
