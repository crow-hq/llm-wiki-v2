# Subdirectories

* [curiosities](curiosities/index.md) - Small personal quirks and observations about Ettore himself, such as recurring lucky numbers and coincidences; not habits (wellbeing) or plans.
* [documents](documents/index.md) - Identity cards, personal records of Ettore and family, and account recovery codes (e.g. Bitbucket 2FA backups).
* [entertainment](entertainment/index.md) - Series, films, music and other leisure or educational watchlists and listening logs (e.g. conflict-management films, world-music tracks); not trips, sports or food.
* [family](family/index.md) - Family members and close people (e.g. Marta, cousin Sofia), Ruggine the dog, friends' contact records, birthdays, gift ideas; not documents or trips.
* [finance](finance/index.md) - Ettore's private money matters: monthly budgets, household bill and tax deadlines, card or ATM incidents, bank references, tax choices such as 8 per mille.
* [food](food/index.md) - Food and drink: preferences, recipes, dinner logs, restaurant and trattoria reviews, rum tastings; shopping lists live in the subfolder.
* [home](home/index.md) - Ettore's home in Padova: living-room furniture, terrace plants and furnishings, inventories of the flat; not vehicle or documents.
* [sports](sports/index.md) - Ettore's sports and outdoor hobbies: windsurfing at Lago di Garda, running, and the gear for them.
* [travel](travel/index.md) - Trips, bookings, itineraries, shared trip expenses (Splitwise) and lake or boat getaways; ideas for future destinations (e.g. Croatia, Puglia).
* [vehicle](vehicle/index.md) - Ettore's car (Volvo V40): maintenance checklists, servicing, inspections and related paperwork.
* [wellbeing](wellbeing/index.md) - Meditation, mindfulness, mental-health apps and habits, healthcare and dental treatment, and personal care such as haircuts and grooming.
