# Instagram Reel Script — Message to Freelancers (Charge More)

Stop underselling your three years of experience for hourly rates that don't match your skill level or your market.

---

### 0:00 – 0:08)

"Am I worth that much though?"  
*(internal whisper, unsure, hand on desk)*  
"Your client's budget won't pay your rent if you don't ask for it. They've already approved the project spend."

### 0:08 – 0:17)

"But what if they say no? Everyone on Upwork is cheaper than me. I've undercut for two years just to get the projects.

You know what? Let them choose cheaper. That's not your actual client."

### 0:17 – 0:27)

"Your skills took **years** to build. Stop pricing them like you're still learning.

Real clients pay real rates for real results, not for your nervousness about asking."

### 0:27 – 0:35)

"How many projects have you done on discount? How many midnight calls? How many scope creeps?

Too many, right? Today is the last one."

### 0:35 – 0:46)

"Call them tomorrow morning. Say your rate is now ninety euros per hour. Pause. Breathe. Let them speak first. Don't fill the silence."

### 0:46 – 0:52)

"They'll either match it.  
Or find someone cheaper.  
You already know which one wins for your future."
