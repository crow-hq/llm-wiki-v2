# Remote Collaboration: Async-First Teams

Async teams demand deliberate design and recorded decisions:

- **Clear Written Communication:** Every important team decision logged asynchronously within 48 hours reduces synchronous meetings across teams.
- **Global Timezone Support:** Enable members to work during peak hours without requiring constant daily synchronous interaction.
