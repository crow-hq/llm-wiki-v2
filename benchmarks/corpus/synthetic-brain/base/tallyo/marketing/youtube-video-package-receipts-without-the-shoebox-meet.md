### Transcript
#Spese senza carta

When was the last time you actually found a receipt you needed? If you're like most people, the answer is never. Three apps send digital receipts, you snap photos of paper ones, and the rest lives in a shoebox gathering dust. Come tax season, you're stressed, your accountant is frustrated, and cleanup becomes expensive.

That's what we saw happening with our own finances. So we started asking: what if expenses could work the way you speak? You come home, open Tallyo, and say, "Lunch with Sergio at Trattoria Padana, eighty-five euros, client development." Tallyo parses it, categorizes it automatically, marks it deductible, and files it. Fifteen seconds. No typing (here's where we handle the voice processing in the background to keep the app responsive).

The photo side is even simpler. You've got the receipt email or the photo already. Snap it once, and Tallyo's OCR reads the merchant, amount, currency, and date without you typing anything. Split restaurant bills with partners? Already parsed and ready to approve.

The Budget Map shows you real-time spending intelligence, not just historical logs. Instead of "work" and "personal" buckets, you see what percentage each client project ate of your monthly budget. Hit a threshold early? Tallyo reminds you. Pro plan includes unlimited custom categories and spending rules for weekly tracking.

Finally, offline-first design. Recording an expense in a restaurant with no WiFi? Tallyo queues it, syncs when you're online, and your dashboard updates automatically. Built on Firebase with local SQLite, the same architecture powering fintech companies worldwide.
