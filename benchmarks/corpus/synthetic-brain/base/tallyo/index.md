# Subdirectories

* [analytics](analytics/index.md) - Tallyo business metrics: revenue reconciliation (Google Play, Adapty payouts), cohort retention and acquisition-quality analyses; paywall mechanics stay in paywall.
* [app-ui](app-ui/index.md) - Tallyo interface and in-app features from the user's side: category taxonomy, merchant search, Budget Map, widgets, dashboard, UI fixes; feedback lives in feedback.
* [backend](backend/index.md) - Tallyo architecture and general server side: overview, search design, shared-expenses repo, tooling (PDF, PyPI, voice); specifics live in mcp-server, llm-workers, operations.
* [business](business/index.md) - Tallyo as a company: seed pitch deck, company account (Brightmoor Apps LLC), accountants and VAT deadlines; metrics stay in analytics, pricing in paywall.
* [feedback](feedback/index.md) - Tallyo user and beta-tester feedback, bug reports and feature-request lists awaiting triage (e.g. Daniel Weir, Ingrid); shipped fixes live in releases.
* [marketing](marketing/index.md) - Tallyo promotion: ad creatives and promo videos, Meta/TikTok/Google ads and pixels, social posts and captions, creator and outreach campaigns, marketing website.
* [paywall](paywall/index.md) - Tallyo subscriptions and paywall: Adapty SDK, localized pricing, TestFlight store tests.
* [releases](releases/index.md) - Tallyo versions: release notes, per-version bug-fix sprints and roadmaps, build/signing/notarization runbooks, store agreements, freeze policy, rollouts (Android, iOS, macOS).
