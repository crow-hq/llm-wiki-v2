3. Second roaster study (Marco)
Marco presented the first draft of the study (attached, 14 pages). Main points:
- The Monday roast runs at 45 kg of the 60 kg capacity of the current Probat-type drum on Mondays. Average capacity used across the week is about 85%.
- Options: (a) a second drum of the same size, 25 kg batch, at about €48,000 installed; (b) a larger drum, 35 kg, which would replace the current machine over two years, cost about €92,000 with the installation of a new extraction; (c) do nothing and buy a part-time roaster for the Saturday and Friday sessions, about €0 capital but more labour.
- Space: the roastery has room for a second machine on the existing floor if the packing station moves 2 m to the left. Marco has drawn it.
- Marco's view: option (a) for the next two years, and to review (b) when the subscription volume passes 1,500 active. He does not want to buy anything before the Huila and Guji lots are fully on the schedule in January.
- Andrea asked about lead time for option (a). About 14 weeks from order.
Decision: the board asked for a costed version of (a) and (c) with a cash plan before the December meeting. No purchase is authorised. Not a second shop; the board reiterated this.

4. Direct trade: contracts and sourcing (Giulia)
Giulia reported on the three direct relationships:
- Finca El Mirador (Rojas family, Huila): contract draft v2 in discussion; the Farm has asked for a change to the premium clause and the currency clause. Expected signature October.
- Asociación de Mujeres Cafeteras del Alto Magdalena (Huila): the cooperative contract is at the same stage; signature expected October with the Rojas contract.
- Gelana (Guji, Ethiopia) through Sidamo Ridge Exporters: pre-financing draft under discussion; samples cupped in September; first lots selected (W-01 and W-02). Expected shipment January to March 2027.
Board discussion: Andrea asked whether the premium policy (item 5) is consistent across the three. Giulia said yes in principle, the detail is in the policy document, which Elena has costed. The total premium commitment for 2027 is about €1,600 for Huila and an estimated €900 for Guji on the chosen lots.
Andrea asked about the risk of single-farm dependence. Giulia: the Rojas and the cooperative supply the same region and the lots are different; the contract allows us to buy elsewhere if a lot fails. Not dependent.
The board supports the direction and asked for a single list of direct-trade commitments in the December pack.
