**Finca El Mirador, Huila**
*Single origin, 2026 harvest, roasted light-medium*

€14.90 per 250 g, whole bean or ground. *(price to be confirmed by Giulia; the Guji is €14.90 so this is the working figure)*

A small farm above Pitalito, in the south of Colombia. The Rojas family has grown coffee on the same hillside for three generations. Don Hernán and his wife Yolanda still pick the ripe cherries by hand with a few members of the family. Their son Andrés has been running the drying since 2022 and has been trying to make it slower and more even.

This year's coffee was dried on raised beds under a tin roof. It was picked over six weeks, only the ripe cherries, which is why the cup is clean.

**In the cup:** red apple, panela, a soft finish of dark chocolate. Medium body, bright acidity. The sweetness shows up in the middle of the cup, not at the start.

**How we roast it:** light-medium, in small batches. We roast this coffee on Thursdays and it is at its best 5 to 10 days after the roast date.

**Brewing:** a V60 or a Chemex works well, 15 g to 250 g water, 93 degrees. For espresso it is less bright; try 18 g in, 36 g out, 28 seconds.

**What the price means:** we pay the Rojas family directly, above the market price, with a premium for lots that score 84 or above on our cupping. The contract is signed by both sides. Read more about how we buy from farms in our transparency report.

*Limited to what the farm produces this year. When it is gone, it is gone until next harvest.*
