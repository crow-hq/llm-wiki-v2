# Farm visit plan 2027 (draft)

Owner: Giulia Ferrero. Draft 17 November 2026. Once the board has approved the direct-trade budget on 15 December, this becomes the calendar.

## Why we visit

Visiting is how we check the coffee before we buy it, how we keep the relationship honest, and how we see the drying, the picking and the mill. Last year we visited once, in July, and the good lots came from the people we had met. The farms have asked for two visits a year, and Marco has said he wants to be there for at least one harvest.

## Who goes

- Giulia: every visit, first two days at least
- Marco: at least one visit in 2027, the harvest visit to Huila (October to November) because the drying is the most important part of the quality
- Sara: once, to Genoa for the Transmar warehouse and the lot check on arrival of the first Huila lot (January 2027), and once in Huila for the traceability sheet (optional)
- Chiara: photos only, with consent in writing and the farms' approval for each image. No posting until the contract is signed (both farms asked for this)
- Elena: none planned. If the cooperative wants the premium explained in person, she goes

## Rules for visits

- We pay our own costs. We do not accept gifts from farms beyond a bag of coffee for the office.
- Photos: ask before taking any photograph of a person. Ask again before posting.
- Do not promise a price or a volume on the visit. Confirm in writing afterwards.
- Make a visit report within 7 days of return, in the drive under "Farm visits".
