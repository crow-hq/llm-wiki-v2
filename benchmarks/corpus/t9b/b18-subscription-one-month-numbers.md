## Comments

The 3-week option is picking up well. The 4-week option is also strong, which I did not expect. I think people who pick 4-week are mostly the ones who moved from 2-week because they were worried about the price.

Referral is working better than I thought in the first week, slower after week 2. Chiara's second email to non-clickers helped (open to click rose from 9% to 14%).

Data quality: the export has one problem, the "frequency changed" event is recorded as a new subscription when the customer changes from the old plan. Sara has a fix on her list, not done. Until then the 1,186 includes about 60 changes. Careful when quoting.
