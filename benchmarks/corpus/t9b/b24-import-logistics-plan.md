1. SCOPE

Consolidation of green coffee from two origin suppliers in Huila (Finca El Mirador, Pitalito; Asociación de Mujeres Cafeteras del Alto Magdalena, La Plata) for shipment to Lumen's bonded storage in Genoa, then delivery to Torino.

Volumes for 2027 harvest (provisional):
- Finca El Mirador: 2,000 kg to 3,000 kg green, in lots of at least 300 kg
- Cooperative: 2,000 kg, lots 300 to 500 kg
Total: about 4,000 to 5,000 kg, 2 to 3 containers (20 ft, 19 tonnes max each, though we expect to fill less than 10 tonnes each).

2. TIMELINE (provisional, depends on harvest)

- Harvest and milling at each origin: Oct to Dec 2026 (depends on weather and drying)
- Samples to Lumen, 2 kg per lot: by 15 November 2026 (Transmar to arrange courier, Lumen pays)
- Lots gathered at Transmar Neiva warehouse: from 20 November 2026
- Export documents: phytosanitary certificate (ICA), certificate of origin, packing list, export declaration (DIAN). Transmar prepares; the Farm and cooperative sign their part.
- Port of loading: Cartagena (Contecar terminal). Vessel departure target: first half of December 2026.
- Transit Cartagena to Genoa: about 22 to 26 days direct, plus port time. Lumen should expect Genoa arrival January 2027. (Earlier I wrote mid-November in the field notes: that was too optimistic and Giulia has been told.)
- Customs (Genoa, bonded): Ines's office, 2 to 4 working days after arrival.
- Transport Genoa to Torino: by road, 1 day, in bonded or cleared form depending on Lumen's choice.

5. RISKS

- Harvest delay: a late harvest pushes the vessel date. Each week of delay is a week of storage at the Neiva warehouse at about €65 per container per week after the first 10 days.
- Drying problems at Finca El Mirador: the lot may come in below the moisture range. The contract (clause 3.2) allows rejection. Transmar will not ship a lot that fails the export moisture check.
- Freight rate changes: quote is valid to 31 October 2026. After that we re-quote.
