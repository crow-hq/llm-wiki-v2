# PRE-FINANCING AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT (DRAFT, Ethiopia Guji)

Draft prepared by Lumen Coffee Roasters for discussion with Sidamo Ridge Exporters. Subject to review by counsel. Not for signature.

Date of draft: 7 September 2026

Between:
(1) Lumen Coffee Roasters S.r.l., Via Madama Cristina 18, 10126 Torino, Italy ("Lumen"), represented by Giulia Ferrero, CEO; and
(2) Sidamo Ridge Exporters PLC, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia ("the Exporter"), represented by Tesfaye Abebe, Managing Director.

Recitals
A. The Exporter sources coffee from smallholder and estate farms in the Guji zone, including the Gelana farm (Tsegaye Alemu, the "Farm").
B. Lumen wishes to buy washed and natural lots from the Farm's 2026 and 2027 harvests.
C. Lumen will advance funds before shipment so the Farm can pay for harvest labour and drying, on the terms below.

1. Definitions
"Lot": a quantity of green coffee identified by a Lot ID as set out in Annex 1.
"Pre-financing Amount": EUR 27,000 for the 2026 harvest, being 30% of the Contract Value of the Lots listed in Annex 1.
"Contract Value": the price of the Lots at the Price in clause 3.
"Traceability Sheet": the sheet in Annex 2 completed for each Lot.

2. Pre-financing
2.1 Lumen will pay the Pre-financing Amount to the Exporter within 5 working days of signature.
2.2 The Exporter will use the Pre-financing Amount only to pay the Farm for harvest labour, drying and milling of the Lots, and will give Lumen a statement of use within 30 days of the payment.
2.3 The Pre-financing Amount is deducted from the final invoice for each Lot, pro rata to the Lot's contract value.
2.4 If a Lot is not shipped by 31 March 2027, the Pre-financing Amount attributable to that Lot is repaid to Lumen within 30 days, with interest at 3% per year from the date of payment.
2.5 If Lumen rejects a Lot under clause 4, the pre-financing for that Lot is not repayable if the Exporter offers a replacement lot of equal weight within 60 days.

3. Price and premium
3.1 The price is the FOB Djibouti price per kg in Annex 1, in EUR.
3.2 The Farm Premium is EUR 0.30 per kg for each Lot scoring 84 or above on Lumen's cupping. It is paid by the Exporter to the Farm, and the Exporter confirms payment with a receipt per Lot.
3.3 Lumen pays the Farm Premium through the Exporter only. Lumen will not pay the Farm directly.
[DRAFTING NOTE: Tesfaye asked whether the Premium can be paid to the washing station. Our answer is no: the Premium goes to the Farm. Keep 3.3 until he replies.]

4. Quality and rejection
4.1 Each Lot must score 80 or above on the Exporter's cupping and 80 or above on Lumen's cupping, performed on arrival in Genoa.
4.2 Lumen may reject a Lot that does not meet 4.1 within 14 days of arrival in Genoa.
4.3 Moisture at export between 10% and 12.5%. Any Lot above 12.5% at arrival is subject to a joint inspection under clause 8.

5. Shipment and delivery
5.1 Delivery terms are FOB Djibouti. Shipping through the Ethiopian Shipping Lines consolidator, documents issued in the Exporter's name.
5.2 Expected shipment: January to March 2027.
5.3 Risk passes at loading in Djibouti.

6. Payment of balance
6.1 Lumen pays the balance of the Contract Value, less the pre-financing applied, within 30 days of the bill of lading.

7. Traceability
7.1 The Exporter provides a completed Traceability Sheet for each Lot with the shipping documents.
7.2 Any Lot without a complete sheet can be rejected without penalty.

8. Joint inspection
8.1 If there is a dispute about moisture or defects, both parties may appoint an independent lab. The lab's result is final for the Lot. Costs are shared equally.

9. Term
9.1 The agreement runs to 30 June 2027.

10. Law
10.1 English law, arbitration in Addis Ababa under ICC rules. [Roberto Ferrara to confirm: our first draft said Italian law and Torino courts, Tesfaye's office asked for arbitration.]
