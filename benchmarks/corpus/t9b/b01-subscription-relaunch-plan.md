# Home subscriptions relaunch: plan (v3)

Giulia, Chiara, Sara, Luca, Davide, Elena, Marco
Last edited 2 June 2026

## Why we are doing this

Subscriptions are about 40% of online revenue but the old model is tired. People cannot change frequency without writing to customer care, the box looks like everything else we send, and churn was 6.1% in Q2. The first thing most people tell us when they cancel is "I drink less than I thought" or "I want a break, not a cancellation". We can answer both.

So the relaunch does three things:

1. A new box, "Lumen Box", with a printed insert that tells the story of the week's coffee (origin, the farm or cooperative where we can name one, roast date, a brewing suggestion). Same 250 g bags, same Aurora / Night Shift / Guji rotation, but the insert is new and the outer carton is the one from the packaging supplier (see the quote thread, Cartonificio Brenta).
2. Flexible frequency: every 2, 3 or 4 weeks, changeable from the account page at any time before the Monday roast. Right now the only options are 2 and 4 weeks and nobody can change them without an email.
3. A referral code. Existing subscribers get a code (format AMICO-xxxx) and the friend gets €10 off the first box; the referrer gets €10 credit on their next box once the friend's first box is paid. Cap at three referrals per account per quarter so we do not attract people who only want the discount.

## Pricing

We are keeping the same per-bag price for the Aurora and Night Shift subscription bags for existing subscribers until 1 July, then moving to the new price list. New subscribers see the new list from day one.

| Bag (250 g, whole bean or ground) | Old sub price | New sub price from 1 July |
|---|---|---|
| Aurora | €11.40 | €12.90 |
| Ethiopia Guji (seasonal) | €13.20 | €14.90 |
| Night Shift decaf | €12.10 | €13.60 |

Shipping on subscriptions stays at €5.90 Italy (free over €45, which a two-bag box reaches only at 3 bags or with the Guji) and €12.90 EU. Elena checked: at the new prices a 2-bag Aurora box is about 33% gross margin before packing labour, which is fine. The Night Shift decaf margin is thinner (about 26%) and we accept that for now because it is 12% of subscription orders and people who drink decaf leave if it disappears.

## What we measure

- New subscriptions per week (target: 150 in the first two weeks, which is a bit above normal)
- Share of subscriptions choosing 3-week frequency (we have no idea; we are guessing 25%)
- Referral invites sent and redeemed
- Cancellations in the first 30 days and the reason picked in the cancel form (the reason list is new: price, too much coffee, travelling, not liking the coffee, other)
- Support contacts per 100 new subscriptions (Davide's number; we want it under 8)

## Risks

The biggest one is a roasting slot problem: if the Monday roast runs over, the Tuesday pack gets pushed and the carrier pickup at 15:00 is missed. Marco has booked the Saturday before 8 June as a test roast day. The second risk is the referral code being abused by people creating accounts to get the discount. Elena will watch for accounts with the same delivery address; the rule is three referrals per quarter and no referral credit on the first box of a new account that was created in the last 30 days.

The third risk is the packaging. The new carton is 10 mm taller than the old one. If it does not fit the existing shipping bags for EU parcels, we use the old bags for Italy only and hold EU boxes until the new bags are in. Sara checked and the EU bags are in stock until the end of July.
