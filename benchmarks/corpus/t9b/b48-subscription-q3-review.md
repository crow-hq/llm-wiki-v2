# Subscriptions: Q3 review (July to September 2026)

Prepared by Elena Rinaldi with Chiara Russo and Davide Gallo. Reviewed at the weekly sync on 19 October. Source: shop export, corrected for the frequency-change bug (about 60 changes in July, now fixed in the export from 1 September; the August and September numbers are clean, July is adjusted).

## Summary

Subscriptions are doing what we wanted after the relaunch. Active subscriptions went from 968 at 8 July to 1,103 at 30 September. Churn was 4.8% in July, 4.5% in August and 4.9% in September, which is lower than Q2 (6.1%). Price is still the first reason people give for leaving.

The one thing that did not go to plan is the 3-week option is popular and the 4-week option is more popular than we guessed. Two-week subscribers are now the smallest group, which means a lot of the roastery planning around Monday is less even than it was.

## Frequency mix (active subscriptions, end of September)

| Frequency | Share |
|---|---|
| Every 2 weeks | 31% |
| Every 3 weeks | 27% |
| Every 4 weeks | 42% |

In June we expected 2 weeks to be the biggest group. It is not. Marco has asked for the roast schedule to be re-planned for the Monday because the Aurora volume is spread across more weeks.

## Coffee mix (active subscriptions)

Aurora 61%, Ethiopia Guji 25% (seasonal, so this moves), Night Shift decaf 14%. Night Shift is slightly above the 12% share of subscription orders we reported in the spring, because the Guji has been out of stock for part of August.

## Cancellation reasons, Q3 (181)

| Reason | Count | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Price | 58 | 32% |
| Too much coffee | 41 | 23% |
| Travelling or a break | 36 | 20% |
| Not liking the coffee | 19 | 10% |
| Other | 27 | 15% |

"Too much coffee" moved up from 18 to 41 when we compare July to September. Davide thinks this is because the holiday pause option is not in the app yet, so people cancel instead of pausing. The holiday pause option is being built (it is not yet live at the date of this review).

## Customer care

Davide: contacts are back to normal after the relaunch. Top three topics in September: changing frequency (the account page banner works, but people still ask), the referral code on wholesale (we say no, they ask again), and where is my box after a Monday order placed late.

The late Monday order is still the biggest single source of "where is my box" messages. We have changed the FAQ and put the cut-off in the confirmation email. Sara wants the cut-off moved to 11:00 on Monday for Italy only. Not decided.

## Referral

Referral is the cheapest customer we have. Redeemed referrals cost €10 each on the friend's side and €10 on the referrer's side. Over Q3 we issued €1,540 in credit. That is about 4.7% of subscription revenue in the quarter. Elena thinks this is fine. Giulia thinks the cap of three referrals per quarter is right.
