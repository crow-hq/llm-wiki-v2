# Roast profile: Finca El Mirador (Rojas), 2026 sample lot

Roaster: main drum, 60 kg batch max. This is a sample roast at 1.5 kg batches (test drum), the production profile to be set when the full lot arrives.
Roaster: Marco Bellini. Date of roast: 6 October 2026.
Samples: 2 kg received from the cooperative for comparison, 1 kg from Finca El Mirador (Rojas) (their drying was late, the sample was dried 4 days longer than the first Rojas sample we cupped in Huila; see the visit report).

## Green

- Moisture at roast: 10.8% (measured, Ketomatic)
- Density: medium-high
- Visual: even colour, a few quakers (2 per 100 g)
- Note: Rojas sample is more uniform than the first sample we saw in August, Andrés says the new drying room is not built yet, so this is done on the old beds with better turning.

Development time 2:10, development ratio 27.7%. Total time 7:50. Light-medium, City+. Target for the single origin: finish at City+ for the cupping; for the filter, drop 15 seconds earlier.

## Cupping result

Cupped at 24 hours after roast: clean, floral, some honey. Sweetness is there but thinner than the cooperative sample. Acidity is high, Chiara and Luca both found it "bright in a good way". Body is light.

Marco's view: the Rojas lot wants a slightly higher development (Dev 2:30) to get more body, which we will test on the 2 kg batch next week.

## Production profile (to be set)

- Full batch 25 kg on the main drum.
- Start with the test profile scaled up. Expect the first crack around 9:00 to 9:30 on the main drum.
- Do not roast the Rojas lot on a Monday. Monday is the subscription roast. Roast it on Thursday or Friday, pack it the next Monday at the earliest, or keep it as a single origin for the cafés.
- Keep the Rojas lot separate on the racks: bag labels, lot number and roast date.

## Notes for the shop page

Do not write "washed" or "natural" on the Rojas page until we confirm the process with Andrés. The sample was dried on beds, but the cherries were pulped before drying. Ask him.
