**SERVICE AGREEMENT**

Between

(1) **Lumen Coffee Roasters S.r.l.**, with registered office at Via Madama Cristina 18, 10126 Torino, VAT and tax code 11829460019 ("Lumen"), represented by **[SIGNATORY NAME AND TITLE]**;

and

(2) **[CLIENT NAME]**, with registered office at **[ADDRESS]**, VAT number **[VAT NUMBER]** ("Client"), represented by **[SIGNATORY NAME AND TITLE]**.

Lumen and the Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Machine" means the espresso machine and grinder listed in Schedule 1, supplied by Ruvo Espresso Systems S.r.l. ("Supplier") and made available to the Client by Lumen under the Supplier's agreement with Lumen.

1.2 "Coffee" means roasted whole bean coffee supplied by Lumen in the quantities and types in Schedule 2.

1.3 "Services" means the installation, the training, the service visits and the support described in clause 4.

1.4 "Office" means the premises at the address above, or any other premises the Parties agree in writing.

1.5 "Term" means the period in clause 10.

## 2. Supply of the Machine

2.1 Lumen makes the Machine available to the Client for the Term. The Machine is and remains the property of the Supplier. The Client has no right to sell, pledge, sub-let or move the Machine out of the Office.

2.2 The Client must keep the Machine in the Office, in a dry place with a stable electricity supply, and must not allow anyone other than Lumen or the Supplier's technicians to repair or open it.

2.3 The Client will tell Lumen within 24 hours of any fault, damage or theft, and in the case of theft, will also report it to the police and send Lumen a copy of the report.

## 3. Supply of the Coffee

3.1 Lumen will supply the Coffee in the quantities in Schedule 2 each calendar month. Coffee above the monthly allowance is charged at the price in Schedule 2.

3.2 Coffee is delivered to the Office on the day in Schedule 2. The Client must give Lumen at least 48 hours notice if the delivery day or address changes.

3.3 Coffee is sold fresh. The Client must store it in the sealed bag in a cool, dry place and use it within 30 days of the roast date printed on the bag.

3.4 Lumen may change the blend or origin of the seasonal coffee from time to time, and will tell the Client at least 14 days in advance. The Aurora blend will not be changed without at least 60 days notice.

## 4. Services

4.1 Lumen will install the Machine at the Office on the date agreed in writing. The Client is responsible for the water supply and drain connection, or for ordering the tank system from Lumen (price in Schedule 1).

4.2 Lumen will arrange service visits by the Supplier's technician or by a trained member of Lumen's team, as set out in Schedule 1 (number of visits per year). Parts for scheduled service are included.

4.3 Emergency call-outs in Torino are attended within 24 hours on a working day. Outside Torino, the Parties will agree a time in writing.

4.4 Training for up to two Client staff is given once at installation. Further training is charged at €60 per hour.

## 5. Fees and payment

5.1 The Client pays the monthly fee in Schedule 1 in advance, by SEPA direct debit, on the first working day of each month.

5.2 Coffee is invoiced monthly in arrears, payable within 30 days of the invoice date.

5.3 All prices exclude VAT, which is added at the rate in force on the invoice date.

5.4 Lumen may review the monthly fee and the coffee prices once a year, in January, by reference to the change in the green coffee price index. Lumen will give 60 days notice of any change.

5.5 Late payment: interest at the rate set by Italian Legislative Decree 231/2002 applies from the due date. Lumen may suspend coffee deliveries after 30 days late payment, after giving 10 working days notice.

## 6. Misuse and damage

6.1 The Client must use the Machine only with water filtered as shown in the user guide and must clean it as the user guide says. Lumen supplies the cleaning products at cost.

6.2 Damage caused by misuse, including cleaning with the wrong products, a missing water filter, or use by people who have not been trained, is not covered by the Supplier's warranty. Lumen will repair it and charge the Client a call-out fee of €90 plus the cost of parts.

6.3 Lumen will tell the Client in writing of any damage it finds, with photographs, before charging for it.

## 7. Liability and insurance

7.1 The Client keeps the Machine insured under its office contents policy, with the Machine named as leased equipment owned by a third party. The Client sends Lumen a copy of the policy schedule or a broker's letter confirming this before installation.

7.2 Lumen is not liable for any indirect loss, loss of profit or loss of business.

7.3 Lumen's total liability under this agreement, for any cause, is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim.

7.4 Nothing in this agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

## 8. Health and safety

8.1 The Client makes sure staff are told how to use the Machine safely, including the steam wand and hot water, and that the Machine is kept out of reach of children.

8.2 Lumen will provide the user guide in Italian and English.

## 9. End of term and the Machine

9.1 At the end of the Term, the Client returns the Machine to the Office door for collection by the Supplier. The Client may ask Lumen to arrange a sale of the Machine to the Client at the price in Schedule 1 (if offered; not available for all models).

9.2 The Client must not remove the Supplier's labels or ownership markings from the Machine.

## 10. Term and termination

10.1 This agreement starts on the installation date and runs for twelve months, then renews for three-month periods unless either Party gives three months written notice before the end of the current period.

10.2 Either Party may terminate with immediate effect by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and does not remedy it within 15 working days of notice, or becomes insolvent.

10.3 On termination, the Client pays for all Coffee delivered and all Services performed up to the date of termination. Clause 9 applies to the Machine.

## 11. General

11.1 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above, or by email to the addresses in Schedule 1.

11.2 This agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties about its subject matter.

11.3 Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.4 This agreement is governed by Italian law. The courts of Torino have exclusive jurisdiction, except that Lumen may bring proceedings for unpaid fees wherever the Client is based.

11.5 Personal data is processed as set out in the privacy notice in Schedule 3 (GDPR; staff names and office contacts only).
