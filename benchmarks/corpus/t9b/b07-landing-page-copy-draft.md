Hero
Your coffee, on your schedule.
Every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. Roasted on Monday, packed Tuesday morning, on your doorstep by Wednesday in Italy. Change the frequency or pause at any time from your account. The new Lumen Box has a card inside that tells you where your coffee came from.

Button: Start my subscription

How it works
1. Choose your coffee: Aurora (our house espresso), Ethiopia Guji (seasonal, changes every few months), or Night Shift decaf (Swiss Water, Colombia Huila).
2. Choose how often. You can change it from your account before Monday 12:00, and we will remind you the day before.
3. We roast it Monday, pack it Tuesday and ship it the same afternoon.

Why the box is different

The new box is smaller at the top and stronger. Inside every box there is a card with the roast date, the origin, and a note from Marco about how we'd brew it. No plastic window, the bag is kraft and recyclable.

What it costs
Aurora and Night Shift 250 g: €12.90 and €13.60 from 1 July (€11.40 and €12.10 until then). Ethiopia Guji 250 g: €14.90. Shipping €5.90 in Italy, free over €45. EU €12.90.

Refer a friend
Give a friend €10 off their first box. You get €10 credit when their first box is paid. Up to three referrals per quarter. Your code is in your account under "Friends".

Questions people ask
Can I skip a box? You can pause. Skipping is the same thing done by changing the frequency, and we will not charge you for the weeks you are paused.
Can I get an invoice? Yes for Italian private customers with a codice fiscale, sent after the order. Business invoicing is through our wholesale team, write to info@lumencoffee.example.
