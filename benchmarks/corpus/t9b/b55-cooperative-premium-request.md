Asociación de Mujeres Cafeteras del Alto Magdalena
Vereda Alto de la Plata, La Plata, Huila, Colombia

La Plata, 2 November 2026

Dear Giulia Ferrero and Lumen Coffee Roasters,

Thank you for your letter of 29 September and for the visit in July. We remember your visit with pleasure, and we were very glad to see Marco's notes on the cupping, which the members asked to read in full.

We write about the premium. The board of the Association met on 28 October and discussed the contract draft that you sent us in September, and the premium of EUR 0.40 per kilo for lots of 84 points or above.

The members asked us to say the following.

1. Our women members are the ones who pick the cherries by hand, sort them and dry the parchment. The premium is for them. Last season the cost of hand picking in our area went up by about 22%, because there are fewer pickers, many of whom go to the coffee farms that pay more in cash. We cannot keep our pickers at the price we pay them now.

2. We would like the premium to be EUR 0.55 per kilo, not EUR 0.40, for lots scoring 84 or above, and a small premium of EUR 0.15 per kilo for lots between 82 and 83.9, which we think is a fair way to reward the work on the lots that are good but not the best.

3. We would like the premium paid at shipment from Neiva, not at arrival in Genoa, so that the pickers are paid in December and January, which is when they need the money. We understand this is a change to the draft and we would like to discuss it.

4. We would like the premium amount for the 2027 harvest fixed in the contract, so that we know the number before the harvest starts. This is the first time we have had a contract with a fixed number from a buyer.

We know that Lumen has to think about its own costs. We are not asking for a big change. The difference in cost to Lumen on 2,000 kilos is about EUR 300 a year if the premium goes to EUR 0.55 and the lower band is included, which we understand is a number you can consider.

Lucía Pardo has asked us to say that the members would prefer a fair contract at a slightly lower premium to a higher premium that is not fixed. But they hope that you will consider the request seriously before the contract is signed.

We would be glad to talk on a call, with a translator if you need one. Our board can meet on any day before 15 December.

With warm regards,

Lucía Pardo
President, Asociación de Mujeres Cafeteras del Alto Magdalena

On behalf of the board: Carmen Ortiz (secretary), Judith Montalvo (treasurer), Blanca Rivera (quality)
