# Office coffee: machine rental terms (internal draft v2)

Written by Elena Rinaldi and Luca Moretti, 11 September 2026. For Giulia's review. Not for customers yet.

Machine: Ruvo Espresso Systems "Ruvo 2 Group" semi-automatic with grinder, supplied by Ruvo (Gianni Ruvo, Asti). We do not own the machine; Ruvo owns it and leases it to us on a 36-month agreement at €118 a month plus VAT. We pass it on to the client.

## Options for the client

| Option | Monthly fee (excl. VAT) | What is included |
|---|---|---|
| A. Machine only | €135 | Machine on loan, installation, one service visit a year, no coffee. Client buys coffee from us at the trade price. |
| B. Machine plus coffee | €165 | Machine, installation, coffee at up to 8 kg a month (Aurora or seasonal), two service visits a year, call-out fee waived. |
| C. Full service | €210 | Machine, installation, coffee up to 15 kg a month, four service visits, emergency call-out within 24 hours in Torino, staff training, milk jug and cups set. |

Coffee above the monthly allowance is charged at the trade price: €16.50/kg for Aurora, €22/kg for seasonal single origin (1 kg bags only for offices).

## Terms

- Minimum term 12 months, then 3-month rolling. Notice 3 months for either side after the first year.
- Installation at the client's cost for water and drain. If there is no drain, we quote separately (no drain kits in stock; Ruvo sell a tank system at €340).
- The machine stays Ruvo's property. The client may not move it, sub-let it or allow anyone outside the office to service it.
- Service visits are by Ruvo's technician or our own trained person (Marco has done the course). Parts are included in the service visits, not in call-outs for misuse.
- Misuse: the call-out fee for damage caused by cleaning with the wrong product, or by a missing water filter, is €90 plus parts. Client is told this in writing at installation.
- Payment monthly in advance by SEPA direct debit, 30 days terms on the coffee invoice.
- Price review once a year at January, on the green coffee index.
