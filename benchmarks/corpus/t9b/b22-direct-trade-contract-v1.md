# SUPPLY AGREEMENT: DIRECT TRADE (DRAFT v1)

Draft for discussion. Not signed. Not for circulation outside Lumen, Finca El Mirador and the Cooperative until reviewed by counsel.

Dated: 10 August 2026

Between
(1) Lumen Coffee Roasters S.r.l., Via Madama Cristina 18, 10126 Torino, Italy ("Lumen"), represented by Giulia Ferrero, CEO
(2) Hernán Rojas and family, trading as Finca El Mirador, vereda Alto de Pitalito, Huila, Colombia ("the Farm")

## 1. Purpose

The Farm agrees to supply, and Lumen agrees to buy, green coffee from the Farm's 2027 harvest (and, if both parties agree in writing, from later harvests) on the terms of this agreement.

## 2. Volume

2.1 Lumen will buy a minimum of 2,000 kg of green coffee per harvest year, in lots of not less than 300 kg.
2.2 The Farm may offer up to 3,000 kg per harvest year. Lumen is not obliged to take more than 2,000 kg, but has a first right of refusal on any additional lot.
2.3 Volumes are stated as green coffee weight (after milling, excluding parchment and husk).

## 3. Quality

3.1 Coffee must be specialty grade (score 80 or above on the SCA cupping protocol performed by Lumen or by a lab appointed by Lumen).
3.2 Moisture at shipment between 10% and 12.5%. Defects not more than 5 per 300 g (SCA green grading).
3.3 Lumen may reject any lot that does not meet 3.1 or 3.2. The Farm may ask for a second cupping within 7 days of rejection.

## 4. Price

4.1 Price is the Base Price plus the Quality Premium.
4.2 The Base Price is the ICE "C" price on the date of the shipment's bill of lading, plus the differential in the Andes Direct contract for Huila, as published monthly, minus the Farm Service Fee of USD 0.05 per pound.
4.3 The Quality Premium is EUR 0.40 per kg for each lot scoring 84 or above on Lumen's cupping, paid on the lot's delivery to Genoa. [NOTE TO GIULIA: 84 agreed in the field. Check against Elena's numbers before we put a figure in. See the premium memo.]
4.4 No Quality Premium is paid on lots scoring between 80 and 83.9. The Base Price applies to all lots.

## 5. Payment

5.1 Lumen pays 50% of the price on the Farm's invoice at the time of shipment from Neiva, and the balance on arrival in Genoa.
5.2 Payments in euros to the Farm's account in Bancolombia, Pitalito branch. Currency risk is shared as set out in Annex B (to be drafted; the parties have not agreed the hedge).
5.3 Bank charges are paid by the party that instructs the transfer.

## 6. Delivery

6.1 Delivery terms are FOB Cartagena (Colombia). Export documentation is the Farm's responsibility through the consolidator, Transmar Freight S.A.S., Neiva.
6.2 Risk passes at loading in Cartagena.
6.3 Lumen arranges and pays the ocean freight to Genoa through Transmar Freight.

## 7. Pre-harvest visits and samples

7.1 Lumen may visit the Farm once a year during harvest, at Lumen's cost.
7.2 The Farm sends Lumen 2 kg of each lot before shipment at the Farm's cost.

## 8. Traceability and labelling

8.1 Each lot is identified by a lot number issued by the Farm. Lumen may print the Farm's name on packaging with the Farm's written consent (consent is needed for each product and each design).
8.2 Lumen may use the Farm's photographs in its own communications with the Farm's consent in writing.

## 9. Term

9.1 The agreement runs from signature to 31 December 2027.
9.2 Either party may end the agreement on 90 days written notice before 1 July 2027.

## 10. Governing law

10.1 Italian law. Courts of Torino.
