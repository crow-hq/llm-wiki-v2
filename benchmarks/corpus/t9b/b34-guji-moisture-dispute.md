# Incident report: Guji lot GEL-W-02, moisture above specification

Date opened: 9 September 2026
Opened by: Marco Bellini
Lot: GEL-W-02, Gelana farm, washed, 300 kg net, sample arrived 4 September (it was one of the 1 kg samples, but the full lot was already in Genoa at that time for a separate test shipment; see below)
Status: open, awaiting independent lab result

## What happened

The GEL-W-02 lot arrived in Genoa on 1 September as a test shipment of 300 kg. Our own check on arrival (Sara and Marco, 2 September) used the Ketomatic handheld meter, which gave 12.9% to 13.4% across 10 bags. The Exporter's certificate, issued at Addis on 18 August, says 11.0%.

The lot is not yet in our bonded warehouse; it is on Ines's floor at Transmar's Genoa office because the customs release was held on 1 September. The Exporter says the meter is wrong and that their lab reading is correct. We say the lab sample may have been taken from the top bags only (the dry ones).

Tesfaye Abebe has said by email that he will not accept a rejection on the handheld meter alone and asks us to do a lab test at a lab both parties agree.

## Facts we have

- Export certificate (Addis lab, 18 August): 11.0% moisture.
- Our handheld (Sara, 2 September, 10 bags, bag top and bag middle): 12.9 to 13.4%.
- Our handheld on the 1 kg sample from the same lot (Marco, 3 September, sample sealed): 12.7%.
- Storage in Genoa since 1 September, room at 21 degrees, 58% humidity. This matters: a lot can gain moisture in humid storage, but 300 kg in a dry room for 10 days should not move 2 points. We think it is either the export test or the meter.
- Clause 4.3 of the draft pre-financing agreement (not signed) sets 12.5% as the top limit at arrival. The sale is not yet signed, so the clause does not bind us, but Tesfaye has been told we will hold the same standard.
- Lot defects and cupping: our cupping on 4 September gave 84.25. Their score was 84.75. So we like the lot.

## Options

1. Independent lab (Eurofins in Turin, or the Sanremo lab that Andes Direct uses). Result in about 5 working days. Cost €140. Both parties to accept the lab. We propose this.
2. Accept the Exporter's 11.0% and release the lot, with our handheld readings in the file. Risk: if the lot is at 13%, mould risk in transit and the cup will be flat.
3. Reject the lot and move to the next lot (GEL-W-03). Costs us the time and the premium on W-02.

Marco recommends option 1. Sara recommends option 1 and asks for the lab sample to be taken from three depths of the bag, not the top.

## Decisions needed

- Sara to hold the lot in the Transmar Genoa office until the result. No release to Torino.
- Marco to take the lab sample from the bags on Friday morning with Ines present, sealed in three depths. Ines to witness.

## Update 14 September

Lab result received from Eurofins (report 2026-09-1187): 11.3% moisture. Within the range. The meter was reading high on the flat sample (we had the calibration done on the wrong reference card; Marco has the card on file now). Lot released to Lumen. Exporter has been told. Tesfaye has said the premium for W-02 will be paid as normal.

Lesson for us: check the meter with the reference card before the lot arrives. Marco has put this in the arrival checklist.
