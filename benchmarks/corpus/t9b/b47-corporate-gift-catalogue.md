# Lumen gifts for business: autumn and Christmas 2026 (draft catalogue)
Draft of Wednesday 14 October 2026

For Chiara, Luca and Giulia. Prices are for the boxes as sold, excl. VAT for business invoices. Photos to come from Marco on Friday (his phone, he says it is fine).

## 1. The Office Tasting Box (€36)

Three 100 g bags: Aurora, Ethiopia Guji (seasonal, currently Gelana W-01), Night Shift decaf. A small card with the roast dates and a note on brewing. Plain kraft box, black ribbon. Good for a team of up to 6 as a thank-you, or for clients who want to try the coffee.

## 2. The Kitchen Box (€68)

Two 250 g bags (Aurora and Guji, or Aurora and Night Shift as the client wishes), a Hario V60 dripper and filter papers, a 250 ml gooseneck kettle is NOT included (we looked at it; the margin does not work at this price and the kettle would break in the post). Card with brew recipe.

## 3. The Espresso Box (€95)

Two 250 g bags of Aurora, a stainless milk jug (350 ml), a set of 2 espresso cups with saucers. Our own printing on the jug is an option: minimum order 20 jugs, 3 weeks.

## 4. The Roastery Box (€145)

Three 250 g bags of the seasonal single origin in different roasts, a tasting card with cupping notes, and a half-day visit to the roastery for 2 people (booked for Saturday mornings, Jan to March 2027 only). Limited to 40 boxes this season.

## 5. The Cold Brew Set (€42)

Four 250 ml bottles of cold brew from the roastery line (our own line, not the co-packer), and a glass with a silicone lid. Not sent outside Italy; delivered in the chilled bag, Torino and Milan only. Please note the cold brew best before date is 30 days from filling.

## Custom labels

Companies can have their logo on the bag or the box for orders of 50 or more of one box. Minimum 4 weeks lead time, 2 weeks for a proof and a sample. The logo must be supplied as vector art. We do not print logos on the Aurora bag itself, only on the outer sleeve.

## Ordering

- Orders for delivery before 15 December must be placed by 25 November.
- Invoices are by email, payment 30 days (larger orders by arrangement; Elena to set the limit).
- Shipping to one address: €5.90 for orders under €200, free above €200. Multiple addresses: quoted by Sara.
- Gift message card included on request, free.
