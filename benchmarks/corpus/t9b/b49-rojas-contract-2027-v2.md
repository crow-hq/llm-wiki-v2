# SUPPLY AGREEMENT: DIRECT TRADE, Finca El Mirador (DRAFT v2)

Changes from v1 (10 August 2026) are in the table at the end. Clauses not listed are unchanged. Draft only, for discussion with Finca El Mirador. Not signed.

Dated: 19 October 2026

Between
(1) Lumen Coffee Roasters S.r.l., Via Madama Cristina 18, 10126 Torino, Italy ("Lumen"), represented by Giulia Ferrero, CEO
(2) Hernán Rojas, Yolanda Ruiz and Andrés Rojas, trading as Finca El Mirador, vereda Alto de Pitalito, Huila, Colombia ("the Farm")

## 1. Purpose

The Farm agrees to supply, and Lumen agrees to buy, green coffee from the Farm's 2027 harvest on the terms of this agreement.

## 2. Volume

2.1 Lumen will buy a minimum of 2,000 kg of green coffee for the 2027 harvest year, in lots of not less than 300 kg. Lumen intends to buy 2,000 kg; it has no obligation to buy more.
2.2 The Farm may offer up to 3,000 kg. Lumen has a first right of refusal on any additional lot, for 10 working days from the lot list.
2.3 Volumes are stated as green coffee weight after milling, excluding parchment and husk.
2.4 The Farm will supply a lot list by 15 January 2027 with estimated volumes by lot.

## 3. Quality

3.1 Specialty grade: score 80 or above on the SCA protocol, cupped by Lumen or by a lab appointed by Lumen.
3.2 Moisture at export between 10% and 12.5%, tested at the Farm's warehouse and at the consolidator's warehouse at Neiva. Both certificates are sent to Lumen.
3.3 Defects not more than 5 per 300 g.
3.4 Lumen may reject any lot that does not meet 3.1 or 3.2. A second cupping may be asked for within 7 days of rejection.
3.5 Processing: the Farm confirms in writing the process for each lot (washed, honey, natural or other) and the drying method.

## 4. Price

4.1 Price per kg is the Base Price plus the Quality Premium.
4.2 The Base Price is the ICE "C" price on the date of the bill of lading, plus the Huila differential published monthly by Andes Direct (Annex A), minus the Farm Service Fee of USD 0.05 per pound.
4.3 The Quality Premium is EUR 0.40 per kg for each lot scoring 84 or above on Lumen's cupping, paid under clause 5.
4.4 No Quality Premium is paid on lots scoring between 80 and 83.9. The Base Price applies to all lots.
4.5 The Farm Service Fee and the Quality Premium are not subject to adjustment during the Term. [Elena: confirm before this goes to the Farm.]

## 5. Payment

5.1 Lumen pays the Base Price and the Quality Premium on the Farm's invoice in two parts: 50% at shipment from Neiva, and 50% on arrival in Genoa.
5.2 Payments are made in euros to the Farm's account at Bancolombia, Pitalito branch. The parties agree the currency arrangement in Annex B. Lumen will not be liable for changes in the COP rate after the shipment date, except as set out in Annex B.
5.3 Payment at shipment is made within 5 working days of the Farm's invoice and the export documents from the consolidator.
5.4 Bank charges are paid by the party that instructs the transfer.

## 6. Delivery

6.1 Delivery is FOB Cartagena. The consolidator, Transmar Freight S.A.S., Neiva, is the exporter of record and issues the export documents. The Farm supplies the lot documentation to Transmar.
6.2 Risk passes at loading in Cartagena.
6.3 Lumen arranges and pays ocean freight to Genoa through Transmar Freight.

## 7. Visits and samples

7.1 Lumen may visit the Farm once a year during harvest, at Lumen's cost.
7.2 The Farm sends Lumen 2 kg of each lot before shipment at the Farm's cost, by courier.

## 8. Labelling and the Farm's name

8.1 Lumen may use the Farm's name on packaging and in product communication with the Farm's written consent, given for each product and each design.
8.2 The Farm may ask to see any product text before publication. Lumen will send it at least 5 working days ahead.
8.3 Photographs of people from the Farm are used only with their consent. Andrés Rojas has said the family agrees to the use of the farm name and the coffee photographs; the Farm will confirm the consent for individual people in writing.

## 9. Premium for Gelana and other lots

Not applicable to this agreement.

## 10. Term and notice

10.1 The agreement runs from signature to 31 December 2027.
10.2 Either party may end the agreement on 90 days written notice, which may not be given before 1 July 2027.

## 11. Governing law

11.1 Italian law. Courts of Torino. Parties may agree to refer disputes to an independent lab for quality questions only (clause 3.4).
