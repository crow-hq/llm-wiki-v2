# Farm premium policy (direct trade), version 1.0

Owner: Giulia Ferrero
Prepared with: Elena Rinaldi (costing), Marco Bellini (cupping and quality), Sara Conti (lots and traceability)
Approved by: Giulia Ferrero, 24 September 2026
Applies to: all direct-trade lots bought by Lumen from 1 October 2026, including lots from the 2026 harvest that have not yet shipped.
Review: every six months, first review March 2027.

## 1. Why we have a policy

We now buy some coffee directly from farms and cooperatives, and we pay a premium above the market price for coffee that meets a quality standard. Without a written policy, each contract would set its own number and the premium would drift. This document sets one rule that applies to all of them, so that a farm in Huila and a farm in Guji are treated in the same way, and so that the board and the staff can see what we are paying and why.

The premium is not a charity payment. It pays for quality the farms produce and for the work they do to get there (picking only ripe cherries, drying for longer, keeping lots separate). It also gives us a reason to keep buying from them.

## 2. What the premium is

The Farm Premium is an amount per kilogram of green coffee, added to the price we pay on top of the base price (the market price plus the differential in the relevant contract). It is paid only on lots that meet the standard in section 3.

It is not a bonus for volume. Buying more kilos does not raise the premium per kilo.

## 3. The standard

A lot earns the premium when:
1. Lumen's own cupping, done by Marco Bellini or by a person he trains, scores the lot 84 or above on the SCA protocol. (The exporter's or the farm's score is shown to us but does not count.)
2. Moisture at export is between 10% and 12.5%, tested at the warehouse before shipment, and the test is on the certificate.
3. The lot has a complete traceability sheet (see the sheet for each lot: plot, altitude, process, picking and drying dates).
4. The lot is delivered to Lumen's bonded storage in Genoa, or in the case of Guji, the Genoa Transmar warehouse.

If a lot misses (3) only because one field is blank, Sara may ask the exporter or farm for the missing field before the premium is decided. The premium is not paid until the sheet is complete.

## 4. The amount

The premium is €0.40 per kg for Huila (both the Finca El Mirador and the cooperative), and €0.30 per kg for Guji. The Huila figure is higher because the price of Colombian coffee on the market was higher than Ethiopian coffee at the time the contracts were priced, and the farms asked for a number that covers the extra drying work. Both figures are fixed for the 2026 and 2027 harvests.

Elena's costing (see memo of 18 September) shows that the premium adds roughly €0.13 per kg to the average cost of the Aurora base, if direct lots make up 15 to 20% of the base by volume. This is within the margin the board has accepted for the Aurora.

## 5. How the premium is paid

- Huila: paid to the Farm (or the cooperative) in euros to the account named in the contract, in two parts: 50% at shipment from Neiva, 50% on arrival in Genoa, together with the base price. The premium is included in the invoice as a separate line.
- Guji: paid to the farm through Sidamo Ridge Exporters. Lumen does not pay the farm directly. Sidamo Ridge gives a receipt per lot showing the amount paid to the farm.
- The cooperative asked for payment at shipment, not at arrival, so that the pickers can be paid. This is in the contract for 2027 and is reflected here as a principle; the timing is in each contract.

## 6. What the premium is not

- It is not a guaranteed amount for any lot that does not meet section 3.
- It is not a reason to buy a lot that fails cupping. A lot that scores below 84 but above 80 is still bought at the base price if we want it for a blend.
- It is not a loan. Pre-financing is a separate agreement and is repaid from the coffee price.

## 7. Communication

Staff may explain the premium to customers in the words of section 2. Do not give farm names or amounts to customers before the farm has agreed in writing (see the transparency report, which is being reviewed).

Anyone asked about the premium by a wholesale customer, a journalist or an investor should send the question to Giulia.

## 8. Changes

This policy can be changed by the CEO with the approval of the board. Changes apply to lots bought after the change, not to lots already contracted.
