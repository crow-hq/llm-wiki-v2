## 1. Summary for the board

The business is in good shape, with two things taking most of our time. The first is the direct trade work, which is larger than we planned: we now have two Huila contracts and one Guji supplier, and the first 2027 decisions are on this agenda. The second is the new office coffee and corporate gift business, which had a strong autumn: four companies signed for office coffee, and corporate gift orders are about €13,900 excl. VAT for Christmas.

The subscriptions are steady. Active subscriptions at the end of October were about 1,140. Churn is around 4.7% a month. The holiday pause went live on 18 November and it is too early to see the effect.

We are asking the board to decide on the premium for 2027 (the cooperative has asked for more), authorise the contracts for signature, and approve a part-time analyst for the contract work from January. The detail is below.

## 3. Subscriptions and the holiday pause

Active subscriptions were 1,103 at 30 September and about 1,140 at 31 October. Churn was 4.9% in September and 4.6% in October.

Cancellation reasons are still led by price (about 30%). "Too much coffee" fell in October after the pause was announced in a staff note; the pause went live on 18 November.

Price review: we agreed in the September board that a price review would take place in October. The review (Luca and Elena) concluded that the Aurora and subscription prices stay as they are until the spring. Night Shift decaf is the product with the thinnest margin (about 26%) and has the most price-sensitive customers (one in five would switch to Aurora at +€1 a bag, from the August survey). The 500 g decaf bag has been asked for by 88 customers and is on hold until we know the cost of the Huila decaf question. We recommend no price change in 2026.

## 5. Office coffee and corporate gifts

Office coffee: four companies have signed for the office coffee service (Option B, €165 a month plus coffee allowance). Brenner Assicurazioni was the first, installed in late September. Vallauri & Partners went in during the week of 2 November. Ottavi Studio and Nuvola Software have signed as well, and two more are in the pipeline. The Ruvo machines are on loan from Ruvo Espresso Systems. One breakdown at Brenner on 12 October was fixed the same day by Ruvo's technician. We now carry a spare filter and a tank kit for installation.

The standard service agreement is version 3, reviewed by Roberto Ferrara. Its main terms are a 12-month minimum, with three-month rolling terms after that, and a call-out fee of €90 for misuse. Clause 9 says the machine goes back to Ruvo at the end of the term; the client may ask for a purchase price, which Ruvo offers for some models only.

Corporate gifts: 10 orders from 9 companies plus one charity, about 233 boxes, €13,900 excl. VAT. Deliveries are scheduled by 19 December. One charity asked for a 10% discount; the answer was no. Nuvola Software has a custom sleeve. Gift catalogue and tracker are in the drive.

Risks: the corporate gift business is seasonal. Office coffee needs a second person on installation and service from January, which is the reason for the part-time analyst. The second roaster study (item 6) is the other question. The machine rental is a 36-month commitment from Ruvo and the board should be aware that a cancellation before 36 months is a cost to us, not to the client, if the client leaves early. Elena is checking the early-termination clause in our Ruvo agreement.
