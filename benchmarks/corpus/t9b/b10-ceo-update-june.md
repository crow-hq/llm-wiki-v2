Subject: Update from Giulia, end of June

Hi all,

A longer one this month, sorry. Some of you asked for it after the board.

Board (16 June)
The board met on the 16th. We approved three things. First, a study of a second roaster: not buying it, just looking at what it would take, costs, space, whether the Monday roast could move to two machines. Marco is leading it with Elena, first draft in September. Second, a pilot of 500 cans of cold brew, which we will test with the Turin wine bars and two gyms before deciding anything bigger. Third, a part-time packer, about 20 hours a week, starting in the autumn if the subscription numbers hold. There is no second shop on the table. The board was clear on that and so am I.

Prices
The new price list takes effect on 1 July. I know that is not a surprise to anyone who has looked at the green coffee market this spring, but I want to say it plainly: Aurora goes from €13.90 to €15.50 for the 250 g bag in the shop, and the subscription price moves as in the relaunch plan. The increase is roughly 11%. Green coffee went up about 19% on the Andes Direct contract for the second half of the year. We are passing on most of it and absorbing the rest.

Please, if a customer asks you why, say the green price went up, not "the market", and do not promise anything about next year.

Subscriptions
First-week numbers were good: 412 new subscriptions, 37 referrals redeemed. Week two is tracking the same. Cancellations are low. I will give the one-month numbers on the 21st, Elena is pulling them. Thanks to Chiara and Davide, who ran the launch week almost alone while the rest of us were on the roaster.

Roaster
Enrico came for the burner. The nozzle is worn; he will replace it in the autumn service, which we have pushed to October. We are running at about 85% of capacity, which is fine for now.

Wholesale
Portello are growing and their second Wednesday delivery in July is agreed. Luca is looking at two new cafés in Moncalieri and Chieri; the rule is still the same: tasting, 2 kg trial, grinder visit, payment on delivery for three orders then 30 days, minimum 5 kg.

Brazil
We've bought the July lot of Brazil from Andes Direct (lot 3, the Mogiana natural). Marco says it is the best cup we have had from them in two years. Our hope is that the Aurora tastes the same in July. If it does not, Marco will tell you before the customers do.
