# Night Shift decaf: customer survey summary

Survey run 4 to 14 August 2026 by Chiara Russo. Sent to the shop mailing list (2,791 contacts) and posted in the subscriptions account page for two weeks. 612 responses (22%).

Questions were: how often you drink decaf, what you drink it in, what you think of Night Shift, what would make you buy it more, and whether a price change would change your order. Free-text answers were coded by Chiara and Davide together, one pass each, then compared.

## Who answered

- Drink Night Shift: 384 (63%)
- Drink another decaf (not ours): 71 (12%)
- Do not drink decaf but have someone at home who does: 97 (16%)
- Other: 60 (10%)

Of the 384 who drink Night Shift, 291 buy it on subscription, 93 buy it in the shop.

## Taste

- "Good, I'd recommend it": 71%
- "Fine, nothing special": 19%
- "Not for me": 10%

Most common words in the free text: chocolate, smooth, "doesn't taste like decaf". The people who did not like it mostly said it was too light for a milk drink. A few said the aroma is weaker than they expected from the bag.

## Price

We asked: "If the price went up by €1 a bag, would you keep buying Night Shift?"

- Yes, same as now: 51%
- Yes but I would buy less often: 22%
- Switch to regular Aurora: 17%
- Stop drinking coffee at home / switch to another brand: 10%

Among subscribers only, the switch-to-Aurora figure is 19%.

## How they brew it

Moka pot 14%, espresso machine 46%, filter / v60 27%, French press 8%, other 5%. Most of the espresso drinkers said they chose decaf because they drink more coffee in the evening.

## What would make them buy more

- Bigger bag size (500 g): 88 mentions
- A cheaper bag: 61
- More information on the origin: 54
- Ground for moka (not fine enough, they say): 19
- Nothing: 143

## Notes for us

1. Night Shift is well liked, but the people who buy it are not loyal to decaf. If the price moves again, a fifth of them change to Aurora. That matters for the October price review.
2. The 500 g size was asked for more than anything else. Marco says we can make it, the packing line is the same, but Sara says the subscription shipping weight goes up and Elena needs the margin before we say yes. Not decided.
3. The origin question is the one we can answer well. The Huila lot is changing in September; the new lot is from the cooperative if Andes Direct can sell it to us. Chiara has written two lines we can use on the page. Marco to check the taste before we say it.
4. Some of the free text was about the decaf process (Swiss Water). Several people asked whether it is "chemical-free". The answer is yes; Chiara has the wording from the importer and will put it on the product page.
