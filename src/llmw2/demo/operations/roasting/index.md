# Notes

* [Aurora roast profile](aurora-roast-profile.md) - Charge at 205 °C, first crack at 9:10, drop at 11:30, with 1:45 of development.
* [Quality control and cupping](quality-control-and-cupping.md) - Every batch is cupped the day after roasting; below 82 points a coffee is not sold as single origin.
* [Roaster maintenance](roaster-maintenance.md) - Chaff cleaned weekly, exhaust monthly, burner serviced yearly; the roaster runs at 85% capacity.
