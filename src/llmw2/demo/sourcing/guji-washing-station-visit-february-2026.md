Two days at the washing station in February 2026.

- About 900 smallholder farmers deliver cherries; the station processes 60 tonnes a season.
- Lots were cupped on site: the best washed lot scored 86.5 and was bought for the seasonal single origin.
- A new drying area with raised beds should cut defects next season.

Agreed for 2026: 1,800 kg at a price fixed on the day of signature, delivered by May.
