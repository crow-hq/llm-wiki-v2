The seasonal single origin for 2026 comes from the same washing station that supplies the Guji in Aurora.

- Process: washed, dried on raised beds.
- Cupping score at arrival: 86.5.
- Notes: jasmine, bergamot, peach.
- Volume: 1,800 kg; about 40% is kept for Aurora, the rest is sold as filter coffee.

It is roasted light for filter and sold only in 250 g bags.
