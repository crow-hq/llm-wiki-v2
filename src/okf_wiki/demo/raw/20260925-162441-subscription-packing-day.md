Subscriptions go out once a week so the coffee is always fresh:

1. **Monday**: roast every coffee needed for the week's subscriptions.
2. **Tuesday morning**: pack and label; about 420 bags a week.
3. **Tuesday 15:00**: the carrier collects.

Packing takes two people for a full morning, which is why a part-time packer is being hired.
