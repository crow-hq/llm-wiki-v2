The reference profile for 12 kg batches of Aurora on the 15 kg roaster.

| Step | Time | Bean temperature |
|---|---|---|
| Charge | 0:00 | 205 °C (drum) |
| Turning point | 1:25 | 92 °C |
| Yellowing | 4:40 | 160 °C |
| First crack | 9:10 | 196 °C |
| Drop | 11:30 | 208 °C |

Development time is 1:45 (about 15% of the roast). Weight loss should be 14-15%.
If first crack comes before 8:45, lower the gas by one step at yellowing.
