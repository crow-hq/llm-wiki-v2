From the cancellation form, April to June 2026 (212 cancellations):

| Reason | Share |
|---|---|
| Too much coffee at home | 41% |
| Price | 23% |
| Moving or travelling | 14% |
| Taste | 9% |
| Other | 13% |

Most people leaving because of volume would have stayed with a pause option.
